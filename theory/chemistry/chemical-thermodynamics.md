# Chemical Thermodynamics — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/chemistry/chemical-thermodynamics.md](../../questions/chemistry/chemical-thermodynamics.md)

## Key ideas
- $\Delta U=q+w$; $w=-P_{ext}\Delta V$ (expansion negative). $\Delta H=\Delta U+\Delta n_gRT$. Free/adiabatic-free expansion of an ideal gas: $q=w=\Delta T=0$.
- Isothermal reversible $w=-nRT\ln\dfrac{V_2}{V_1}$; adiabatic $\Delta U=nC_v\Delta T$.
- Hess's law; $\Delta_rH^\circ=\sum\Delta_fH^\circ(\text{prod})-\sum\Delta_fH^\circ(\text{react})$; with bond energies $\Delta H=\sum\text{(broken)}-\sum\text{(formed)}$. Neutralisation of strong acid/base $\approx-57$ kJ per mole of water.
- $\Delta G=\Delta H-T\Delta S$; spontaneous if $\Delta G<0$; $\Delta G^\circ=-RT\ln K=-2.303RT\log K=-nFE^\circ$. Switching temperature $T=\dfrac{\Delta H}{\Delta S}$.
- Heat of hydration/solution cycles: $\Delta H_{hyd}=\Delta H_{sol}(\text{anhydrous})-\Delta H_{sol}(\text{hydrate})$.
## Common traps
- Units of $\Delta S$ (J vs kJ). $\Delta n_g$ counts only gases.
