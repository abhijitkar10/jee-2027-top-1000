# Solutions & Colligative Properties — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/chemistry/solutions.md](../../questions/chemistry/solutions.md)

## Key ideas
- Raoult: $P=x_AP_A^\circ$; relative lowering $\dfrac{\Delta P}{P^\circ}=x_{solute}$.
- $\Delta T_b=iK_bm$, $\Delta T_f=iK_fm$, $\pi=iCRT$ (van't Hoff $i$); $i=1+(n-1)\alpha$ for dissociation, $i=1-\alpha(1-\tfrac1n)$ for association.
- $x_{solute}$ from molality: $x=\dfrac{m}{m+1000/M_{solvent}}$. Mass percent $=\dfrac{w_{solute}}{w_{solution}}\times100$.
- Ideal solutions ($\Delta H_{mix}=0$, $\Delta V=0$); positive deviation $\to$ minimum-boiling azeotrope.
- Osmosis: solvent flows from lower to higher concentration; two solutions with an ionic outside: net $\pi=(C_{in}-iC_{out})RT$.
## Common traps
- More dilute electrolyte $\Rightarrow$ larger $i$. Compare freezing points via $i\times m$.
