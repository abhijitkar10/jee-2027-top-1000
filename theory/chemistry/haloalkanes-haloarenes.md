# Haloalkanes & Haloarenes — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/chemistry/haloalkanes-haloarenes.md](../../questions/chemistry/haloalkanes-haloarenes.md)

## Key ideas
- **S$_N$1** (tertiary/benzylic, planar carbocation, racemisation, polar protic solvent) vs **S$_N$2** (methyl/primary, inversion, backside attack, polar aprotic). Benzyl halide is faster in both.
- Elimination (E2) with alcoholic KOH follows Zaitsev; aqueous KOH favours substitution. KCN gives R–CN, AgCN gives R–NC.
- Aryl halides: resonance makes C–X strong; nucleophilic substitution needs o/p electron-withdrawing groups (addition–elimination); benzyne mechanism with NaNH$_2$.
- Finkelstein (NaI/acetone), Swarts (AgF), Sandmeyer (CuCl/CuBr), Wurtz–Fittig. Grignard: RMgX + D$_2$O $\to$ RD.
