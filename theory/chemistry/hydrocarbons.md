# Hydrocarbons — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/chemistry/hydrocarbons.md](../../questions/chemistry/hydrocarbons.md)

## Key ideas
- Alkanes: free-radical halogenation (Br is selective, Cl less so). Alkenes: electrophilic addition with Markovnikov (HBr) and peroxide effect (anti-Markovnikov, HBr only); hydroboration–oxidation gives anti-Markovnikov alcohol; ozonolysis; KMnO$_4$ (cold dil. $\to$ diol, hot $\to$ acids/ketones).
- Alkynes: Lindlar $\to$ cis-alkene; Na/liq. NH$_3$ $\to$ trans-alkene; terminal alkyne is acidic; hydration $\to$ ketone (Markovnikov). Wurtz reaction.
- Benzene: EAS mechanism ($\sigma$-complex); nitration uses $\text{NO}_2^+$; Friedel–Crafts fails with strongly deactivated rings and aniline.
- Stability: trans-2-butene $>$ cis; dipole moment cis $>$ trans (trans-2-butene $=0$).
