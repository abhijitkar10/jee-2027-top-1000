# Gaseous State — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/chemistry/gaseous-state.md](../../questions/chemistry/gaseous-state.md)

## Key ideas
- $PV=nRT$; Graham $\dfrac{r_1}{r_2}=\sqrt{\dfrac{M_2}{M_1}}$; Dalton $P_i=x_iP$; $v_{rms}=\sqrt{3RT/M}$.
- **van der Waals** $\left(P+\dfrac{an^2}{V^2}\right)(V-nb)=nRT$; as a cubic in $V_m$: $PV_m^3-(Pb+RT)V_m^2+aV_m-ab=0$. $Z=\dfrac{PV}{nRT}$; $Z<1$ attraction dominates, $Z>1$ repulsion. Critical constants $T_c=\dfrac{8a}{27Rb}$, $P_c=\dfrac{a}{27b^2}$, $V_c=3b$. Boyle temperature $\dfrac{a}{Rb}$.
## Common traps
- Units of $R$ ($0.082$ L atm) with $a$ in L$^2$ atm mol$^{-2}$.
