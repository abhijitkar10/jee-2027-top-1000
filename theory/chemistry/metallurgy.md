# General Principles of Metallurgy — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/chemistry/metallurgy.md](../../questions/chemistry/metallurgy.md)

## Key ideas
- Concentration (froth flotation for sulphides; leaching Al via Bayer, Ag/Au via cyanide), roasting/calcination, reduction. **Ellingham diagram:** lines with lower $\Delta G$ reduce oxides above them; C reduces above $\sim$1000$^\circ$C.
- Extraction: Fe (blast furnace: CO reduces Fe$_2$O$_3$; slag CaSiO$_3$), Cu (self-reduction in Bessemer converter), Al (Hall–Héroult, cryolite), Zn, Ag/Au (cyanide process). Refining: electrolytic, zone, van Arkel, Mond.
