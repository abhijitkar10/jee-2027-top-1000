# Practical Organic Chemistry — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/chemistry/practical-organic.md](../../questions/chemistry/practical-organic.md)

## Key ideas
- **Lassaigne's test:** sodium fusion converts N, S, X to NaCN, Na$_2$S, NaX (detects N, S, halogens; hydrazine (no C) fails). Kjeldahl (CuSO$_4$ catalyst) does not work for nitro, azo, ring-N (pyridine).
- Purification: crystallisation, distillation, vacuum distillation (glycerol), steam distillation (aniline is immiscible), chromatography ($R_f=\dfrac{\text{solute}}{\text{solvent front}}\le1$; polar compounds have lower $R_f$).
- Phosphorus test: (NH$_4$)$_3$PO$_4\cdot12$MoO$_3$ (yellow). Carius method for halogens/S.
