# Amines & Diazonium Salts — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/chemistry/amines.md](../../questions/chemistry/amines.md)

## Key ideas
- Basic strength (aqueous): $2^\circ>3^\circ>1^\circ$ for small alkyl, aliphatic $>$ NH$_3>$ aromatic; piperidine $>$ pyridine $>$ pyrrole.
- **Hinsberg test:** TsCl — primary gives soluble sulfonamide (in NaOH), secondary gives insoluble, tertiary no reaction (solution remains clear).
- Reactions: carbylamine (primary only), Hoffmann bromamide, Gabriel phthalimide (primary), diazotisation (NaNO$_2$/HCl, 273–278 K); aliphatic amine + HNO$_2$ $\to$ alcohol + N$_2$; coupling with phenol/aniline (azo dyes; aniline yellow = $p$-aminoazobenzene).
- Sandmeyer (CuCl/CuBr/CuCN) for Cl, Br, CN; iodo via KI; fluoro via Balz–Schiemann. Aniline is o/p-directing; forms 2,4,6-tribromoaniline with Br$_2$ water; Friedel–Crafts fails (NH$_2$–AlCl$_3$).
