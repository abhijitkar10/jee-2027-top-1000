# Chemical Bonding & Molecular Structure — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/chemistry/chemical-bonding.md](../../questions/chemistry/chemical-bonding.md)

## Key ideas
- **VSEPR / hybridisation:** count $\sigma$ pairs + lone pairs: 2 $sp$ (linear), 3 $sp^2$, 4 $sp^3$, 5 $sp^3d$ (TBP: PF$_5$; see-saw SF$_4$; T-shape ClF$_3$; linear XeF$_2$), 6 $sp^3d^2$ (octahedral SF$_6$; square pyramidal BrF$_5$; square planar XeF$_4$), 7 $sp^3d^3$ (IF$_7$ pentagonal bipyramid).
- **MOT:** order (O$_2$, F$_2$): $\sigma_{2s}<\sigma^*_{2s}<\sigma_{2p}<\pi_{2p}<\pi^*_{2p}<\sigma^*_{2p}$; for $\le$N$_2$ $\pi_{2p}$ is below $\sigma_{2p}$. Bond order $=\tfrac12(N_b-N_a)$; O$_2$, O$_2^-$, O$_2^{2-}$ have 2, 1.5, 1; O$_2^+$ has 2.5. $\psi_A-\psi_B$ gives $\sigma^*$. HOMO of F$_2$ is $\pi^*$.
- Dipole moments: NH$_3$ (1.47 D) > NF$_3$ (0.24 D); symmetric molecules (CO$_2$, BF$_3$, CH$_4$, XeF$_4$) are non-polar. Hydrogen bonding: intra (o-nitrophenol) vs inter (HF, alcohols).
- Octet exceptions: BF$_3$, BeF$_2$ (deficient); NO$_2$, ClO$_2$ (odd electron); PCl$_5$, SF$_6$, H$_2$SO$_4$ (expanded).
## Common traps
- Ionic character increases with electronegativity difference (Fajans: small cation, large anion $\Rightarrow$ covalent).
