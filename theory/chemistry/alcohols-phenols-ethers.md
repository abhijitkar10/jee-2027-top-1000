# Alcohols, Phenols & Ethers — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/chemistry/alcohols-phenols-ethers.md](../../questions/chemistry/alcohols-phenols-ethers.md)

## Key ideas
- Boiling point: alcohol $>$ aldehyde/ketone $>$ ether $>$ alkane (hydrogen bonding). Acidity: phenol ($pK_a\approx10$) reacts with NaOH; alcohols do not.
- Lucas test ($3^\circ$ fastest); oxidation ($1^\circ\to$ aldehyde with PCC, acid with KMnO$_4$; $2^\circ\to$ ketone). Dehydration with conc. H$_2$SO$_4$ (E1, rearrangements).
- Phenols: Reimer–Tiemann (CHCl$_3$/NaOH $\to$ salicylaldehyde), Kolbe (CO$_2$/NaOH $\to$ salicylic acid), Zn dust $\to$ benzene, Br$_2$/H$_2$O $\to$ 2,4,6-tribromophenol; picric acid via disulphonic acid + conc. HNO$_3$; in low-polarity solvents mono-bromination without Lewis acid.
- Ethers: Williamson synthesis, cleavage with HI. Ceric ammonium nitrate test for alcohols; FeCl$_3$ test for phenols.
