# Carboxylic Acids & Derivatives — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/chemistry/carboxylic-acids.md](../../questions/chemistry/carboxylic-acids.md)

## Key ideas
- Acidity order HCOOH $>$ CH$_3$COOH $>$ higher acids; $-I$ groups (Cl, NO$_2$) increase acidity.
- Preparation/reactions: HVZ (Br$_2$/red P $\to\alpha$-bromo acid), decarboxylation with soda lime (RCOONa $\to$ RH), Kolbe electrolysis, esterification, LiAlH$_4$ reduction, acid chloride (SOCl$_2$), amide $\to$ nitrile (P$_4$O$_{10}$).
- Oxidation of alkylbenzene by KMnO$_4$/KOH gives benzoic acid; nitration of benzoic acid gives the *meta* product.
