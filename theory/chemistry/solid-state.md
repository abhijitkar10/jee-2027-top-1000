# Solid State — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/chemistry/solid-state.md](../../questions/chemistry/solid-state.md)

## Key ideas
- Unit cells: SC 1 atom/cell (packing 52%), BCC 2 (68%), FCC 4 (74%); coordination numbers 6, 8, 12. Edge relations: SC $a=2r$, BCC $\sqrt3a=4r$, FCC $\sqrt2a=4r$.
- Density $\rho=\dfrac{ZM}{N_Aa^3}$. Voids: octahedral $=N$, tetrahedral $=2N$ per $N$ atoms.
- Defects: Schottky (density falls; NaCl), Frenkel (AgBr), metal excess (F-centres, colour).
- Conductors, semiconductors (n/p), insulators; magnetic properties.
## Common traps
- Convert pm to cm ($10^{-10}$ cm per Å) before using density.
