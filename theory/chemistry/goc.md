# General Organic Chemistry — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/chemistry/goc.md](../../questions/chemistry/goc.md)

## Key ideas
- Electronic effects: $-I$ (NO$_2$, CN, halogens), $+I$ (alkyl); resonance $+R$ (NH$_2$, OH, OR) and $-R$ (NO$_2$, CHO, COOH); hyperconjugation (number of $\alpha$-H); electromeric effect is temporary and needs a reagent.
- Stability: carbocation $3^\circ>2^\circ>1^\circ$, benzyl/allyl stabilised; carbanion opposite ($sp$ carbanion most stable, $-I$ groups stabilise); free radical $3^\circ>2^\circ>1^\circ$.
- Acidity of carboxylic acids: HCOOH $>$ CH$_3$COOH; $-I$ substituents increase; ortho effect. Basicity of amines (aliphatic $>$ NH$_3>$ aromatic); pyrrole is not basic.
- Aromaticity: cyclic, planar, conjugated, $(4n+2)\pi$ electrons.
- Directing groups: activating o/p (OH, NH$_2$, OR, alkyl), deactivating m (NO$_2$, CN, CHO, COR, COOH, SO$_3$H), halogens deactivating but o/p. Carbocation shape trigonal planar.
- Methods of purification: crystallisation, distillation, steam distillation (immiscible), chromatography (polar compounds have lower $R_f$ on silica).
## Common traps
- Compare *conjugate base* stability for acidity questions.
