# Polymers & Chemistry in Everyday Life — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/chemistry/polymers-everyday.md](../../questions/chemistry/polymers-everyday.md)

## Key ideas
- Addition polymers (PE, PVC, teflon, PAN), condensation (nylon-6,6 from hexamethylenediamine + adipic acid, terylene, bakelite), natural rubber (cis-polyisoprene; vulcanisation), biodegradable PHBV, nylon-2-nylon-6.
- Everyday: analgesics, antipyretics, antibiotics, antiseptics (iodoform), disinfectants, artificial sweeteners; CCl$_4$ (fire extinguisher), CH$_2$Cl$_2$ (paint remover), Freons (refrigerants), DDT (non-biodegradable insecticide). Fluoroapatite $3\text{Ca}_3(\text{PO}_4)_2\cdot\text{CaF}_2$ in enamel.
