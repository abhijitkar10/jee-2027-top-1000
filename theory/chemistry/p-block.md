# p-Block Elements — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/chemistry/p-block.md](../../questions/chemistry/p-block.md)

## Key ideas
- **Group 13:** inert pair effect ($+1$ stable for Tl); Al passivated by conc. HNO$_3$; anomalous IE order $\text{B}>\text{Tl}>\text{Ga}>\text{Al}$; boron burns to B$_2$O$_3$ (+BN). **Group 14:** catenation; $\text{SiO}_2,\text{GeO}_2$ acidic, SnO/PbO amphoteric; PbO$_2$, Pb$^{4+}$ oxidising.
- **Group 15:** N$_2$ inert, max covalency 4; $+5$ becomes *less* stable down the group; BiH$_3$ strongest reducing hydride; NH$_4$NO$_2$ $\to$ N$_2$, NH$_4$NO$_3$ (200–250$^\circ$C) $\to$ N$_2$O.
- **Group 16/17:** oxygen O$_2$ vs S$_8$; PbS + dil. HNO$_3$ $\to$ NO, S, Pb(NO$_3$)$_2$; MnO$_4^-$ + I$^-$ (neutral) $\to$ IO$_3^-$; halogens (except F$_2$) disproportionate in alkali; interhalogens ICl (linear), ICl$_3$ (T-shaped), BrF$_5$ (sq. pyramid), IF$_7$.
- H-bonding explains boiling points (NH$_3>$PH$_3$). Oxoacid strengths, thiosulphate with I$_2$ ($\text{S}_4\text{O}_6^{2-}$) vs Br$_2$ ($\text{SO}_4^{2-}$).
## Common traps
- Shapes via VSEPR (SF$_4$ see-saw, XeF$_4$ square planar). Ozone, SO$_2$, NO$_2$ resonance structures.
