# Coordination Compounds — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/chemistry/coordination-compounds.md](../../questions/chemistry/coordination-compounds.md)

## Key ideas
- **Naming:** ligands alphabetically; $\text{di}/\text{tri}$ for simple, $\text{bis}/\text{tris}$ for complex ligands; oxidation state in Roman numerals; anionic complex ends in *-ate*.
- **Werner:** ionisable (outer sphere) ions precipitate with AgNO$_3$; e.g. CoCl$_3\cdot5$NH$_3\to$ 2 AgCl. Ambidentate: NO$_2^-$, SCN$^-$, CN$^-$; bidentate: en, ox.
- **CFT:** octahedral $t_{2g}$/$e_g$ split $\Delta_o$; strong-field ligands (CN$^-$, CO $>$ en $>$ NH$_3$ $>$ H$_2$O $>$ F$^-$ $>$ Cl$^-$) give low spin (pairing) for $d^4$–$d^7$. Tetrahedral: $e$ below $t_2$, $\Delta_t=\tfrac49\Delta_o$ (always high spin). $\lambda_{max}\propto1/\Delta$.
- Magnetic moment $\sqrt{n(n+2)}$. Colours from $d$–$d$ transitions ($d^0$ and $d^{10}$ colourless; charge transfer for MnO$_4^-$).
- Isomerism: geometrical ($[\text{M(AA)}_2\text{X}_2]$: cis/trans, cis is optically active; $[\text{MA}_3\text{B}_3]$ fac/mer), ionisation, linkage, coordination. Tetrahedral MABXL has no geometric isomers.
## Common traps
- Hybridisation: $[\text{Ni(CN)}_4]^{2-}$ square planar $dsp^2$, $[\text{NiCl}_4]^{2-}$ tetrahedral $sp^3$; $\text{Co}^{3+}$ aqua and ammine are low spin.
