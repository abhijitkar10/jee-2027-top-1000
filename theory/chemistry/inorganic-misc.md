# Hydrogen, Environmental Chemistry & Salt Analysis — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/chemistry/inorganic-misc.md](../../questions/chemistry/inorganic-misc.md)

## Key ideas
- **Salt analysis:** group reagents — I: dil. HCl (Pb$^{2+}$); II: H$_2$S in dil. HCl (Cu$^{2+}$, Cd$^{2+}$); III: NH$_4$OH + NH$_4$Cl (Fe$^{3+}$, Al$^{3+}$); IV: H$_2$S + NH$_4$OH (Mn$^{2+}$, Zn$^{2+}$, Ni$^{2+}$, Co$^{2+}$); V: (NH$_4$)$_2$CO$_3$ (Ba$^{2+}$, Sr$^{2+}$, Ca$^{2+}$); VI: Mg$^{2+}$. NH$_4$Cl suppresses $[\text{OH}^-]$.
- Anions: pale-yellow AgBr, sparingly soluble in NH$_4$OH; AgCl white soluble; AgI yellow insoluble. Brown-ring test (NO$_3^-$), Prussian blue (Fe$^{3+}$ + ferrocyanide), blood-red [Fe(SCN)]$^{2+}$, violet [Fe(CN)$_5$NOS]$^{4-}$, (NH$_4$)$_3$PO$_4\cdot12$MoO$_3$ yellow.
- Bead/cobalt-nitrate/flame tests. Hydrogen peroxide, hardness of water, ozone depletion (CFCs), greenhouse gases.
