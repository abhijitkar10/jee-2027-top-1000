# d- and f-Block Elements — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/chemistry/d-f-block.md](../../questions/chemistry/d-f-block.md)

## Key ideas
- 3d configurations (Cr, Cu exceptions). Maximum oxidation state at Mn ($+7$); highest enthalpy of atomisation V (3d). Only $+3$ for Sc. Smallest metallic radius among 3d ends near Cr/Ni–Cu region (Cr$<$Mn).
- Half-filled/filled stability explains $E^\circ$ trends: $\text{Mn}^{3+}/\text{Mn}^{2+}=+1.57$ V, $\text{Cr}^{3+}/\text{Cr}^{2+}=-0.41$ V, $\text{Co}^{3+}/\text{Co}^{2+}=+1.97$ V. $\text{Cu}^+$ disproportionates (hydration energy of Cu$^{2+}$).
- Compounds: $2\text{CrO}_4^{2-}+2\text{H}^+\rightleftharpoons\text{Cr}_2\text{O}_7^{2-}+\text{H}_2\text{O}$; KMnO$_4$ reductions (acid $\to$ Mn$^{2+}$, neutral $\to$ MnO$_2$, alkaline $\to$ MnO$_4^{2-}$); $\text{MnO}_2+\text{KOH}+\text{O}_2\to\text{K}_2\text{MnO}_4$; chromite fusion gives Na$_2$CrO$_4$ + Fe$_2$O$_3$.
- Oxide character: MO (basic) $\to$ M$_2$O$_3$ (amphoteric) $\to$ MO$_3$ (acidic) with rising oxidation state.
- Lanthanoids: $Z=58$–$71$, lanthanoid contraction; colourless $4f^0$ (La$^{3+}$) and $4f^{14}$ (Lu$^{3+}$). Actinoids: Cm is one.
## Common traps
- Spin-only moments: $d^5$ 5.92, $d^4$ 4.90, $d^3$ 3.87, $d^2$ 2.83, $d^1$ 1.73 BM.
