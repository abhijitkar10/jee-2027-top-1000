# Biomolecules — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/chemistry/biomolecules.md](../../questions/chemistry/biomolecules.md)

## Key ideas
- Carbohydrates: glucose (aldohexose), fructose (ketohexose), $\alpha/\beta$ anomers (C-1 differ), epimers (one carbon), sucrose non-reducing; oxidation with Br$_2$ water $\to$ gluconic acid, HNO$_3$ $\to$ saccharic acid.
- Amino acids: zwitterion, isoelectric point; essential: Arg, His, Ile, Leu, Lys, Met, Phe, Thr, Trp, Val. Ninhydrin test (purple) for $\alpha$-amino acids/proteins. Peptides: $n$ different amino acids give $n!$ tripeptides for $n=3$.
- Proteins: primary–quaternary structure; denaturation (heat) destroys secondary/tertiary structure, not peptide bonds; enzymes are specific globular proteins (maltase hydrolyses maltose).
- DNA: A–T, G–C; RNA has uracil; tyrosine has 9 carbons.
