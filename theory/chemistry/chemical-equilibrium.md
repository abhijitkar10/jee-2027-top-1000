# Chemical Equilibrium — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/chemistry/chemical-equilibrium.md](../../questions/chemistry/chemical-equilibrium.md)

## Key ideas
- $K_c=\dfrac{[\text{prod}]}{[\text{react}]}$ (pure solids/liquids omitted); $K_p=K_c(RT)^{\Delta n_g}$. Reverse: $1/K$; multiply equation by $n$: $K^n$; add reactions: multiply $K$.
- Reaction quotient $Q$ vs $K$ decides direction. Le Chatelier: concentration, pressure (fewer moles), temperature (endothermic favoured by heating); catalyst changes rate not position. Adding a pure solid does not shift equilibrium.
- $\Delta G^\circ=-RT\ln K$; van't Hoff $\ln\dfrac{K_2}{K_1}=-\dfrac{\Delta H}{R}\left(\dfrac1{T_2}-\dfrac1{T_1}\right)$.
- Degree of dissociation for $A\rightleftharpoons B+C$: $K_c=\dfrac{\alpha^2C}{1-\alpha}$.
## Common traps
- Equal *numbers of molecules* means equal partial pressures. $K_p$ needs partial pressures in atm/bar consistently.
