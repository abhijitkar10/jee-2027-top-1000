# s-Block Elements — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/chemistry/s-block.md](../../questions/chemistry/s-block.md)

## Key ideas
- Alkali metals: reactivity, hydration energy ($\text{Li}^+$ largest), flame colours (Li crimson, Na golden, K violet); anomalous Li resembles Mg. Na$_2$CO$_3$ (Solvay), NaOH (Castner–Kellner), NaHCO$_3$.
- Alkaline earths: solubility of sulphates decreases down the group, hydroxides increase; Ca(OH)$_2$ (lime water), CaO, gypsum, plaster of Paris $\text{CaSO}_4\cdot\tfrac12\text{H}_2\text{O}$; BeO amphoteric; Be–Al diagonal relationship.
