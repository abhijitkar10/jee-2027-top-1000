# Redox & Electrochemistry — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/chemistry/electrochemistry.md](../../questions/chemistry/electrochemistry.md)

## Key ideas
- Oxidation numbers; balance by ion-electron method. Disproportionation: same element up and down (Cu$^+$, MnO$_4^{2-}$ in acid, Cl$_2$ in alkali); comproportionation is the reverse.
- $E^\circ_{cell}=E^\circ_{cathode}-E^\circ_{anode}$; Nernst $E=E^\circ-\dfrac{0.0591}{n}\log Q$; $\log K=\dfrac{nE^\circ}{0.0591}$; $\Delta G^\circ=-nFE^\circ$.
- Conductance: $\kappa=\dfrac{1}{R}\dfrac lA$; $\Lambda_m=\dfrac{\kappa\times1000}{M}$. Kohlrausch $\Lambda_m^\circ=\nu_+\lambda_++\nu_-\lambda_-$; strong electrolyte $\Lambda_m=\Lambda_m^\circ-A\sqrt c$ (steep fall $\Rightarrow$ weak electrolyte).
- Faraday: $m=\dfrac{ItM}{nF}$; 1 F $=96500$ C. Electrolysis products: check water vs ion discharge.
- Cells: dry cell (MnO$_2$ reduced, $+4\to+3$), lead storage, fuel cell (H$_2$–O$_2$, $\sim70\%$ efficient), corrosion.
## Common traps
- Use $n$ from the *balanced* equation. Half-cell potentials are not multiplied by coefficients (but $\Delta G$ is).
