# Atomic Structure — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/chemistry/atomic-structure.md](../../questions/chemistry/atomic-structure.md)

## Key ideas
- Bohr: $r_n=0.529\dfrac{n^2}{Z}$ Å, $E_n=-13.6\dfrac{Z^2}{n^2}$ eV, $v_n=2.18\times10^6\dfrac Zn$ m/s; $mvr=\dfrac{nh}{2\pi}$; de Broglie $2\pi r=n\lambda$. Wavenumber $\bar\nu=\dfrac1\lambda=R_HZ^2\left(\dfrac1{n_1^2}-\dfrac1{n_2^2}\right)$.
- Quantum numbers: $n$, $l=0..n-1$, $m_l=-l..+l$, $m_s=\pm\tfrac12$. Orbitals per shell $n^2$; electrons $2n^2$. Radial nodes $=n-l-1$, angular nodes $=l$.
- Filling: Aufbau ($n+l$ rule, lower $n$ first on ties), Pauli, Hund. Exceptions: Cr $3d^54s^1$, Cu $3d^{10}4s^1$.
- Spin-only moment $\mu=\sqrt{n(n+2)}$ BM. Heisenberg $\Delta x\Delta p\ge\dfrac{h}{4\pi}$.
## Common traps
- For $|m_l|=1$ or other counting questions, list allowed $l$ first. Frequency of the de Broglie wave $=\dfrac{mv^2}{h}=\dfrac{2KE}{h}$.
