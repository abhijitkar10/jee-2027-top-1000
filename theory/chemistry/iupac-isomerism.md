# IUPAC Nomenclature & Isomerism — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/chemistry/iupac-isomerism.md](../../questions/chemistry/iupac-isomerism.md)

## Key ideas
- Longest chain + lowest locants; priority: acid $>$ ester $>$ amide $>$ nitrile $>$ aldehyde $>$ ketone $>$ alcohol $>$ amine. Aniline $=$ aminobenzene; catechol $=$ benzene-1,2-diol.
- **Structural isomerism:** chain, position, functional, metamerism, tautomerism. Isomers of C$_7$H$_{16}$: 9.
- **Stereoisomerism:** $n$ stereocentres give up to $2^n$ (fewer if meso/symmetric); geometrical isomers (cis/trans, E/Z). Count monochlorination products by distinct H types, plus enantiomers (2-methylbutane: 6).
- Sigma/pi counting: single bond $1\sigma$; double $1\sigma+1\pi$; triple $1\sigma+2\pi$.
