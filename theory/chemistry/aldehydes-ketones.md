# Aldehydes & Ketones — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/chemistry/aldehydes-ketones.md](../../questions/chemistry/aldehydes-ketones.md)

## Key ideas
- Nucleophilic addition: reactivity aldehydes $>$ ketones; aromatic aldehydes less reactive than aliphatic; $-I$ groups increase reactivity.
- Named reactions: aldol (needs $\alpha$-H), cross-aldol/Claisen–Schmidt (benzaldehyde + acetone $\to$ dibenzalacetone, 2:1), Cannizzaro (no $\alpha$-H), Clemmensen (Zn–Hg/HCl) and Wolff–Kishner (N$_2$H$_4$/KOH) reductions, Rosenmund, Stephen, Etard, Gattermann–Koch (CO/HCl/AlCl$_3$).
- Reagents: NaBH$_4$ (C=O, not esters), LiAlH$_4$, DIBAL-H (ester $\to$ aldehyde), Grignard (aldehyde $\to$ $2^\circ$ alcohol).
- Tests: Tollens, Fehling, Schiff, iodoform (CH$_3$CO–), 2,4-DNP.
