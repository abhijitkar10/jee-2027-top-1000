# Periodic Table & Periodicity — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/chemistry/periodic-table.md](../../questions/chemistry/periodic-table.md)

## Key ideas
- Radius: decreases across a period, increases down a group; isoelectronic species: higher $Z$ $\Rightarrow$ smaller (O$^{2-}>$F$^->$Na$^+>$Mg$^{2+}$).
- Ionisation enthalpy: increases across (exceptions Be>B, N>O, Mg>Al, P>S) and decreases down; $\text{Tl}>\text{Ga}\approx\text{Al}$ anomaly (poor $d/f$ shielding). Electron gain enthalpy: Cl most negative, F less than Cl; noble gases and N are positive/less negative.
- Electronegativity (Pauling) rises across; oxidation state is defined via electronegativity. Diagonal relationships (Li–Mg, Be–Al, B–Si).
- Oxide nature: acidic (non-metal), basic (metal), amphoteric (Al$_2$O$_3$, ZnO, SnO, PbO, BeO).
## Common traps
- Second-period anomalies (F, O have smaller EA than Cl, S).
