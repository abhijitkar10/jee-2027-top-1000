# Magnetic Effects of Current & Magnetism — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/physics/magnetism.md](../../questions/physics/magnetism.md)

## Key ideas
- Lorentz force $\vec F=q(\vec E+\vec v\times\vec B)$; radius $r=\dfrac{mv}{qB}$, period $T=\dfrac{2\pi m}{qB}$; velocity selector $E=vB$.
- **Biot–Savart:** $dB=\dfrac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\dfrac{I\,dl\sin\theta}{r^2}$. Straight wire $\dfrac{\mu_0I}{2\pi r}$; circular loop centre $\dfrac{\mu_0I}{2R}$; solenoid $\mu_0nI$. Regular $n$-gon at centre: $B=\dfrac{n\mu_0I}{2\pi R}\tan\dfrac\pi n$.
- **Ampère:** $\oint\vec B\cdot d\vec l=\mu_0I_{enc}$. Wire of radius $a$: inside $\dfrac{\mu_0Ir}{2\pi a^2}$, outside $\dfrac{\mu_0I}{2\pi r}$; coaxial cable $B=0$ outside.
- Force on wire $\vec F=I\vec l\times\vec B$; two parallel wires $\dfrac{\mu_0I_1I_2}{2\pi d}$ per length. Loop torque $\tau=NIAB\sin\theta$; work to rotate $=MB(\cos\theta_1-\cos\theta_2)$.
- Moving-coil galvanometer $NIAB=k\phi$, sensitivity $\dfrac{\phi}{I}=\dfrac{NAB}{k}$.
- Magnetic materials: dia ($\chi<0$), para (small $\chi>0$, $\propto1/T$), ferro (large; hysteresis, coercivity $H=nI$ to demagnetise).
## Common traps
- In non-uniform $B$ the net force on a loop $=I\,L\,\Delta B$ on opposite sides. $\vec F\perp\vec v$, so magnetic force does no work.
