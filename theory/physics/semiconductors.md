# Semiconductors & Electronic Devices — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/physics/semiconductors.md](../../questions/physics/semiconductors.md)

## Key ideas
- Intrinsic Si/Ge: $n_e=n_h$. n-type (donors, e.g. P): majority electrons; p-type (acceptors, e.g. B): majority holes; $n_en_h=n_i^2$. Conductivity of semiconductors *increases* with temperature.
- Band gap and light: $\lambda_{max}=\dfrac{1240}{E_g\,(\text{eV})}$ nm (LED/photodiode/threshold for photoconduction).
- **p–n junction:** forward bias lowers the barrier (conducts); reverse bias gives a tiny leakage current. Dynamic resistance $r_d=\dfrac{\Delta V}{\Delta I}$ is measured in forward bias. Zener regulates in reverse breakdown: $R_s=\dfrac{V_{in}-V_z}{I_z+I_L}$.
- Rectifier: half-wave output frequency $=f$, full-wave $=2f$. LED emits on forward bias; photodiode is used in reverse bias.
- **Logic gates:** AND, OR, NOT, NAND, NOR, XOR; NAND and NOR are universal. De Morgan: $\overline{A+B}=\bar A\bar B$, $\overline{AB}=\bar A+\bar B$.
## Common traps
- Read the circuit stage by stage for truth tables. Check the diode direction before assuming current flows.
