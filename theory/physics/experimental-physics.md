# Experimental Skills — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/physics/experimental-physics.md](../../questions/physics/experimental-physics.md)

## Key ideas
- **Vernier caliper:** LC $=\dfrac{\text{1 MSD}}{N}$ (or $1-\dfrac{N-1}{N}$ MSD); reading $=\text{MSR}+\text{VC}\times\text{LC}-\text{zero error}$. **Screw gauge:** LC $=\dfrac{\text{pitch}}{\text{circular divisions}}$; a zero error above/below the reference line is subtracted/added with sign.
- Errors: fractional errors add for products/quotients (e.g. $Y=49000\dfrac{m}{l}$ dyn/cm$^2$: $\dfrac{\Delta Y}{Y}=\dfrac{\Delta m}{m}+\dfrac{\Delta l}{l}$).
- Sonometer, metre bridge, potentiometer, half-deflection method (figure of merit $k=\dfrac{E}{R\theta}$), refractive index by travelling microscope ($\mu=\dfrac{\text{real}}{\text{apparent}}$).
- Significant figures of a computed result follow the least precise input.
## Common traps
- The least count of the *instrument* sets the absolute error; do not add the LC of two readings unless both are subtracted (take $2\times$LC).
