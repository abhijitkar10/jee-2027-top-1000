# Atoms & Nuclei — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/physics/atoms-nuclei.md](../../questions/physics/atoms-nuclei.md)

## Key ideas
- **Bohr (H-like):** $r_n=0.529\dfrac{n^2}{Z}\ \text{\AA}$, $v_n=2.19\times10^6\dfrac Zn$ m/s, $E_n=-13.6\dfrac{Z^2}{n^2}$ eV, $L=\dfrac{nh}{2\pi}$; $KE=-E$, $U=2E$.
- Spectral lines $\dfrac1\lambda=RZ^2\left(\dfrac1{n_1^2}-\dfrac1{n_2^2}\right)$; Lyman ($n_1=1$, UV), Balmer ($2$, visible), Paschen ($3$, IR). Series limit gives the shortest wavelength; Balmer limit : Lyman limit $=4:1$.
- Rutherford closest approach $r_0=\dfrac{kZe\cdot2e}{K}$. Nuclear radius $R=R_0A^{1/3}$.
- **Binding energy** $=\Delta m\,c^2$, $1\,\text{u}=931.5$ MeV. Fission of $^{235}$U releases $\approx200$ MeV; fusion of light nuclei releases energy up to iron peak.
- **Radioactivity:** $N=N_0e^{-\lambda t}$, $t_{1/2}=\dfrac{0.693}{\lambda}$, $A=\lambda N$. $\alpha$: $A-4,Z-2$; $\beta^-$: $Z+1$.
## Common traps
- Use atomic masses consistently (electron masses cancel in $\beta^-$ but not in $\beta^+$). Mass balance: neutron has $A=1,Z=0$.
