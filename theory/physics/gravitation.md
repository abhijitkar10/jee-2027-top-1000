# Gravitation — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/physics/gravitation.md](../../questions/physics/gravitation.md)

## Key ideas
- $F=\dfrac{GMm}{r^2}$; $g=\dfrac{GM}{R^2}$. Variation: height $g'=g\left(\dfrac{R}{R+h}\right)^2$; depth $g'=g\left(1-\dfrac dR\right)$; latitude $g'=g-\omega^2R\cos^2\lambda$.
- Potential energy $U=-\dfrac{GMm}{r}$; escape speed $v_e=\sqrt{2gR}=\sqrt{\dfrac{2GM}{R}}$; orbital speed $v_o=\sqrt{\dfrac{GM}{r}}$ ($v_e=\sqrt2v_o$).
- Satellite: $E=-\dfrac{GMm}{2r}=-KE=\dfrac U2$. Kepler III: $T^2\propto r^3$. Geostationary: $T=24$ h, $r\approx4.2\times10^4$ km.
- Angular momentum of a circular orbit $L=m\sqrt{GMr}$; Kepler II $\Rightarrow$ areal velocity $\dfrac{L}{2m}$ constant.
- Force law $F\propto r^{-n}$ gives $T^2\propto r^{(n+1)}$ (e.g. $F\propto r^{-3/2}\Rightarrow T^2\propto r^{5/2}$).
## Common traps
- Energy to lift to orbit $=E_{orbit}-E_{surface}=\dfrac{GMm}{R}\left(1-\dfrac{R}{2r}\right)$ (surface KE is zero in the non-rotating frame).
