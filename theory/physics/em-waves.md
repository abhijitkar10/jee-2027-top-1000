# Electromagnetic Waves — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/physics/em-waves.md](../../questions/physics/em-waves.md)

## Key ideas
- $c=\dfrac1{\sqrt{\mu_0\varepsilon_0}}$; in a medium $v=\dfrac{c}{\sqrt{\mu_r\varepsilon_r}}$; $\dfrac EB=c$ (magnitudes), $\vec E\times\vec B$ along propagation.
- Intensity $I=\tfrac12\varepsilon_0cE_0^2=\dfrac{cB_0^2}{2\mu_0}$; average energy density $u=\tfrac12\varepsilon_0E_0^2$ with $u_E=u_B$.
- Radiation pressure: absorber $\dfrac Ic$, perfect reflector $\dfrac{2I}c$; force $F=\dfrac{IA}{c}$.
- Spectrum (long to short $\lambda$): radio, microwave, infrared, visible, UV, X-ray, $\gamma$. Ozone absorbs UV; greenhouse effect traps IR.
- Displacement current $I_d=\varepsilon_0\dfrac{d\Phi_E}{dt}$ completes Ampère's law.
## Common traps
- Identify propagation direction from $\hat E\times\hat B$; the same frequency but different wavelength when the medium changes.
