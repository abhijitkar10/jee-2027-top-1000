# Dual Nature of Matter & Radiation — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/physics/dual-nature.md](../../questions/physics/dual-nature.md)

## Key ideas
- Photon energy $E=h\nu=\dfrac{hc}{\lambda}=\dfrac{1240}{\lambda\,(\text{nm})}$ eV. Photon number rate $=\dfrac{P}{h\nu}$; momentum $\dfrac h\lambda$.
- **Photoelectric effect:** $K_{max}=h\nu-\phi=eV_0$; threshold $\nu_0=\phi/h$. $V_0$–$\nu$ graph has slope $\dfrac he$ (same for all metals); intensity changes saturation current only, not $V_0$.
- **de Broglie:** $\lambda=\dfrac hp=\dfrac{h}{\sqrt{2mK}}$; for an electron accelerated through $V$: $\lambda=\dfrac{12.27}{\sqrt V}\ \text{\AA}$. Equal $K$: $\lambda\propto\dfrac1{\sqrt m}$; equal $\lambda$: $K\propto\dfrac1m$.
- Davisson–Germer confirms electron diffraction; thermal neutron $\lambda=\dfrac{h}{\sqrt{2m_Nk_BT}}$.
## Common traps
- Higher frequency at constant power means *fewer* photons per second. Stopping potential is in volts, KE in eV.
