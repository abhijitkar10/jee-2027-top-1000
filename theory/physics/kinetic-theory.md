# Kinetic Theory of Gases — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/physics/kinetic-theory.md](../../questions/physics/kinetic-theory.md)

## Key ideas
- $P=\tfrac13\rho\overline{v^2}$; $\tfrac12m\overline{v^2}=\tfrac32k_BT$. $v_{rms}=\sqrt{\dfrac{3RT}{M}}$, $v_{avg}=\sqrt{\dfrac{8RT}{\pi M}}$, $v_{mp}=\sqrt{\dfrac{2RT}{M}}$ ($v_{mp}:v_{avg}:v_{rms}=1:1.13:1.22$).
- **Equipartition:** $\tfrac12k_BT$ per quadratic degree of freedom. $U=\tfrac f2nRT$. Mono $f=3$; rigid diatomic $5$; non-rigid diatomic $7$; non-linear polyatomic $6$ (rigid).
- Mixture: $C_v=\dfrac{n_1C_{v1}+n_2C_{v2}}{n_1+n_2}$, $\gamma_{mix}=1+\dfrac{n_1+n_2}{n_1f_1/2+n_2f_2/2}$.
- Mean free path $\lambda=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2n\pi d^2}=\dfrac{k_BT}{\sqrt2\pi d^2P}$; collision frequency $\propto\sqrt T$ at constant volume.
## Common traps
- $v_{rms}$ depends only on $T$ and $M$ (not pressure). Real-gas (van der Waals) corrections are outside the ideal treatment.
