# Electromagnetic Induction — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/physics/emi.md](../../questions/physics/emi.md)

## Key ideas
- Faraday–Lenz: $\varepsilon=-N\dfrac{d\Phi}{dt}$; charge through the coil $q=\dfrac{N\Delta\Phi}{R}$.
- Motional emf $\varepsilon=Blv$; rod rotating about one end $\varepsilon=\tfrac12B\omega l^2$; about its *midpoint* the potential difference between the ends is $0$.
- Coil rotating in a field: $\varepsilon_{max}=NAB\omega$.
- Self-inductance $L$: $\varepsilon=-L\dfrac{dI}{dt}$, $U=\tfrac12LI^2$; solenoid $L=\mu_0n^2Al$. Mutual inductance $M=\dfrac{\Phi_2}{I_1}$ (small coaxial loop in a large one: $M=\dfrac{\mu_0\pi b^2}{2a}$).
- **LR circuit:** $I=I_0(1-e^{-t/\tau})$, $\tau=\dfrac LR$.
- Loop pulled out of a field slowly: force $\dfrac{B^2l^2v}{R}$, power $\dfrac{B^2l^2v^2}{R}$; a loop entering a field with initial velocity slows as $v=v_0e^{-Kt}$, $K=\dfrac{B^2l^2}{MR}$.
## Common traps
- Use Lenz's law only for direction. Uniform field over a whole loop moving translationally gives no emf.
