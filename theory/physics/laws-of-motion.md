# Laws of Motion & Friction — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/physics/laws-of-motion.md](../../questions/physics/laws-of-motion.md)

## Key ideas
- $\vec F=\dfrac{d\vec p}{dt}$; impulse $=\Delta p=\int F\,dt$. Force from a stopping catch: $F=\dfrac{m\Delta v}{\Delta t}$.
- **Atwood machine:** $a=\dfrac{(m_2-m_1)g}{m_1+m_2}$, $T=\dfrac{2m_1m_2g}{m_1+m_2}$. Apparent weight in a lift: $m(g\pm a)$.
- **Friction:** static $f\le\mu_sN$, kinetic $f=\mu_kN$. Block on incline slides when $\tan\theta>\mu_s$. Sliding time ratio (rough/smooth) $n$ at $45^\circ$: $\mu=1-\dfrac1{n^2}$.
- **Circular motion:** $a_c=\dfrac{v^2}{r}=\omega^2r$. Banked road: $v^2=rg\dfrac{\tan\theta+\mu}{1-\mu\tan\theta}$; no-friction speed $v^2=rg\tan\theta$. Vertical circle: $v_{bottom}\ge\sqrt{5gR}$ for a string; $T_{bottom}-T_{top}=6mg$.
- Conical pendulum: $\omega^2=\dfrac{g}{l\cos\theta}$.
## Common traps
- Draw the free-body diagram *before* choosing axes. Tension in a massless string is the same everywhere; a *chain's* weight adds to the support force. Pseudo-force in accelerating frames acts opposite to $a$.
