# Kinematics — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/physics/kinematics.md](../../questions/physics/kinematics.md)

## Key ideas
- Uniform acceleration: $v=u+at,\ s=ut+\tfrac12at^2,\ v^2=u^2+2as$. Distance in the $n$th second: $s_n=u+a(n-\tfrac12)$; from rest $S_n\propto(2n-1)$.
- Variable motion: $a=\dfrac{dv}{dt}=v\dfrac{dv}{dx}$. Use calculus when $v(x)$ or $x(t)$ is given; area under $v$–$t$ = displacement, under $a$–$t$ = change in velocity.
- **Projectile:** $R=\dfrac{u^2\sin2\theta}{g}$, $H=\dfrac{u^2\sin^2\theta}{2g}$, $T=\dfrac{2u\sin\theta}{g}$; $R=H$ at $\tan\theta=4$; range equal for $\theta$ and $90^\circ-\theta$.
- **Relative motion:** $\vec v_{AB}=\vec v_A-\vec v_B$. River-boat: shortest time when heading perpendicular; shortest path when $v_b\sin\theta=v_r$.
- **Free fall from a tower with up/down throws:** $t_{drop}=\sqrt{t_1t_2}$.
- Average speed for equal-time halves is the arithmetic mean; for equal-distance halves it is the harmonic mean.
## Common traps
- Average speed $\ne$ magnitude of average velocity when direction reverses. Use signs consistently (choose $\uparrow$ or $\downarrow$ once).
