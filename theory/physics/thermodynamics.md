# Thermodynamics — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/physics/thermodynamics.md](../../questions/physics/thermodynamics.md)

## Key ideas
- First law $\Delta U=Q+W_{on}$ (or $Q=\Delta U+W_{by}$); $\Delta U=nC_v\Delta T$ for any ideal-gas process.
- $C_p-C_v=R$, $\gamma=\dfrac{C_p}{C_v}=1+\dfrac2f$: mono $\tfrac53$, diatomic $\tfrac75$. Isobaric: $Q=\dfrac{\gamma}{\gamma-1}W$.
- Isothermal $W=nRT\ln\dfrac{V_2}{V_1}$; adiabatic $PV^\gamma=$ const, $TV^{\gamma-1}=$ const, $W=\dfrac{P_1V_1-P_2V_2}{\gamma-1}$. Polytropic $PV^n=$ const: $C=C_v+\dfrac{R}{1-n}$.
- Adiabatic against constant external pressure (irreversible): $nC_v(T_2-T_1)=-P_{ext}(V_2-V_1)$.
- Efficiencies: heat engine $\eta=1-\dfrac{T_2}{T_1}$ (Carnot), refrigerator $\beta=\dfrac{T_2}{T_1-T_2}$. Free expansion: $Q=W=\Delta U=0$.
- Work in a cyclic process = area enclosed; $\Delta U=0$ over a cycle.
## Common traps
- Sign conventions ($W$ by vs on the gas). Two adiabats between two isotherms give $\dfrac{V_a}{V_d}=\dfrac{V_b}{V_c}$.
