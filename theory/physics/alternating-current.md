# Alternating Current — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/physics/alternating-current.md](../../questions/physics/alternating-current.md)

## Key ideas
- $I_{rms}=\dfrac{I_0}{\sqrt2}$; for $i=I_1+I_2\sin\omega t$, $I_{rms}^2=I_1^2+\tfrac12I_2^2$. Reactances $X_L=\omega L$, $X_C=\dfrac{1}{\omega C}$.
- Series LCR: $Z=\sqrt{R^2+(X_L-X_C)^2}$, $\tan\phi=\dfrac{X_L-X_C}{R}$, power $P=V_{rms}I_{rms}\cos\phi$. Resonance: $\omega_0=\dfrac1{\sqrt{LC}}$, $Z=R$, current maximum ($I=V/R$), $Q=\dfrac{\omega_0L}{R}$.
- Pure $L$ or $C$: current and voltage $90^\circ$ apart, zero average power. A dielectric in a capacitor raises $C$, lowers $X_C$, raises current.
- Transformer $\dfrac{V_s}{V_p}=\dfrac{N_s}{N_p}$, efficiency $=\dfrac{P_{out}}{P_{in}}$. A coil on DC behaves only as $R$; on AC $Z=\sqrt{R^2+X_L^2}$.
- Displacement current in a capacitor $I_d=\omega CV_0$ (amplitude), equal to the conduction current.
## Common traps
- Voltmeters/ammeters read rms. Resonance frequency does not depend on $R$.
