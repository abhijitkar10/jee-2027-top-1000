# Capacitors — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/physics/capacitors.md](../../questions/physics/capacitors.md)

## Key ideas
- $C=\dfrac{Q}{V}$; parallel plate $C=\dfrac{\varepsilon_0A}{d}$; with a slab of thickness $t$ and constant $K$: $C=\dfrac{\varepsilon_0A}{d-t+t/K}$. Series $\dfrac1C=\sum\dfrac1{C_i}$, parallel $C=\sum C_i$.
- Energy $U=\tfrac12CV^2=\dfrac{Q^2}{2C}$; energy density $\tfrac12\varepsilon_0E^2$. Force between plates $F=\dfrac{Q^2}{2\varepsilon_0A}$.
- **Battery connected:** $V$ fixed; inserting a dielectric raises $C$ and $U$ by $K$. **Battery disconnected:** $Q$ fixed; $V$ and $U$ fall by $K$.
- Sharing charge between two capacitors: $V=\dfrac{C_1V_1+C_2V_2}{C_1+C_2}$; heat lost $=\dfrac{C_1C_2(V_1-V_2)^2}{2(C_1+C_2)}$.
- Discharge through $R$: $q=q_0e^{-t/RC}$; charging $q=q_0(1-e^{-t/RC})$; in steady state capacitors carry no current.
## Common traps
- Stair-like or partially filled plates = capacitors in parallel/series by region. Separate what is constant ($Q$ or $V$) first.
