# Calorimetry, Thermal Expansion & Heat Transfer — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/physics/heat-transfer.md](../../questions/physics/heat-transfer.md)

## Key ideas
- $Q=ms\Delta T$, $Q=mL$. Temperature scales: $\dfrac{C}{5}=\dfrac{F-32}{9}=\dfrac{K-273}{5}$; a change of $1^\circ$C $=1.8^\circ$F. Platinum resistance thermometer: $t=\dfrac{R_t-R_0}{R_{100}-R_0}\times100$.
- **Expansion:** $\Delta L=L\alpha\Delta T$, $\gamma=2\alpha\ (\text{area}),\ 3\alpha\ (\text{volume})$.
- **Conduction:** $\dfrac{Q}{t}=\dfrac{kA\Delta T}{L}$; series $R=\sum\dfrac{L}{kA}$, parallel conductances add.
- **Radiation:** Stefan $P=e\sigma AT^4$; Wien $\lambda_mT=b$; Newton cooling $\dfrac{dT}{dt}=-k(T-T_0)$. Black-body plates between two plates in steady state: the same power passes each gap, so $n$ gaps give $W_0/W_S=n$.
## Common traps
- Use kelvin in $T^4$. Latent-heat problems: check whether the whole mass changes phase.
