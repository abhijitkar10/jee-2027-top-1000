# Work, Energy & Power — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/physics/work-energy-power.md](../../questions/physics/work-energy-power.md)

## Key ideas
- $W=\int\vec F\cdot d\vec r$; work–energy theorem $W_{net}=\Delta KE$. Conservative forces: $W=-\Delta U$, path independent (gravity work depends only on height).
- Momentum–energy link: $p=\sqrt{2mK}$. For equal $K$, $p\propto\sqrt m$; for equal $p$, $K\propto1/m$.
- **Power:** $P=\vec F\cdot\vec v$. Constant power $\Rightarrow v\propto t^{1/2}$, $x\propto t^{3/2}$.
- Spring: $U=\tfrac12kx^2$; block sliding on rough incline into a spring: use $mg\sin\theta\,(d+x)-\mu mg\cos\theta\,(d+x)=\tfrac12kx^2$.
- Loss in an inelastic bounce: $e=\sqrt{h'/h}$; energy loss fraction $1-e^2$.
## Common traps
- Rolling up a *smooth* incline conserves rotational KE (no torque), unlike a rough incline. Friction in pure rolling does no work.
