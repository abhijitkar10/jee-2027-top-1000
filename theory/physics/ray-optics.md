# Ray Optics & Optical Instruments — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/physics/ray-optics.md](../../questions/physics/ray-optics.md)

## Key ideas
- Mirror $\dfrac1v+\dfrac1u=\dfrac1f$, $f=\dfrac R2$. Refraction at a spherical surface $\dfrac{n_2}{v}-\dfrac{n_1}{u}=\dfrac{n_2-n_1}{R}$. Thin lens $\dfrac1v-\dfrac1u=\dfrac1f=(\mu-1)\left(\dfrac1{R_1}-\dfrac1{R_2}\right)$; power $P=\dfrac1f$ (m), combination $P=\sum P_i$; lenses in contact.
- Snell $n_1\sin i=n_2\sin r$; critical angle $\sin C=\dfrac{n_2}{n_1}$; TIR needs denser $\to$ rarer and $i>C$. Lateral shift in a slab $d=\dfrac{t\sin(i-r)}{\cos r}$; apparent depth $=\dfrac{\text{real}}{n}$.
- **Prism:** $\mu=\dfrac{\sin\frac{A+\delta_m}{2}}{\sin\frac A2}$; thin prism $\delta=(\mu-1)A$; dispersion $\mu_v>\mu_r$ (red deviates least).
- Microscope $M=\dfrac{L}{f_o}\left(1+\dfrac D{f_e}\right)$; telescope $M=\dfrac{f_o}{f_e}$ (normal adjustment).
- A point source at the focus gives a plane wavefront.
## Common traps
- Sign convention (Cartesian): distances measured against the incident light are negative. A concave lens always gives a virtual erect diminished image.
