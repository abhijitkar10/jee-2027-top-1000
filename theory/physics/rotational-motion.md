# Rotational Motion — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/physics/rotational-motion.md](../../questions/physics/rotational-motion.md)

## Key ideas
- $\tau=I\alpha=\dfrac{dL}{dt}$, $L=I\omega$, $KE=\tfrac12I\omega^2$. Parallel axis $I=I_{cm}+Md^2$; perpendicular axis (lamina) $I_z=I_x+I_y$.
- Moments of inertia about the centre/diameter: ring $MR^2$ ($\tfrac12MR^2$ about diameter), disc $\tfrac12MR^2$ ($\tfrac14MR^2$), solid sphere $\tfrac25MR^2$, hollow sphere $\tfrac23MR^2$, rod (centre) $\tfrac1{12}ML^2$, rod (end) $\tfrac13ML^2$.
- **Rolling without slipping:** $v=\omega R$; $KE=\tfrac12Mv^2\left(1+\dfrac{k^2}{R^2}\right)$. Acceleration on a rough incline $a=\dfrac{g\sin\theta}{1+k^2/R^2}$; height reached $h=\dfrac{v^2}{2g}\left(1+\dfrac{k^2}{R^2}\right)$.
- Angular momentum is conserved when no external torque acts (a disc dropped on a spinning disc, changing radius of the earth, ice skater).
- Impulse $J$ at the end of a free rod: $\omega=\dfrac{J(L/2)}{ML^2/12}$.
## Common traps
- Use the axis about which torque and $I$ are both taken. Friction in rolling is static and does no work.
