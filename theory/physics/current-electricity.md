# Current Electricity — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/physics/current-electricity.md](../../questions/physics/current-electricity.md)

## Key ideas
- $I=nAev_d$; $R=\rho\dfrac lA$ (stretching a wire: volume constant, $R\propto l^2\propto r^{-4}$); $R_t=R_0(1+\alpha\Delta T)$.
- Kirchhoff: $\sum I=0$ at a node, $\sum\Delta V=0$ in a loop. Cell: $V=E-Ir$; max power transfer when $R=r$.
- Power $P=VI=I^2R=\dfrac{V^2}{R}$; at reduced voltage $P\propto V^2$. Heating time $\propto R\propto l$.
- **Instruments:** galvanometer $\to$ ammeter, shunt $S=\dfrac{I_gG}{I-I_g}$; $\to$ voltmeter, series $R=\dfrac VI_g-G$. Ammeter resistance $\dfrac{GS}{G+S}$.
- **Meter bridge:** $\dfrac RS=\dfrac{l}{100-l}$; **potentiometer:** $\dfrac{E_1}{E_2}=\dfrac{l_1}{l_2}$, internal resistance $r=R\dfrac{l_1-l_2}{l_2}$.
- Wheatstone balance: $\dfrac PQ=\dfrac RS$; ideal voltmeter draws no current, ideal ammeter has zero resistance.
## Common traps
- Redraw the circuit at nodes of equal potential. Capacitor branches carry no steady current.
