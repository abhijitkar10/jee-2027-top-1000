# Electrostatics — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/physics/electrostatics.md](../../questions/physics/electrostatics.md)

## Key ideas
- Coulomb $F=\dfrac{q_1q_2}{4\pi\varepsilon_0r^2}$; field of point charge $\dfrac{kq}{r^2}$; potential $\dfrac{kq}{r}$.
- **Gauss:** $\Phi=\dfrac{q_{enc}}{\varepsilon_0}$. Infinite sheet $\dfrac{\sigma}{2\varepsilon_0}$, conductor surface $\dfrac{\sigma}{\varepsilon_0}$, line charge $\dfrac{\lambda}{2\pi\varepsilon_0r}$. A charge at the centre of a cube face gives $\dfrac{q}{2\varepsilon_0}$ through the cube; at a corner $\dfrac{q}{8\varepsilon_0}$ (cube).
- Dipole: axial $E=\dfrac{2kp}{r^3}$, equatorial $\dfrac{kp}{r^3}$; torque $\tau=pE\sin\theta$; $U=-\vec p\cdot\vec E$.
- Potential energy of a system $U=\sum\dfrac{kq_iq_j}{r_{ij}}$. Half ring of radius $R$: $V_{centre}=\pi k\lambda$.
- Connected conductors reach equal potential: $\dfrac{q_1}{q_2}=\dfrac{r_1}{r_2}$. Identical-sphere contact shares the charge equally.
- Equilibrium of charged spheres on threads: $\tan\theta=\dfrac{F_e}{mg}$; in a liquid replace $mg$ by $mg(1-\rho_l/\rho)$ and $F_e$ by $F_e/K$.
## Common traps
- Potential is scalar (add algebraically), field is vector. Inside a hollow conductor $E=0$ but $V\ne0$.
