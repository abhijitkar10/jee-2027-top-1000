# Waves & Sound — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/physics/waves-sound.md](../../questions/physics/waves-sound.md)

## Key ideas
- Travelling wave $y=A\sin(\omega t-kx)$; $v=f\lambda=\omega/k$. String $v=\sqrt{T/\mu}$. Sound in a gas $v=\sqrt{\gamma P/\rho}=\sqrt{\gamma RT/M}$.
- **Standing waves:** string fixed at both ends $f_n=\dfrac{nv}{2L}$; open pipe $f_n=\dfrac{nv}{2L}$ (all harmonics); closed pipe $f_n=\dfrac{(2n-1)v}{4L}$ (odd harmonics). $n$th overtone: open $(n+1)$th harmonic, closed $(2n+1)$th.
- Intensity $I=\tfrac12\rho v\omega^2A^2$; decibel $\beta=10\log\dfrac{I}{I_0}$. Beats $f_b=|f_1-f_2|$.
- **Doppler:** $f'=f\dfrac{v\pm v_o}{v\mp v_s}$ (upper sign when approaching).
- Reflection at a denser boundary inverts phase ($\pi$); a wave crossing strings keeps $\omega$, and $k=\omega/v$ changes.
- Tension change in a sonometer: $f\propto\sqrt T$.
## Common traps
- End correction for pipes if given. Take $T$ from the *string* tension, not weights of unrelated masses.
