# Oscillations (SHM) — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/physics/oscillations.md](../../questions/physics/oscillations.md)

## Key ideas
- $x=A\sin(\omega t+\phi)$, $v=\omega\sqrt{A^2-x^2}$, $a=-\omega^2x$; $v_{max}=A\omega$, $a_{max}=A\omega^2$.
- $E=\tfrac12kA^2=\tfrac12m\omega^2A^2$ (independent of mass for the same $k$, $A$); $KE=\tfrac12k(A^2-x^2)$.
- Spring–mass $T=2\pi\sqrt{m/k}$; series $\dfrac1k=\sum\dfrac1{k_i}$, parallel $k=\sum k_i$. Simple pendulum $T=2\pi\sqrt{l/g}$ ($g_{eff}$ in accelerating frames); physical pendulum $T=2\pi\sqrt{I/mgd}$.
- Energy method: write $KE=\tfrac12M_{eff}\dot q^2$ and $PU=\tfrac12K_{eff}q^2$; $\omega^2=K_{eff}/M_{eff}$ (e.g. a disc rolling in a ring with a spring $\Rightarrow\omega^2=\tfrac23\left[\tfrac g{R-r}+\tfrac km\right]$).
- Superposition of two SHMs of equal frequency: $A^2=A_1^2+A_2^2+2A_1A_2\cos\delta$.
## Common traps
- Convert frequency to $\omega=2\pi f$. For a spring on an incline only the *equilibrium* position shifts; $T$ is unchanged.
