# Elasticity, Fluids, Surface Tension & Viscosity — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/physics/properties-of-matter.md](../../questions/physics/properties-of-matter.md)

## Key ideas
- **Elasticity:** $Y=\dfrac{F/A}{\Delta L/L}$, $B=-\dfrac{\Delta P}{\Delta V/V}$, $\eta=\dfrac{\text{shear stress}}{\text{shear strain}}$. Energy density $\tfrac12\times$stress$\times$strain. Spring constant of a wire $k=\dfrac{YA}{L}$. Series wires: tension the same, extensions add.
- **Hydrostatics:** $P=P_0+\rho gh$; Pascal $F_2=F_1\dfrac{A_2}{A_1}$; buoyancy $F_b=\rho_{liq}Vg$. Floating: weight = buoyancy.
- **Fluid flow:** continuity $A_1v_1=A_2v_2$; Bernoulli $P+\tfrac12\rho v^2+\rho gh=$ const; Torricelli $v=\sqrt{2gh}$.
- **Surface tension:** excess pressure drop $\dfrac{2S}{R}$, soap bubble $\dfrac{4S}{R}$; capillary rise $h=\dfrac{2S\cos\theta}{r\rho g}$. Coalescence of $n$ drops: $R=n^{1/3}r$, energy released $=4\pi r^2S\,n\left(1-n^{-1/3}\right)$.
- **Viscosity:** Stokes $F=6\pi\eta rv$; terminal speed $v_T=\dfrac{2r^2(\rho-\sigma)g}{9\eta}\propto r^2$.
## Common traps
- A soap bubble has *two* surfaces (work $=2S\Delta A$). At terminal speed viscous force $=mg-$ buoyancy.
