# Centre of Mass & Collisions — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/physics/centre-of-mass-collisions.md](../../questions/physics/centre-of-mass-collisions.md)

## Key ideas
- $\vec r_{cm}=\dfrac{\sum m_i\vec r_i}{\sum m_i}$; with no external force $\vec v_{cm}$ is constant. In a two-body internal motion $m_1\Delta x_1=m_2\Delta x_2$.
- **Collisions:** momentum always conserved. Elastic: also KE. Coefficient of restitution $e=\dfrac{v_2-v_1}{u_1-u_2}$.
- 1-D elastic: $v_1=\dfrac{m_1-m_2}{m_1+m_2}u_1$, $v_2=\dfrac{2m_1}{m_1+m_2}u_1$. Equal masses exchange velocities.
- Perfectly inelastic: $v=\dfrac{m_1u_1+m_2u_2}{m_1+m_2}$; KE lost $=\dfrac12\mu_{red}(u_1-u_2)^2$.
- Elastic scattering of $m_1$ on stationary $m_2$: if $m_1>m_2$ the maximum deflection satisfies $\sin\theta_{max}=\dfrac{m_2}{m_1}$.
- Ballistic pendulum: $v=\dfrac{m+M}{m}\sqrt{2gh}$.
## Common traps
- A bullet embedding in a bob: momentum conserved *during* the collision, energy conserved only *afterwards*.
