# Statistics — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/maths/statistics.md](../../questions/maths/statistics.md)

## Key ideas
- Mean, median; mean deviation about $a$: $\dfrac{\sum|x_i-a|}{n}$; variance $\sigma^2=\dfrac{\sum x_i^2}n-\bar x^2$; standard deviation. Correcting a wrong observation: recompute $\sum x$ and $\sum x^2$.
- Adding a constant leaves variance unchanged; multiplying by $k$ scales it by $k^2$. Frequency distributions: use cumulative frequency for the median.
