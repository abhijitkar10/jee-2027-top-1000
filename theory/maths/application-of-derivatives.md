# Differentiation & Application of Derivatives — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/maths/application-of-derivatives.md](../../questions/maths/application-of-derivatives.md)

## Key ideas
- Chain, product, quotient, parametric, implicit, logarithmic differentiation; inverse function $g'(y)=\dfrac1{f'(x)}$.
- Increasing where $f'>0$; local max/min via sign change or $f''$; critical points include where $f'$ is undefined. Range of rational functions by discriminant. Optimisation: area, distance, functional inequalities (e.g. $x^x$ increases for $x>1/e$).
- Tangent/normal, Rolle, Mean value theorem. $y=5x^4-2x^2-4$ type: increasing where $y'>0$.
