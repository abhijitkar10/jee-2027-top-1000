# Binomial Theorem — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/maths/binomial-theorem.md](../../questions/maths/binomial-theorem.md)

## Key ideas
- $T_{r+1}=\binom nr x^{n-r}a^r$; middle term(s); greatest coefficient $\binom n{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}$; $\sum\binom nr=2^n$; $\binom nr=\binom n{n-r}$.
- Constant/independent term: set the power of $x$ to zero. Rational terms: exponents integral. Coefficient of $x^k$ in a product: convolution.
- Ratio of consecutive coefficients $\dfrac{T_{r+1}}{T_r}=\dfrac{n-r+1}{r}\dfrac ax$. Coefficients in AP/GP give equations in $n$.
- Sum of coefficients: put $x=1$. Remainders using $(1+x)^n$ modular expansion.
