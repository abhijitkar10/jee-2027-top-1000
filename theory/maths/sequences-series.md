# Sequences & Series — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/maths/sequences-series.md](../../questions/maths/sequences-series.md)

## Key ideas
- AP: $a_n=a+(n-1)d$, $S_n=\tfrac n2[2a+(n-1)d]$. GP: $S_n=a\dfrac{r^n-1}{r-1}$, $S_\infty=\dfrac a{1-r}$ ($|r|<1$). AM $\ge$ GM $\ge$ HM.
- $\sum k=\tfrac{n(n+1)}2$, $\sum k^2=\tfrac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}6$, $\sum k^3=\left[\tfrac{n(n+1)}2\right]^2$. Telescoping: $\dfrac1{k(k+1)}=\dfrac1k-\dfrac1{k+1}$; $\dfrac1{\sqrt k+\sqrt{k+1}}=\sqrt{k+1}-\sqrt k$.
- Common terms of two APs form an AP with $d=\text{lcm}$. Exponential Cauchy equation gives GP of $f(a_i)$.
- Arithmetico-geometric series: multiply by $r$ and subtract.
