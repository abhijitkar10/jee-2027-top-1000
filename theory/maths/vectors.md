# Vectors — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/maths/vectors.md](../../questions/maths/vectors.md)

## Key ideas
- $\vec a\cdot\vec b=|a||b|\cos\theta$; $|\vec a\times\vec b|=|a||b|\sin\theta$ (area of parallelogram); projection of $\vec a$ on $\vec b$ $=\dfrac{\vec a\cdot\vec b}{|b|}$; scalar triple product $=0$ iff coplanar; $\vec a\times(\vec b\times\vec c)=(\vec a\cdot\vec c)\vec b-(\vec a\cdot\vec b)\vec c$.
- $\vec r\times\vec a=\vec b\times\vec a\Rightarrow\vec r=\vec b+t\vec a$. Angle bisector divides opposite side in ratio of adjacent sides. Centroid: $\vec S=\dfrac{P+5Q+6R}{12}$ for weights 1, 5, 6.
