# Sets, Relations & Functions — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/maths/sets-relations-functions.md](../../questions/maths/sets-relations-functions.md)

## Key ideas
- $n(A\cup B)=n(A)+n(B)-n(A\cap B)$. Relations: reflexive, symmetric, transitive; equivalence = all three (partitions the set).
- Counting relations on an $n$-set: total $2^{n^2}$; reflexive $2^{n^2-n}$; symmetric $2^{n(n+1)/2}$. Reflexive + symmetric with exactly $n+2k$ pairs: $\binom{\binom n2}{k}$.
- Functions: one-one (injective), onto (range = codomain); $f\circ g$ one-one $\Rightarrow$ $g$ one-one, onto $\Rightarrow$ $f$ onto. Number of one-one maps $A\to B$: $\dfrac{|B|!}{(|B|-|A|)!}$.
- Range via discriminant ($y=\dfrac{ax^2+bx+c}{px^2+qx+r}$); check periodicity, domain restrictions, greatest integer function.
- Inverse exists iff bijective; $g'(f(a))=\dfrac1{f'(a)}$.
