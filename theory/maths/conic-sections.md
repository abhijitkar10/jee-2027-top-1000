# Parabola, Ellipse & Hyperbola — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/maths/conic-sections.md](../../questions/maths/conic-sections.md)

## Key ideas
- Parabola $y^2=4ax$: focus $(a,0)$, latus rectum $4a$, focal chord length $\dfrac{4a}{\sin^2\theta}$, tangent $y=mx+\dfrac am$. Ellipse: $e^2=1-\dfrac{b^2}{a^2}$, latus rectum $\dfrac{2b^2}a$, focal sum $2a$. Hyperbola: $e^2=1+\dfrac{b^2}{a^2}$; product of focal distances of $(x_0,y_0)$ is $e^2x_0^2-a^2$ type expressions; conjugate axes.
- Tangent/normal conditions: ellipse $c^2=a^2m^2+b^2$, hyperbola $c^2=a^2m^2-b^2$. Auxiliary circle for ellipse ($x^2+y^2=a^2$). Locus by ratio division gives a scaled ellipse.
