# Matrices & Determinants — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/maths/matrices-determinants.md](../../questions/maths/matrices-determinants.md)

## Key ideas
- $|AB|=|A||B|$, $|kA|=k^n|A|$ ($n\times n$), $|A^T|=|A|$, $\text{adj}(A)=|A|A^{-1}$, $|\text{adj}A|=|A|^{n-1}$, $A\,\text{adj}A=|A|I$, $(AB)^{-1}=B^{-1}A^{-1}$.
- Systems $AX=B$: unique if $|A|\ne0$; if $|A|=0$, infinitely many when consistent (all $D_i=0$ or third equation dependent), no solution otherwise. Homogeneous: non-trivial solution iff $|A|=0$.
- Orthogonal matrix $A^TA=I$; reflection $I-2MM^T$ has eigenvalues $\pm1$. Cayley–Hamilton to reduce powers.
- Integer orthogonal matrices are signed permutations.
