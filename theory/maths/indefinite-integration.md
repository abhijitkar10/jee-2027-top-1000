# Indefinite Integration — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/maths/indefinite-integration.md](../../questions/maths/indefinite-integration.md)

## Key ideas
- Standard forms; substitution; parts (ILATE); partial fractions; $\int e^x[f+f']dx=e^xf$; $\int\dfrac{dx}{a^2\sin^2x+b^2\cos^2x}=\dfrac1{ab}\tan^{-1}\left(\dfrac ba\tan x\right)$; reduction formulas for $\csc^nx$, $\sec^nx$.
- Rationalise $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{(x-a)^m(x-b)^n}}$ with $m+n=2$ style substitution $t=\dfrac{x-a}{x-b}$.
