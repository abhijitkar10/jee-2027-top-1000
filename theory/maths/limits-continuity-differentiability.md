# Limits, Continuity & Differentiability — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/maths/limits-continuity-differentiability.md](../../questions/maths/limits-continuity-differentiability.md)

## Key ideas
- Standard limits $\dfrac{\sin x}x,\dfrac{\tan x}x,\dfrac{e^x-1}x,\dfrac{\ln(1+x)}x\to1$, $\dfrac{1-\cos x}{x^2}\to\tfrac12$, $(1+x)^{1/x}\to e$, $\dfrac{a^x-1}{x}\to\ln a$. Use Taylor expansions/L'Hôpital for $0/0$; $1^\infty$: $e^{\lim g(f-1)}$.
- Continuity: LHL = RHL = $f(a)$. Differentiability implies continuity; check left/right derivatives at kinks ($|x|$, greatest integer, piecewise). $x^2\sin\tfrac1x$ (extended by 0) is differentiable at 0 but $f'$ is discontinuous there.
- Number of non-differentiable points of $|f|$: simple roots of $f$ (not repeated roots).
