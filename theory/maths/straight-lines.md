# Straight Lines — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/maths/straight-lines.md](../../questions/maths/straight-lines.md)

## Key ideas
- Slope form, two-point, intercept form $\dfrac xa+\dfrac yb=1$; distance $\dfrac{|ax_0+by_0+c|}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}$; parallel-line distance $\dfrac{|c_1-c_2|}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}$.
- Angle between lines $\tan\theta=\left|\dfrac{m_1-m_2}{1+m_1m_2}\right|$; reflection of a point in a line; image formula.
- Bisectors, family $L_1+\lambda L_2=0$; centroid, orthocentre, circumcentre, incentre; area via determinant. Ray-reflection problems: use images. Minimum-area triangle cut by a line through $(a,b)$ has area $2ab$.
