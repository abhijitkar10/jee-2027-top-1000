# 3D Geometry — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/maths/three-d-geometry.md](../../questions/maths/three-d-geometry.md)

## Key ideas
- Line $\dfrac{x-x_1}a=\dfrac{y-y_1}b=\dfrac{z-z_1}c$; plane $ax+by+cz=d$; direction of intersection of two planes $=\vec n_1\times\vec n_2$.
- Shortest distance between skew lines $=\dfrac{|(\vec a_2-\vec a_1)\cdot(\vec d_1\times\vec d_2)|}{|\vec d_1\times\vec d_2|}$; distance of a point from a plane $\dfrac{|ax_0+by_0+cz_0-d|}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2+c^2}}$; foot/image of a point in a plane or a line.
- Angle between line and plane: $\sin\theta=\dfrac{|\vec d\cdot\vec n|}{|d||n|}$. Coplanarity via triple product.
