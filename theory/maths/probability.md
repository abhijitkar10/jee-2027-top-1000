# Probability — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/maths/probability.md](../../questions/maths/probability.md)

## Key ideas
- $P(A|B)=\dfrac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}$; total probability; **Bayes** $P(E_i|A)=\dfrac{P(E_i)P(A|E_i)}{\sum P(E_j)P(A|E_j)}$. Independent events multiply; $P(\text{at least one})=1-\prod(1-p_i)$.
- Binomial $P(X=r)=\binom nrp^rq^{n-r}$, mean $np$, variance $npq$; hypergeometric (without replacement) variance $n\dfrac KN\left(1-\dfrac KN\right)\dfrac{N-n}{N-1}$. Random variable mean $\sum xP$, variance $\sum x^2P-\mu^2$.
- Counting favourable cases: repeated roots ($b^2=4ac$), dice sums, distributions of letters into addresses.
