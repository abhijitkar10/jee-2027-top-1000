# Differential Equations — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/maths/differential-equations.md](../../questions/maths/differential-equations.md)

## Key ideas
- Variable separable; homogeneous ($y=vx$); linear $\dfrac{dy}{dx}+Py=Q$ with IF $e^{\int P\,dx}$; Bernoulli ($u=y^{1-n}$); exact; substitution $u=x+y$ for $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=f(x+y)$.
- Formation of a DE: differentiate the family and eliminate constants (order = number of constants). Circles through the origin with centre on $y=x$ give $(x^2-y^2+2xy)dx=(x^2-y^2-2xy)dy$.
- Solve $\dfrac{dx}{dy}$ when $x$ appears linearly. Initial conditions fix $C$.
