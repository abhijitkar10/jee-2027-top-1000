# Area Under Curves — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/maths/area-under-curves.md](../../questions/maths/area-under-curves.md)

## Key ideas
- Area $=\int|f-g|dx$ between intersection points (sketch first, integrate in $x$ or $y$, whichever is simpler). Standard: area between $y^2=4ax$ and $x^2=4by$ is $\dfrac{16ab}3$.
- Use symmetry; area under $|x|$-type functions splits at 0; hyperbola $y=\dfrac{16}{x+4}$ gives logarithmic terms.
