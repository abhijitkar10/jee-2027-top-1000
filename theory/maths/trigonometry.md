# Trigonometry & Inverse Trigonometry — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/maths/trigonometry.md](../../questions/maths/trigonometry.md)

## Key ideas
- $\sin(A\pm B)$, $\cos(A\pm B)$, $\tan(A\pm B)$; double/triple angle ($\sin3\theta=3\sin\theta-4\sin^3\theta$); product $\cos\theta\cos(60^\circ-\theta)\cos(60^\circ+\theta)=\tfrac14\cos3\theta$.
- $a\sin\theta+b\cos\theta=R\sin(\theta+\phi)$, range $[-\sqrt{a^2+b^2},\sqrt{a^2+b^2}]$. Values: $\sin18^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt5-1}4$, $\cos36^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt5+1}4$.
- Principal values: $\sin^{-1}\in[-\tfrac\pi2,\tfrac\pi2]$, $\cos^{-1}\in[0,\pi]$, $\tan^{-1}\in(-\tfrac\pi2,\tfrac\pi2)$; $\sin^{-1}x+\cos^{-1}x=\tfrac\pi2$.
- Equations: bound both sides (e.g. cubic in $\cos x$ with no root in $[-1,1]$). Triangle: sine/cosine rules.
