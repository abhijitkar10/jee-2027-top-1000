# Permutations & Combinations — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/maths/permutations-combinations.md](../../questions/maths/permutations-combinations.md)

## Key ideas
- $^nP_r=\dfrac{n!}{(n-r)!}$, $^nC_r=\dfrac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}$; arrangements with repetition $\dfrac{n!}{p!q!\ldots}$; circular $(n-1)!$.
- Dictionary rank: count words with smaller first letters, then next letter etc.
- Distribution: identical objects into distinct boxes $\binom{n+r-1}{r-1}$; non-negative solutions of $x+2y+3z=N$ by looping over $z$. Partitions into at most $k$ non-empty groups $=\sum S(n,i)$ (Stirling).
- Geometry counting: triangles $=\binom n3-\sum\binom{n_i}3$ (collinear); maximum intersection points of lines with parallel and concurrent sets.
- Inclusion–exclusion for "at least one" conditions; complement counting.
