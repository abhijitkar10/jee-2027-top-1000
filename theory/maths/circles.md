# Circles — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/maths/circles.md](../../questions/maths/circles.md)

## Key ideas
- $(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2$; general $x^2+y^2+2gx+2fy+c=0$, $r^2=g^2+f^2-c$. Tangent length $\sqrt{S_1}$; chord of contact $T=0$; midpoint chord $T=S_1$; radical axis $S_1-S_2=0$.
- Two circles: external tangent $d=r_1+r_2$, internal $d=|r_1-r_2|$, intersect at two points when $|r_1-r_2|<d<r_1+r_2$.
- Locus of midpoints of chords through a fixed point on the circle is a circle on the join as diameter. For a square inscribed in a circle the sum of $|P|^2$ over vertices is $4(|C|^2+r^2)$.
