# Quadratic Equations & Inequalities — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/maths/quadratic-equations.md](../../questions/maths/quadratic-equations.md)

## Key ideas
- $x=\dfrac{-b\pm\sqrt D}{2a}$; sum $-\dfrac ba$, product $\dfrac ca$; $D>0$ two real roots. $\alpha^2+\beta^2=(\alpha+\beta)^2-2\alpha\beta$; $(\alpha-\beta)^2=(\alpha+\beta)^2-4\alpha\beta$. New equation with roots $f(\alpha),f(\beta)$: use symmetric functions.
- Exponential quadratics: put $t=a^x$. Complex roots occur in conjugate pairs (real coefficients); irrational in conjugate surds (rational coefficients).
- Location of roots: sign of $af(k)$, vertex, $D$. Polynomial identity: a real polynomial of odd degree has a real root.
