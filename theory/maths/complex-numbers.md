# Complex Numbers — theory

> Questions: [../../questions/maths/complex-numbers.md](../../questions/maths/complex-numbers.md)

## Key ideas
- $z=x+iy=r e^{i\theta}$; $|z|^2=z\bar z$; $\arg$; De Moivre; $n$th roots of unity sum to 0.
- Triangle inequality $|z_1+z_2|\le|z_1|+|z_2|$; $|z_1-z_2|^2+|z_1+z_2|^2=2(|z_1|^2+|z_2|^2)$.
- Loci: $|z-z_1|=k|z-z_2|$ is a circle ($k\ne1$), a line ($k=1$); $|z-a|\le r$ disc; $\arg\dfrac{z-a}{z-b}=\text{const}$ is an arc. Minimum $|z+w|$ over $|z|\le1$ is $|w|-1$.
- $(\bar z)^2+|z|=0$ has solutions $z=\pm i$ (and $0$).
