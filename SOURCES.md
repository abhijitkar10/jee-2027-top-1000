## What was excluded

Questions relying on a figure or a structure image, questions whose text could not be rebuilt with confidence, and questions where my independent answer disagreed with the published key.

## Tools used

Papers were converted to text with a PDF-to-text service; all question reconstruction, solving and theory writing were done by hand (scripts in `scripts/` only validate and renumber files).
