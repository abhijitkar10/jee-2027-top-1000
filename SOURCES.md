# Sources

All questions are previous-year JEE questions. Nothing is made up.

## JEE Main

| Paper | Source used | Answer key |
|---|---|---|
| JEE Main 2026, January session (21, 22, 23, 24, 28 Jan; Shift 1 and 2) | MathonGo "JEE Main January 2026 Chapter-wise Questions with Answer Keys" (one PDF per chapter, English) | Key printed in the same PDF |
| JEE Main 2025, January and April sessions | MathonGo "JEE Main 2025 (January / April) Chapter-wise Question Bank" (one PDF per subject, with solutions) | Key and solutions printed in the same PDF |
| JEE Main 2024, January (27 Jan – 1 Feb) and April (4 – 9 Apr) sessions | MathonGo "JEE Main 2024 (date, shift) Previous Year Paper with Answer Keys" for the text questions; MathonGo "JEE Main 2024 January / April Question Bank" chapter PDFs for the figure questions | Key printed in the same PDF |
| JEE Main 2023 (January) and 2022 | MathonGo chapter-wise PDFs, used only for chapters that left the Main syllabus (Solid State, States of Matter, Surface Chemistry, s-block, Metallurgy, Mathematical Reasoning) | Official NTA key as printed in the PDF |

NTA publishes the original papers and provisional/final keys at <https://jeemain.nta.nic.in>. The MathonGo PDFs reproduce those papers and keys. Figures were cropped from these PDFs.

## Verification status

See the "Answer status" section of the README. In short: 2024 and Advanced entries were independently re-derived (`key ✔`); 2025, 2026 and the added 2024 figure questions follow the published key and have a checked worked solution (`key (compiled)`); 7 Sequences & Series questions have no published key and carry my own answer (`answer derived`).

## What was left out

Questions whose statement could not be rebuilt with confidence, questions where the printed options contradict the key or are ambiguous, and questions that depend on a figure I could not read reliably.

## Tools

PDFs were read with PyMuPDF. Every question, solution and note was written by hand; the scripts in `scripts/` only validate, select by quota, number, and generate the plan and tracker.
