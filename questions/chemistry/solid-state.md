### § — JEE Main 2022 · 24 Jun Shift 1 · Q55 · Numerical

Atoms of element X form an hcp lattice and those of element Y occupy $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of its tetrahedral voids. The percentage of element X in the lattice is ______. (Nearest integer)

**Answer: 43** · key ✔

**Solution.** In hcp, for every atom of X there are 2 tetrahedral voids (and 1 octahedral void). If Y fills $\tfrac23$ of the tetrahedral voids, Y atoms per X atom $=2\times\tfrac23=\tfrac43$, so the formula is $\text{X}_3\text{Y}_4$. Percentage of X $=\dfrac{3}{3+4}\times100=42.86\approx 43$.

### § — JEE Main 2023 · 29 Jan Shift 2 · Solid set Q2 · Numerical

A metal M forms a hexagonal close-packed structure. The total number of voids in 0.02 mol of it is ______ $\times10^{21}$ (nearest integer). (Given $N_A=6.02\times10^{23}$)

**Answer: 36** · key (compiled)

**Solution.** In hcp (and ccp) every atom comes with 1 octahedral and 2 tetrahedral voids, i.e. 3 voids per atom. Number of voids $=3\times0.02\times6.02\times10^{23}=3.6\times10^{22}\approx36\times10^{21}$.

### § — JEE Main 2023 · 30 Jan Shift 2 · Solid set Q3 · Numerical

Iron oxide FeO crystallises in a cubic lattice with a unit cell edge length of 5.0 Å. If the density of FeO in the crystal is 4.0 g cm$^{-3}$, then the number of FeO units present per unit cell is ______ (nearest integer). Given: molar mass of Fe and O is 56 and 16 g mol$^{-1}$ respectively; $N_A=6.0\times10^{23}$ mol$^{-1}$.

**Answer: 4** · key (compiled)

**Solution.** $d=\dfrac{ZM}{N_Aa^3}\Rightarrow Z=\dfrac{4.0\times6.0\times10^{23}\times(5.0\times10^{-8})^3}{72}=\dfrac{4.0\times6.0\times10^{23}\times1.25\times10^{-22}}{72}=4.17\approx4$.

### § — JEE Main 2023 · 31 Jan Shift 2 · Solid set Q4 · Numerical

A sample of a metal oxide has the formula M$_{0.83}$O$_{1.00}$. The metal M can exist in two oxidation states, $+2$ and $+3$. In the sample of M$_{0.83}$O$_{1.00}$, the percentage of metal ions existing in $+2$ oxidation state is ______ % (nearest integer).

**Answer: 59** · key (compiled)

**Solution.** Let $x$ be the amount of M$^{2+}$; then $M^{3+}=0.83-x$. Charge neutrality with O$^{2-}$: $2x+3(0.83-x)=2\Rightarrow x=0.49$. Percentage of M$^{2+}=\dfrac{0.49}{0.83}\times100=59\%$.

### § — JEE Main 2023 · 1 Feb Shift 2 · Solid set Q6 · Numerical

A metal M crystallises into two lattices, face-centred cubic (fcc) and body-centred cubic (bcc), with unit cell edge lengths of 2.0 Å and 2.5 Å respectively. The ratio of the densities of the lattices fcc to bcc for the metal M is ______ (nearest integer).

**Answer: 4** · key (compiled)

**Solution.** $d=\dfrac{ZM}{N_Aa^3}$. $\dfrac{d_{fcc}}{d_{bcc}}=\dfrac{4/(2.0)^3}{2/(2.5)^3}=\dfrac{4}{8}\cdot\dfrac{15.625}{2}=3.9\approx4$.
