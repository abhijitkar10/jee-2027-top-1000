### § — JEE Main 2022 · 24 Jun Shift 1 · Q55 · Numerical

Atoms of element X form an hcp lattice and those of element Y occupy $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of its tetrahedral voids. The percentage of element X in the lattice is ______. (Nearest integer)

**Answer: 43** · key ✔

**Solution.** In hcp, for every atom of X there are 2 tetrahedral voids (and 1 octahedral void). If Y fills $\tfrac23$ of the tetrahedral voids, Y atoms per X atom $=2\times\tfrac23=\tfrac43$, so the formula is $\text{X}_3\text{Y}_4$. Percentage of X $=\dfrac{3}{3+4}\times100=42.86\approx 43$.
