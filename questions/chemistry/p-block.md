### C-PBL-01 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 1 · Q36 · MCQ

Assertion (A): The melting point of boron is unusually high among Group 13 elements.
Reason (R): Solid boron has a very strong crystalline lattice.

(1) Both true, R is not the correct explanation  (2) Both true, R is the correct explanation  (3) A true, R false  (4) A false, R true

**Answer: (2)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Boron exists as a covalent network of $\text{B}_{12}$ icosahedral units, so a very strong lattice must be broken to melt it (m.p. about 2453 K). This explains the anomalously high melting point.

### C-PBL-02 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 1 · Q43 · MCQ

The element not showing a variable oxidation state is

(1) Bromine  (2) Iodine  (3) Chlorine  (4) Fluorine

**Answer: (4)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Fluorine is the most electronegative element and has no vacant d-orbitals, so it shows only $-1$ (besides 0). Cl, Br and I show $+1,+3,+5,+7$.

### C-PBL-03 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 2 · Q40 · MCQ

Statement I: Oxygen, the first member of group 16, exhibits only the $-2$ oxidation state.
Statement II: Down group 16 the stability of the $+4$ oxidation state decreases and that of the $+6$ oxidation state increases.

(1) I correct, II incorrect  (2) Both correct  (3) Both incorrect  (4) I incorrect, II correct

**Answer: (3)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Oxygen also shows $-1$ (peroxides), $-\tfrac12$ (superoxides), $-\tfrac13$ (ozonide) and $+2$ (in $\text{OF}_2$), so I is wrong. Because of the inert-pair effect, down the group the $+4$ state becomes more stable and $+6$ less stable, the reverse of II, so II is wrong.

### C-PBL-05 — JEE Main 2024 · 29 Jan Shift 1 · Q36 · MCQ

Statement I: The electronegativity of group 14 elements from Si to Pb gradually decreases.
Statement II: Group 14 contains non-metallic, metallic as well as metalloid elements.

(1) I false, II true  (2) I true, II false  (3) Both true  (4) Both false

### C-PBL-08 — JEE Main 2024 · 30 Jan Shift 2 · Q33 · MCQ

Assertion A: $\text{H}_2\text{Te}$ is more acidic than $\text{H}_2\text{S}$.
Reason R: The bond dissociation enthalpy of $\text{H}_2\text{Te}$ is lower than that of $\text{H}_2\text{S}$.

### C-PBL-09 — JEE Main 2024 · 30 Jan Shift 2 · Q41 · MCQ

Choose the correct statements about the hydrides of group 15 elements.
A. Stability decreases in the order $\text{NH}_3>\text{PH}_3>\text{AsH}_3>\text{SbH}_3>\text{BiH}_3$.

C. Among the hydrides, $\text{NH}_3$ is a strong reducing agent while $\text{BiH}_3$ is a mild reducing agent.
