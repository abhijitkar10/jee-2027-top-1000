### C-ATS-01 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 1 · Q32 · MCQ

Which of the following configurations would have the highest magnetic moment?

(1) $[\text{Ar}]3d^7$  (2) $[\text{Ar}]3d^8$  (3) $[\text{Ar}]3d^3$  (4) $[\text{Ar}]3d^6$

**Answer: (4)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Unpaired electrons in the free-ion (Hund) configuration: $d^7\to3$, $d^8\to2$, $d^3\to3$, $d^6\to4$. $\mu=\sqrt{n(n+2)}$ is largest for $n=4$, i.e. $d^6$ ($\approx4.90$ BM).

### C-ATS-02 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 1 · Q52 · Numerical

The number of electrons present in all the completely filled subshells having $n=4$ and $s=+\tfrac12$ is ______. ($n$ = principal quantum number, $s$ = spin quantum number)

**Answer: 16** · key ✔

**Solution.** The completely filled $n=4$ subshells are $4s,4p,4d,4f$ with $1+3+5+7=16$ orbitals. Each filled orbital holds exactly one electron with $s=+\tfrac12$, so the count is $16$.
