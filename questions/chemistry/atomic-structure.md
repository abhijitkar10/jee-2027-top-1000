### C-ATS-01 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 1 · Q32 · MCQ

Which of the following configurations would have the highest magnetic moment?

(1) $[\text{Ar}]3d^7$  (2) $[\text{Ar}]3d^8$  (3) $[\text{Ar}]3d^3$  (4) $[\text{Ar}]3d^6$

**Answer: (4)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Unpaired electrons in the free-ion (Hund) configuration: $d^7\to3$, $d^8\to2$, $d^3\to3$, $d^6\to4$. $\mu=\sqrt{n(n+2)}$ is largest for $n=4$, i.e. $d^6$ ($\approx4.90$ BM).

### C-ATS-04 — JEE Main 2024 · 30 Jan Shift 1 · Q31 · MCQ

Statement I: Orbitals having the same energy are called degenerate orbitals.
Statement II: In a hydrogen atom, $3p$ and $3d$ orbitals are not degenerate orbitals.

(1) I true, II false  (2) Both true  (3) Both false  (4) I false, II true

**Answer: (1)** · key ✔

**Solution.** I is the definition of degeneracy. In a hydrogen atom (a one-electron system) energy depends only on $n$, so $3s,3p,3d$ are all degenerate; II is false. (In multi-electron atoms $l$ also matters and the degeneracy is lifted.)

### C-ATS-10 — JEE Main 2024 · 5 Apr Shift 1 · Q52 · Numerical

### C-ATS-12 — JEE Main 2024 · 6 Apr Shift 1 · Q51 · Numerical

The frequency of the de Broglie wave of the electron in Bohr's first orbit of hydrogen is ______ $\times10^{13}$ Hz (nearest integer). ($R_H=2.18\times10^{-18}$ J, $h=6.6\times10^{-34}$ J s)

**Answer: 661** · key ✔

**Solution.** $\nu=\dfrac{v}{\lambda}$ with $\lambda=\dfrac hp=\dfrac h{mv}$, so $\nu=\dfrac{mv^2}{h}$. In the first orbit $\tfrac12mv^2=R_H$, so $\nu=\dfrac{2R_H}{h}=\dfrac{2\times2.18\times10^{-18}}{6.6\times10^{-34}}=6.61\times10^{15}=661\times10^{13}$ Hz.

**Solution.** Statement I is Heisenberg's uncertainty principle. For Statement II, $\Delta x=\Delta p$ and $\Delta x\Delta p\ge\dfrac h{4\pi}$ give $\Delta p\ge\sqrt{\dfrac h{4\pi}}=\dfrac12\sqrt{\dfrac h\pi}$, so $\Delta v\ge\dfrac1{2m}\sqrt{\dfrac h\pi}$: true.

### § — JEE Main 2025 · Apr session · Atom set Q5 · MCQ

(2) An atom can take only certain distinct energies E$_1$, E$_2$, E$_3$, etc. These allowed states of constant energy are called the stationary states of the atom. ### § — JEE Main 2025 · Apr session · Atom set Q6 · MCQ

Which one of the following about an electron occupying the 1s orbital in a hydrogen atom is incorrect? (Bohr's radius is represented by $a_0$)

(1) The probability density of finding the electron is maximum at the nucleus.  (2) The electron can be found at a distance $2a_0$ from the nucleus.  (3) The 1s orbital is spherically symmetrical.  (4) The total energy of the electron is maximum when it is at a distance $a_0$ from the nucleus.

**Answer: (4)** · key (compiled)

**Solution.** The 1s probability density $|\psi|^2$ is maximum at the nucleus (1 true), the electron has a non-zero probability at $2a_0$ (2 true), and 1s is spherical (3 true). The total energy of the 1s electron is a fixed value ($-13.6$ eV) and does not depend on distance, so (4) is incorrect.
