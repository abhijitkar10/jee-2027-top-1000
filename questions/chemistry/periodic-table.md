### C-PER-01 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 1 · Q45 · MCQ

Statement I: The 4f and 5f series of elements are placed separately in the periodic table to preserve the principle of classification.
Statement II: s-block elements can be found in pure form in nature.

(1) I false, II true  (2) Both true  (3) I true, II false  (4) Both false

**Answer: (3)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Placing the 14 lanthanoids and 14 actinoids inside the main table would break its width and the similarity-based grouping, so I is true. s-block metals are highly electropositive and occur only as compounds, so II is false.

### C-PER-02 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 2 · Q54 · Numerical

The total number of ions from the following with noble gas configuration is: $\text{Sr}^{2+}(Z=38),\ \text{Cs}^+(Z=55),\ \text{La}^{2+}(Z=57),\ \text{Pb}^{2+}(Z=82),\ \text{Yb}^{2+}(Z=70),\ \text{Fe}^{2+}(Z=26)$

**Answer: 2** · key ✔

**Solution.** $\text{Sr}^{2+}$ has $36$ electrons ($[\text{Kr}]$) and $\text{Cs}^+$ has $54$ ($[\text{Xe}]$). $\text{La}^{2+}=[\text{Xe}]5d^1$, $\text{Pb}^{2+}=[\text{Xe}]4f^{14}5d^{10}6s^2$, $\text{Yb}^{2+}=[\text{Xe}]4f^{14}$ and $\text{Fe}^{2+}=[\text{Ar}]3d^6$ are not noble-gas configurations. Count $=2$.

### C-PER-03 — JEE Main 2024 · 29 Jan Shift 1 · Q32 · MCQ

Assertion A: The first ionisation enthalpy decreases across a period.
Reason R: The increasing nuclear charge outweighs the shielding across the period.

(1) Both true and R explains A  (2) A true, R false  (3) A false, R true  (4) Both true but R does not explain A

**Answer: (3)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Across a period $Z_{\text{eff}}$ increases (nuclear charge grows faster than screening), atomic size shrinks and ionisation enthalpy generally increases, so A is false and R is true.

### C-PER-04 — JEE Main 2024 · 29 Jan Shift 2 · Q32 · MCQ

The element having the highest first ionisation enthalpy is

(1) Si  (2) Al  (3) N  (4) C

**Answer: (3)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Ionisation enthalpy rises across a period and falls down a group. N ($1402$ kJ/mol) is further right than C ($1086$) and in the same period, while Al and Si are in the third period with lower values. N has the highest.

### C-PER-05 — JEE Main 2024 · 29 Jan Shift 2 · Q33 · MCQ

Statement I: Fluorine has the most negative electron gain enthalpy in its group.
Statement II: Oxygen has the least negative electron gain enthalpy in its group.

(1) Both true  (2) I true, II false  (3) Both false  (4) I false, II true

**Answer: (4)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Because of its very small size and strong inter-electronic repulsion in the compact $2p$ subshell, the first row element in a group is less negative than the second: chlorine ($-349$ kJ/mol) is more negative than fluorine ($-328$), so I is false. In group 16, O ($-141$) is the least negative (S: $-200$), so II is true.
