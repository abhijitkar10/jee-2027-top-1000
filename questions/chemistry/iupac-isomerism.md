### C-ISO-01 — JEE Main 2024 · 30 Jan Shift 2 · Q34 · MCQ

The IUPAC name of $\text{CH}_3\text{-CH(NH}_2)\text{-CH}_2\text{-CN}$ is

(1) 2-Aminopentanenitrile  (2) 2-Aminobutanenitrile  (3) 3-Aminobutanenitrile  (4) 3-Aminopropanenitrile

**Answer: (3)** · key ✔

**Solution.** The nitrile carbon is C-1: $\overset{4}{\text{C}}\text{H}_3-\overset{3}{\text{C}}\text{H(NH}_2)-\overset{2}{\text{C}}\text{H}_2-\overset{1}{\text{C}}\text{N}$. The chain has $4$ carbons with $-\text{NH}_2$ at C-3: 3-aminobutanenitrile.

### C-ISO-02 — JEE Main 2024 · 30 Jan Shift 2 · Q58 · Numerical

2-Chlorobutane $+\ \text{Cl}_2\to\text{C}_4\text{H}_8\text{Cl}_2$ (isomers). The total number of optically active isomers shown by $\text{C}_4\text{H}_8\text{Cl}_2$ obtained in the above reaction is ______.

**Answer: 6** · key ✔

**Solution.** Substitution can occur at each carbon of 2-chlorobutane: C-1 gives 1,2-dichlorobutane (chiral: $2$ enantiomers); C-2 gives 2,2-dichlorobutane (achiral); C-3 gives 2,3-dichlorobutane ($2$ optically active stereoisomers plus an inactive meso form); C-4 gives 1,3-dichlorobutane (chiral: $2$). Optically active isomers: $2+2+2=6$.
