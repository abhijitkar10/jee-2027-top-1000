### C-PRO-01 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 2 · Q36 · MCQ

The technique used for purification of a steam-volatile, water-immiscible substance is

(1) Fractional distillation  (2) Fractional distillation under reduced pressure  (3) Distillation  (4) Steam distillation

**Answer: (4)** · key ✔

**Solution.** In steam distillation the liquid boils when the sum of its vapour pressure and that of water equals atmospheric pressure, so it distils below $100^\circ$C without decomposition. Fractional distillation separates miscible liquids with close boiling points; reduced pressure is for substances that decompose near their boiling points.

### C-PRO-02 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 2 · Q47 · MCQ

A phenolic group can be identified by a positive

(1) Phthalein dye test  (2) Lucas test  (3) Tollens' test  (4) Carbylamine test

**Answer: (1)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Phenol heated with phthalic anhydride and conc. $\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$, then made alkaline, gives phenolphthalein (pink). Lucas tests alcohols, Tollens' tests aldehydes, and the carbylamine test detects primary amines.

### C-PRO-03 — JEE Main 2024 · 29 Jan Shift 1 · Q39 · MCQ

Appearance of a blood-red colour on treating the sodium fusion extract of an organic compound with $\text{FeSO}_4$ in the presence of conc. $\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$ indicates the presence of the element(s)

(1) Br  (2) N  (3) N and S  (4) S

**Answer: (3)** · key ✔

**Solution.** In Lassaigne's test, sodium fusion converts N and S to $\text{NaSCN}$ when both are present. $\text{SCN}^-$ then reacts with $\text{Fe}^{3+}$ (from air oxidation of $\text{Fe}^{2+}$) to give the blood-red $[\text{Fe(SCN)}]^{2+}$. N alone gives Prussian blue and S alone gives a violet colour with nitroprusside.
