### C-ION-01 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 1 · Q35 · MCQ

Statement I: An aqueous solution of ammonium carbonate is basic.
Statement II: The acidic/basic nature of the salt of a weak acid and a weak base depends on the $K_a$ and $K_b$ values of the acid and the base forming it.

(1) Both statements are correct  (2) I correct, II incorrect  (3) Both incorrect  (4) I incorrect, II correct

**Answer: (1)** · key ✔

**Solution.** For a weak-acid/weak-base salt, $\text{pH}=7+\tfrac12(\text{p}K_a-\text{p}K_b)$, so II is true. In $(\text{NH}_4)_2\text{CO}_3$, $K_b(\text{NH}_3)\approx1.8\times10^{-5}$ exceeds $K_a(\text{HCO}_3^-)\approx4.7\times10^{-11}$ (the acid conjugate to $\text{CO}_3^{2-}$), so carbonate hydrolyses more and the solution is basic. I is true.

### C-ION-02 — JEE Main 2024 · 30 Jan Shift 1 · Q55 · Numerical

The pH at which $\text{Mg(OH)}_2$ [$K_{sp}=1\times10^{-11}$] begins to precipitate from a solution containing $0.10$ M $\text{Mg}^{2+}$ ions is ______.

**Answer: 9** · key ✔

**Solution.** Precipitation begins when the ionic product equals $K_{sp}$: $[\text{OH}^-]^2=\dfrac{10^{-11}}{0.10}=10^{-10}\Rightarrow[\text{OH}^-]=10^{-5}$ M. $\text{pOH}=5$ so $\text{pH}=9$.

### C-ION-03 — JEE Main 2024 · 30 Jan Shift 2 · Q53 · Numerical

The pH of an aqueous solution containing $1$ M benzoic acid ($\text{p}K_a=4.20$) and $1$ M sodium benzoate is $4.5$. The volume of benzoic acid solution in $300$ mL of this buffer solution is ______ mL.

**Answer: 100** · key ✔

**Solution.** Henderson–Hasselbalch: $4.5=4.20+\log\dfrac{V_{\text{salt}}}{V_{\text{acid}}}\Rightarrow\dfrac{V_{\text{salt}}}{V_{\text{acid}}}=10^{0.3}=2$. Since both solutions are $1$ M, the mole ratio equals the volume ratio, and $V_{\text{acid}}+V_{\text{salt}}=300$ gives $V_{\text{acid}}=100$ mL.

### C-ION-04 — JEE Main 2024 · 1 Feb Shift 1 · Q38 · MCQ

Statement I: Potassium hydrogen phthalate is a primary standard for standardisation of sodium hydroxide solution.
Statement II: In this titration phenolphthalein can be used as indicator.

(1) Both true  (2) I true, II false  (3) I false, II true  (4) Both false

**Answer: (1)** · key ✔

**Solution.** KHP is pure, non-hygroscopic and stable, so it is a primary standard for NaOH. The titration is weak acid–strong base, with an equivalence point in the basic region (pH $\approx 8.5$–$9$), which suits phenolphthalein (range $8.3$–$10$).

### C-ION-05 — JEE Main 2024 · 1 Feb Shift 1 · Q54 · Numerical

$K_a$ for $\text{CH}_3\text{COOH}$ is $1.8\times10^{-5}$ and $K_b$ for $\text{NH}_4\text{OH}$ is $1.8\times10^{-5}$. The pH of ammonium acetate solution will be ______.

**Answer: 7** · key ✔

**Solution.** For a salt of a weak acid and weak base, $\text{pH}=7+\tfrac12(\text{p}K_a-\text{p}K_b)$. With $K_a=K_b$ the correction vanishes: $\text{pH}=7$.

### C-ION-06 — JEE Main 2024 · 1 Feb Shift 2 · Q35 · MCQ

The solubility of calcium phosphate (molar mass $M$) in water is $W$ g per $100$ mL at $25^\circ$C. Its solubility product is approximately:

(1) $10^7\dfrac{W^3}{M^3}$  (2) $10^7\dfrac{W^5}{M^5}$  (3) $10^3\dfrac{W^5}{M^5}$  (4) $10^5\dfrac{W^5}{M^5}$

**Answer: (2)** · key ✔

**Solution.** $s=\dfrac{W}{M}\times10\ \text{mol L}^{-1}$. For $\text{Ca}_3(\text{PO}_4)_2$: $K_{sp}=(3s)^3(2s)^2=108s^5=108\times10^5\dfrac{W^5}{M^5}\approx10^7\dfrac{W^5}{M^5}$.

### C-ION-07 — JEE Main 2024 · 4 Apr Shift 1 · Q42 · MCQ

The decreasing order of basic strength of the conjugate bases $\text{OH}^-$, $\text{RO}^-$, $\text{CH}_3\text{COO}^-$, $\text{Cl}^-$ is:

(1) $\text{RO}^->\text{OH}^->\text{CH}_3\text{COO}^->\text{Cl}^-$  (2) $\text{Cl}^->\text{RO}^->\text{OH}^->\text{CH}_3\text{COO}^-$  (3) $\text{OH}^->\text{RO}^->\text{CH}_3\text{COO}^->\text{Cl}^-$  (4) $\text{Cl}^->\text{OH}^->\text{RO}^->\text{CH}_3\text{COO}^-$

**Answer: (1)** · key ✔

**Solution.** A stronger acid has a weaker conjugate base. Acid strength: $\text{HCl}>\text{CH}_3\text{COOH}>\text{H}_2\text{O}>\text{ROH}$, so base strength is $\text{RO}^->\text{OH}^->\text{CH}_3\text{COO}^->\text{Cl}^-$ (the alkyl group's $+I$ effect makes $\text{RO}^-$ the strongest).

### C-ION-08 — JEE Main 2024 · 8 Apr Shift 2 · Q35 · MCQ

Statement I: A buffer solution is a mixture of a salt and an acid or a base mixed in any particular quantities.
Statement II: Blood is a naturally occurring buffer solution whose pH is maintained by $\text{H}_2\text{CO}_3/\text{HCO}_3^-$ concentrations.

(1) I true, II false  (2) Both true  (3) I false, II true  (4) Both false

**Answer: (3)** · key ✔

**Solution.** A buffer needs a weak acid (or base) and its conjugate in comparable amounts (typically within a $1:10$ to $10:1$ ratio), not "any quantities": I is false. Blood pH ($\approx7.4$) is held by the carbonic acid/bicarbonate system: II is true.

### C-ION-09 — JEE Advanced 2025 · Paper 1 · Q9 · Numerical

At $25^\circ$C, the concentration of $\text{H}^+$ in a $1.00\times10^{-3}$ M aqueous solution of a weak monobasic acid with $K_a=4.00\times10^{-11}$ is $X\times10^{-7}$ M. $X=$ ______. ($K_w=1.00\times10^{-14}$)

**Answer: ≈ 2.24** (accepted range $2.2$–$2.3$) · official key ✔ (re-derived)

**Solution.** The acid is so weak that water's own ionisation cannot be ignored. Charge balance: $[\text{H}^+]=[\text{A}^-]+[\text{OH}^-]$, with $[\text{A}^-]=\dfrac{K_aC}{[\text{H}^+]}$ and $[\text{OH}^-]=\dfrac{K_w}{[\text{H}^+]}$. So $[\text{H}^+]^2=K_aC+K_w=4\times10^{-14}+1\times10^{-14}=5\times10^{-14}$, giving $[\text{H}^+]=2.24\times10^{-7}$ M.

### C-ION-10 — JEE Main 2023 · 24 Jan Shift 1 · Q53 · Numerical

The dissociation constant of acetic acid is $x\times10^{-5}$. When $25$ mL of $0.2$ M $\text{CH}_3\text{COONa}$ solution is mixed with $25$ mL of $0.02$ M $\text{CH}_3\text{COOH}$ solution, the pH of the resulting solution is $5$. $x=$ ______.

**Answer: 10** · key ✔

**Solution.** After mixing (volume doubled): $[\text{salt}]=0.1$ M and $[\text{acid}]=0.01$ M. Henderson: $\text{pH}=\text{p}K_a+\log\dfrac{0.1}{0.01}=\text{p}K_a+1=5\Rightarrow\text{p}K_a=4$, so $K_a=10^{-4}=10\times10^{-5}$, $x=10$.
