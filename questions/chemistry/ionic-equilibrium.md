### C-ION-01 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 1 · Q35 · MCQ

Statement I: An aqueous solution of ammonium carbonate is basic.
Statement II: The acidic/basic nature of the salt of a weak acid and a weak base depends on the $K_a$ and $K_b$ values of the acid and the base forming it.

(1) Both statements are correct  (2) I correct, II incorrect  (3) Both incorrect  (4) I incorrect, II correct

**Answer: (1)** · key ✔

**Solution.** For a weak-acid/weak-base salt, $\text{pH}=7+\tfrac12(\text{p}K_a-\text{p}K_b)$, so II is true. In $(\text{NH}_4)_2\text{CO}_3$, $K_b(\text{NH}_3)\approx1.8\times10^{-5}$ exceeds $K_a(\text{HCO}_3^-)\approx4.7\times10^{-11}$ (the acid conjugate to $\text{CO}_3^{2-}$), so carbonate hydrolyses more and the solution is basic. I is true.

### C-ION-02 — JEE Main 2024 · 30 Jan Shift 1 · Q55 · Numerical

The pH at which $\text{Mg(OH)}_2$ [$K_{sp}=1\times10^{-11}$] begins to precipitate from a solution containing $0.10$ M $\text{Mg}^{2+}$ ions is ______.

**Answer: 9** · key ✔

**Solution.** Precipitation begins when the ionic product equals $K_{sp}$: $[\text{OH}^-]^2=\dfrac{10^{-11}}{0.10}=10^{-10}\Rightarrow[\text{OH}^-]=10^{-5}$ M. $\text{pOH}=5$ so $\text{pH}=9$.

### C-ION-03 — JEE Main 2024 · 30 Jan Shift 2 · Q53 · Numerical

The pH of an aqueous solution containing $1$ M benzoic acid ($\text{p}K_a=4.20$) and $1$ M sodium benzoate is $4.5$. The volume of benzoic acid solution in $300$ mL of this buffer solution is ______ mL.

**Answer: 100** · key ✔

**Solution.** Henderson–Hasselbalch: $4.5=4.20+\log\dfrac{V_{\text{salt}}}{V_{\text{acid}}}\Rightarrow\dfrac{V_{\text{salt}}}{V_{\text{acid}}}=10^{0.3}=2$. Since both solutions are $1$ M, the mole ratio equals the volume ratio, and $V_{\text{acid}}+V_{\text{salt}}=300$ gives $V_{\text{acid}}=100$ mL.

### C-ION-04 — JEE Main 2024 · 1 Feb Shift 1 · Q38 · MCQ

Statement I: Potassium hydrogen phthalate is a primary standard for standardisation of sodium hydroxide solution.
Statement II: In this titration phenolphthalein can be used as indicator.

(1) Both true  (2) I true, II false  (3) I false, II true  (4) Both false

**Answer: (1)** · key ✔

**Solution.** KHP is pure, non-hygroscopic and stable, so it is a primary standard for NaOH. The titration is weak acid–strong base, with an equivalence point in the basic region (pH $\approx 8.5$–$9$), which suits phenolphthalein (range $8.3$–$10$).

### C-ION-05 — JEE Main 2024 · 1 Feb Shift 1 · Q54 · Numerical

$K_a$ for $\text{CH}_3\text{COOH}$ is $1.8\times10^{-5}$ and $K_b$ for $\text{NH}_4\text{OH}$ is $1.8\times10^{-5}$. The pH of ammonium acetate solution will be ______.

**Answer: 7** · key ✔

**Solution.** For a salt of a weak acid and weak base, $\text{pH}=7+\tfrac12(\text{p}K_a-\text{p}K_b)$. With $K_a=K_b$ the correction vanishes: $\text{pH}=7$.
