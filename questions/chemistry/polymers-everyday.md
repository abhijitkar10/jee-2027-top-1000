### C-POL-01 — JEE Main 2024 · 1 Feb Shift 2 · Q36 · MCQ

Match List I (compound) with List II (use): (A) carbon tetrachloride, (B) methylene chloride, (C) DDT, (D) Freons; (I) paint remover, (II) refrigerators and air conditioners, (III) fire extinguisher, (IV) non-biodegradable insecticide.

(1) A-I, B-II, C-III, D-IV  (2) A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II  (3) A-IV, B-III, C-II, D-I  (4) A-II, B-III, C-I, D-IV

**Answer: (2)** · key ✔

**Solution.** $\text{CCl}_4$ – fire extinguisher (III); $\text{CH}_2\text{Cl}_2$ – paint remover (I); DDT – non-biodegradable insecticide (IV); Freons ($\text{CF}_2\text{Cl}_2$) – refrigerants (II).

### C-POL-02 — JEE Main 2024 · 9 Apr Shift 1 · Q45 · MCQ

Fluoride ions make the enamel on teeth much harder by converting hydroxyapatite into the much harder fluoroapatite of formula:

(1) $[3(\text{Ca}_3(\text{PO}_4)_2)\cdot\text{Ca(OH)}_2]$  (2) $[3(\text{Ca}_3(\text{PO}_4)_3)\cdot\text{CaF}_2]$  (3) $[3(\text{Ca}_2(\text{PO}_4)_2)\cdot\text{Ca(OH)}_2]$  (4) $[3(\text{Ca}_3(\text{PO}_4)_2)\cdot\text{CaF}_2]$

**Answer: (4)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Hydroxyapatite is $3\text{Ca}_3(\text{PO}_4)_2\cdot\text{Ca(OH)}_2$ [$\text{Ca}_5(\text{PO}_4)_3\text{OH}$]. $\text{F}^-$ replaces $\text{OH}^-$, giving fluoroapatite $3\text{Ca}_3(\text{PO}_4)_2\cdot\text{CaF}_2$, which is harder and less soluble in acid.

### C-POL-03 — JEE Main 2023 · 24 Jan Shift 1 · Q37 · MCQ

Which of the following is true about freons?

(1) These are chlorofluorocarbon compounds  (2) These are chemicals causing skin cancer  (3) These are radicals of chlorine and chlorine monoxide  (4) All radicals are called freons

**Answer: (1)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Freons are chlorofluorocarbons (e.g. $\text{CF}_2\text{Cl}_2$), used as refrigerants. Their photolysis in the stratosphere releases Cl atoms, which deplete ozone (increasing UV and skin-cancer risk), but the freons themselves are neither the radicals nor the cancer-causing agent.
