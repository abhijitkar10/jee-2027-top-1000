### C-DFB-01 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 1 · Q31 · MCQ

The electronic configuration of neodymium is (atomic number $60$):

(1) $[\text{Xe}]4f^4\,6s^2$  (2) $[\text{Xe}]5f^4\,7s^2$  (3) $[\text{Xe}]4f^6\,6s^2$  (4) $[\text{Xe}]4f^1\,5d^1\,6s^2$

**Answer: (1)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Xenon has $Z=54$, so Nd ($Z=60$) has $6$ electrons beyond the [Xe] core. Lanthanoids fill $4f$ before $5d$ (except La, Ce, Gd, Lu), giving $[\text{Xe}]4f^46s^2$. Option (2) is an actinoid-type pattern, (3) is Sm ($Z=62$), and (4) is Ce ($Z=58$).

### C-DFB-02 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 2 · Q41 · MCQ

Choose the option in which all the elements have a $d^{10}$ electronic configuration:

(1) ${}_{27}$Co, ${}_{28}$Ni, ${}_{26}$Fe, ${}_{24}$Cr  (2) ${}_{29}$Cu, ${}_{30}$Zn, ${}_{48}$Cd, ${}_{47}$Ag  (3) ${}_{46}$Pd, ${}_{28}$Ni, ${}_{26}$Fe, ${}_{24}$Cr  (4) ${}_{28}$Ni, ${}_{24}$Cr, ${}_{26}$Fe, ${}_{29}$Cu

**Answer: (2)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Cu $3d^{10}4s^1$, Zn $3d^{10}4s^2$, Cd $4d^{10}5s^2$ and Ag $4d^{10}5s^1$ all have a filled $d$ subshell. Pd is $4d^{10}$ (no $s$ electron) but Ni, Fe, Cr, Co do not have $d^{10}$, which rules out the other options.

### C-DFB-03 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 2 · Q42 · MCQ

Statement I: Among the lanthanoids, the formation of $\text{Ce}^{4+}$ is favoured by its noble gas configuration.
Statement II: $\text{Ce}^{4+}$ is a strong oxidant, reverting to the common $+3$ state.

(1) I false, II true  (2) Both true  (3) I true, II false  (4) Both false

**Answer: (2)** · key ✔

**Solution.** $\text{Ce}^{4+}$ is $[\text{Xe}]$, a noble-gas core, which explains why $+4$ is accessible. But $E^\circ(\text{Ce}^{4+}/\text{Ce}^{3+})=+1.74$ V, so it is a strong oxidising agent that reverts to $+3$, the common lanthanoid oxidation state.

### C-DFB-04 — JEE Main 2024 · 29 Jan Shift 1 · Q35 · MCQ

$\text{KMnO}_4$ decomposes on heating at $513$ K to form $\text{O}_2$ along with

(1) $\text{MnO}_2$ and $\text{K}_2\text{O}_2$  (2) $\text{K}_2\text{MnO}_4$ and Mn  (3) Mn and $\text{KO}_2$  (4) $\text{K}_2\text{MnO}_4$ and $\text{MnO}_2$

**Answer: (4)** · key ✔

**Solution.** $2\text{KMnO}_4\xrightarrow{513\,\text{K}}\text{K}_2\text{MnO}_4+\text{MnO}_2+\text{O}_2$ (used in the laboratory preparation of dioxygen).

### C-DFB-05 — JEE Main 2024 · 29 Jan Shift 2 · Q40 · MCQ

Which of the following acts as a strong reducing agent? (Atomic numbers: Ce = 58, Eu = 63, Gd = 64, Lu = 71)

(1) $\text{Lu}^{3+}$  (2) $\text{Gd}^{3+}$  (3) $\text{Eu}^{2+}$  (4) $\text{Ce}^{4+}$

**Answer: (3)** · key ✔

**Solution.** $\text{Eu}^{2+}$ ($4f^7$, half-filled) readily loses an electron to reach the stable $+3$ state ($E^\circ=-0.35$ V), so it is a strong reductant. $\text{Lu}^{3+}$ ($4f^{14}$) and $\text{Gd}^{3+}$ ($4f^7$) are already stable, and $\text{Ce}^{4+}$ is an oxidant.

### C-DFB-06 — JEE Main 2024 · 29 Jan Shift 2 · Q41 · MCQ

Which of the following statements are correct about Zn, Cd and Hg?
A. They exhibit high enthalpy of atomisation as the $d$-subshell is full.
B. Zn and Cd do not show variable oxidation state while Hg shows $+\text{I}$ and $+\text{II}$.
C. Compounds of Zn, Cd and Hg are paramagnetic in nature.
D. Zn, Cd and Hg are called soft metals.

(1) B, D only  (2) B, C only  (3) A, D only  (4) C, D only

**Answer: (1)** · key ✔

**Solution.** A is false: the filled $d^{10}$ subshell means weak metallic bonding and low enthalpies of atomisation (low melting points). B is true ($\text{Hg}_2^{2+}$ and $\text{Hg}^{2+}$). C is false: $d^{10}$ compounds are diamagnetic. D is true (soft, low melting).
