### C-INM-01 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 1 · Q44 · MCQ

$\text{NaCl}$ reacts with conc. $\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$ and $\text{K}_2\text{Cr}_2\text{O}_7$ to give reddish fumes (B), which react with $\text{NaOH}$ to give a yellow solution (C). (B) and (C) respectively are

(1) $\text{CrO}_2\text{Cl}_2,\ \text{Na}_2\text{CrO}_4$  (2) $\text{Na}_2\text{CrO}_4,\ \text{CrO}_2\text{Cl}_2$  (3) $\text{CrO}_2\text{Cl}_2,\ \text{KHSO}_4$  (4) $\text{CrO}_2\text{Cl}_2,\ \text{Na}_2\text{Cr}_2\text{O}_7$

**Answer: (1)** · key ✔

**Solution.** This is the chromyl chloride test for chloride. Red vapours of $\text{CrO}_2\text{Cl}_2$ form; on passing into $\text{NaOH}$: $\text{CrO}_2\text{Cl}_2+4\text{NaOH}\to\text{Na}_2\text{CrO}_4+2\text{NaCl}+2\text{H}_2\text{O}$, giving yellow sodium chromate.

### C-INM-02 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 2 · Q43 · MCQ

Identify the incorrect pair:

(1) Photography – $\text{AgBr}$  (2) Polythene preparation – $\text{TiCl}_4,\ \text{Al(CH}_3)_3$  (3) Haber process – Iron  (4) Wacker process – $\text{PtCl}_2$

**Answer: (4)** · key ✔

**Solution.** The Wacker process (ethene to ethanal) uses $\text{PdCl}_2$ (with $\text{CuCl}_2$), not $\text{PtCl}_2$. $\text{AgBr}$ is the light-sensitive salt in photography, Ziegler–Natta catalyst ($\text{TiCl}_4$ + trialkylaluminium) makes polythene, and iron catalyses the Haber process.

### C-INM-03 — JEE Main 2024 · 29 Jan Shift 1 · Q45 · MCQ

In the chromyl chloride test for the $\text{Cl}^-$ ion, a yellow solution is obtained. Acidification of the solution and addition of amyl alcohol and $10\%\ \text{H}_2\text{O}_2$ turns the organic layer blue, indicating formation of chromium pentoxide. The oxidation state of chromium in that species is

(1) $+6$  (2) $+5$  (3) $+10$  (4) $+3$

**Answer: (1)** · key ✔

**Solution.** The yellow solution contains $\text{CrO}_4^{2-}$. With acid and $\text{H}_2\text{O}_2$ it forms the blue peroxo compound $\text{CrO}(\text{O}_2)_2$, i.e. "$\text{CrO}_5$", which has one oxo ($-2$) and two peroxo ($-1$ each, $-4$ total) groups, so $x-2-4=0\Rightarrow x=+6$. No change in Cr oxidation state occurs.

### C-INM-04 — JEE Main 2024 · 29 Jan Shift 2 · Q48 · MCQ

On passing a gas $X$ through Nessler's reagent, a brown precipitate is obtained. The gas $X$ is

(1) $\text{H}_2\text{S}$  (2) $\text{CO}_2$  (3) $\text{NH}_3$  (4) $\text{Cl}_2$

**Answer: (3)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Nessler's reagent ($\text{K}_2[\text{HgI}_4]$ in alkali) gives a brown precipitate of the oxo-dimercuric ammonium iodide ($\text{H}_2\text{N-Hg-O-Hg-I}$ type) with $\text{NH}_3$ or $\text{NH}_4^+$.

### C-INM-05 — JEE Main 2024 · 29 Jan Shift 2 · Q49 · MCQ

A reagent which gives a brilliant red precipitate with nickel ions in basic medium is

(1) sodium nitroprusside  (2) neutral $\text{FeCl}_3$  (3) meta-dinitrobenzene  (4) dimethylglyoxime

**Answer: (4)** · key ✔

**Solution.** $\text{Ni}^{2+}$ with dimethylglyoxime (DMG) in ammoniacal (basic) solution forms the bright red, chelated bis(dimethylglyoximato)nickel(II) precipitate.

### C-INM-06 — JEE Main 2024 · 30 Jan Shift 2 · Q43 · MCQ

$A$ and $B$ formed in the following reactions are: $\text{CrO}_2\text{Cl}_2+4\text{NaOH}\to A+2\text{NaCl}+2\text{H}_2\text{O}$; $A+2\text{HCl}+2\text{H}_2\text{O}_2\to B+3\text{H}_2\text{O}$.

(1) $A=\text{Na}_2\text{CrO}_4,\ B=\text{CrO}_5$  (2) $A=\text{Na}_2\text{Cr}_2\text{O}_4,\ B=\text{CrO}_4$  (3) $A=\text{Na}_2\text{Cr}_2\text{O}_7,\ B=\text{CrO}_3$  (4) $A=\text{Na}_2\text{Cr}_2\text{O}_7,\ B=\text{CrO}_5$

**Answer: (1)** · key ✔

**Solution.** $\text{CrO}_2\text{Cl}_2+4\text{NaOH}\to\text{Na}_2\text{CrO}_4+2\text{NaCl}+2\text{H}_2\text{O}$ (yellow chromate). With acid and $\text{H}_2\text{O}_2$: $\text{Na}_2\text{CrO}_4+2\text{HCl}+2\text{H}_2\text{O}_2\to\text{CrO}_5+2\text{NaCl}+3\text{H}_2\text{O}$ (oxygen balance: $8=5+3$), the blue peroxide.

### C-INM-07 — JEE Main 2024 · 30 Jan Shift 2 · Q59 · Numerical

The number of metal ions characterised by a flame test among the following is ______: $\text{Sr}^{2+},\ \text{Ba}^{2+},\ \text{Ca}^{2+},\ \text{Cu}^{2+},\ \text{Zn}^{2+},\ \text{Co}^{2+},\ \text{Fe}^{2+}$

**Answer: 4** · key ✔

**Solution.** Characteristic flame colours: $\text{Sr}^{2+}$ crimson red, $\text{Ba}^{2+}$ apple green, $\text{Ca}^{2+}$ brick red, $\text{Cu}^{2+}$ blue-green. $\text{Zn}^{2+}$, $\text{Co}^{2+}$ and $\text{Fe}^{2+}$ give no characteristic flame colour. Count $=4$.

### C-INM-08 — JEE Main 2024 · 31 Jan Shift 1 · Q49 · MCQ

The compound that is white in colour is

(1) ammonium sulphide  (2) lead sulphate  (3) lead iodide  (4) ammonium arsenomolybdate

**Answer: (2)** · key ✔

**Solution.** $\text{PbSO}_4$ is a white precipitate. $\text{PbI}_2$ is yellow, ammonium arsenomolybdate is canary yellow, and ammonium sulphide solution is yellow.

### C-INM-09 — JEE Main 2024 · 31 Jan Shift 1 · Q60 · Numerical

The molar mass of the salt from $\text{NaBr},\ \text{NaNO}_3,\ \text{KI}$ and $\text{CaF}_2$ which does not evolve coloured vapours on heating with concentrated $\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$ is ______ g mol$^{-1}$. (Na = 23, N = 14, K = 39, O = 16, Br = 80, I = 127, F = 19, Ca = 40)

**Answer: 78** · key ✔

**Solution.** $\text{NaBr}$ gives brown $\text{Br}_2$, $\text{KI}$ gives violet $\text{I}_2$ vapour, and $\text{NaNO}_3$ (on heating with conc. $\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$) gives brown $\text{NO}_2$. $\text{CaF}_2$ only gives colourless HF: $\text{CaF}_2+\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4\to\text{CaSO}_4+2\text{HF}$. Molar mass $=40+2(19)=78$ g mol$^{-1}$.
