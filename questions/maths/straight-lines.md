### M-STL-01 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 1 · Q65 · MCQ

The portion of the line $4x+5y=20$ in the first quadrant is trisected by the lines $L_1$ and $L_2$ passing through the origin. The tangent of an angle between $L_1$ and $L_2$ is

(1) $\tfrac85$  (2) $\tfrac{25}{41}$  (3) $\tfrac25$  (4) $\tfrac{30}{41}$

**Answer: (4)** · key ✔

**Solution.** The segment runs from $(5,0)$ to $(0,4)$, with trisection points $\left(\tfrac{10}3,\tfrac43\right)$ and $\left(\tfrac53,\tfrac83\right)$. Slopes: $m_1=\tfrac25$, $m_2=\tfrac85$. $\tan\theta=\left|\dfrac{m_2-m_1}{1+m_1m_2}\right|=\dfrac{6/5}{1+16/25}=\dfrac{6/5}{41/25}=\dfrac{30}{41}$.

### M-STL-02 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 2 · Q66 · MCQ

Let $R$ be the interior region between the lines $3x-y+1=0$ and $x+2y-5=0$ containing the origin. The set of all values of $a$ for which the points $(a^2,a+1)$ lie in $R$ is

(1) $(-3,-1)\cup\left(-\tfrac13,1\right)$  (2) $(-3,0)\cup\left(\tfrac13,1\right)$  (3) $(-3,0)\cup\left(\tfrac23,1\right)$  (4) $(-3,-1)\cup\left(\tfrac13,1\right)$

**Answer: (2)** · key ✔

**Solution.** At the origin $3x-y+1=1>0$ and $x+2y-5=-5<0$, so $R:\ 3x-y+1>0,\ x+2y-5<0$. For $(a^2,a+1)$: $3a^2-(a+1)+1=3a^2-a>0\Rightarrow a<0$ or $a>\tfrac13$. And $a^2+2(a+1)-5=a^2+2a-3<0\Rightarrow-3<a<1$. Intersection: $(-3,0)\cup\left(\tfrac13,1\right)$.

### M-STL-03 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 2 · Q83 · Numerical

If the sum of the squares of all real values of $\alpha$ for which the lines $2x-y+3=0$, $6x+3y+1=0$ and $\alpha x+2y-2=0$ do not form a triangle is $p$, then the greatest integer $\le p$ is ______.

**Answer: 32** · key ✔

**Solution.** Three lines fail to form a triangle if two are parallel or all three are concurrent. Slopes of the first two are $2$ and $-2$. Third parallel to first: $-\tfrac\alpha2=2\Rightarrow\alpha=-4$. Parallel to second: $\alpha=4$. Concurrency: the first two meet at $\left(-\tfrac56,\tfrac43\right)$; substituting in the third: $-\tfrac{5\alpha}6+\tfrac83-2=0\Rightarrow\alpha=\tfrac45$. $p=16+16+\tfrac{16}{25}=32.64$, so $\lfloor p\rfloor=32$.
