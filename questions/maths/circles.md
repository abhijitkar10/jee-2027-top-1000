### M-CIR-01 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 1 · Q66 · MCQ

Four distinct points $(2k,3k),(1,0),(0,1)$ and $(0,0)$ lie on a circle for $k$ equal to

(1) $\tfrac2{13}$  (2) $\tfrac3{13}$  (3) $\tfrac5{13}$  (4) $\tfrac1{13}$

**Answer: (3)** · key ✔

**Solution.** The circle through $(0,0),(1,0),(0,1)$ is $x^2+y^2-x-y=0$. Substituting $(2k,3k)$: $13k^2-5k=0\Rightarrow k=\tfrac5{13}$ (since $k\ne0$ for distinct points).
