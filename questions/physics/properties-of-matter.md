### P-POM-01 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 1 · Q23 · Numerical

The average depth of an ocean is $4000$ m and the bulk modulus of water is $2\times10^{9}\ \text{N m}^{-2}$. The fractional compression of water at the bottom of the ocean is $\alpha\times10^{-2}$. Find $\alpha$. (Given $g=10\ \text{m s}^{-2}$, $\rho=1000\ \text{kg m}^{-3}$)

**Answer: 2** · key ✔

**Solution.** $\dfrac{\Delta V}{V}=\dfrac{\Delta P}{B}=\dfrac{\rho gh}{B}=\dfrac{1000\times10\times4000}{2\times10^9}=2\times10^{-2}$.
