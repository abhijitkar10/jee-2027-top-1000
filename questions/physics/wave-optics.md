### P-WOP-01 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 2 · Q16 · MCQ

When a polaroid sheet is rotated between two crossed polaroids, the transmitted light intensity will be maximum for a rotation of

(1) $60^\circ$  (2) $30^\circ$  (3) $90^\circ$  (4) $45^\circ$

**Answer: (4)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Let the middle sheet make angle $\theta$ with the first; then it makes $90^\circ-\theta$ with the third. By Malus's law $I=I_0\cos^2\theta\cos^2(90^\circ-\theta)=\dfrac{I_0}4\sin^22\theta$, which is maximum at $\theta=45^\circ$.

### P-WOP-02 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 2 · Q29 · Numerical

A parallel beam of monochromatic light of wavelength $5000$ Å is incident normally on a single narrow slit of width $0.001$ mm. The light is focused by a convex lens on a screen placed in its focal plane. The first minimum forms at a diffraction angle of ______ degrees.

**Answer: 30** · key ✔

**Solution.** First minimum: $a\sin\theta=\lambda\Rightarrow\sin\theta=\dfrac{5\times10^{-7}}{10^{-6}}=\dfrac12\Rightarrow\theta=30^\circ$.

### P-WOP-03 — JEE Main 2024 · 29 Jan Shift 2 · Q17 · MCQ

In Young's double slit experiment, light from two identical sources superimposes on a screen. The path difference between the two lights reaching a point on the screen is $\dfrac{7\lambda}4$. The ratio of the intensity of the fringe at this point to the maximum intensity of the fringe is

(1) $\tfrac12$  (2) $\tfrac34$  (3) $\tfrac13$  (4) $\tfrac14$

**Answer: (1)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Phase difference $\phi=\dfrac{2\pi}\lambda\cdot\dfrac{7\lambda}4=\dfrac{7\pi}2$. $I=I_{\max}\cos^2\dfrac\phi2=I_{\max}\cos^2\dfrac{7\pi}4=I_{\max}\cdot\tfrac12$.

### P-WOP-04 — JEE Main 2024 · 29 Jan Shift 2 · Q29 · Numerical

In a single-slit diffraction pattern, light of wavelength $6000$ Å is used. The distance between the first and third minima is $3$ mm when the screen is $50$ cm away from the slit. The width of the slit is ______ $\times10^{-4}$ m.

**Answer: 2** · key ✔

**Solution.** The $n$th minimum is at $y_n=\dfrac{n\lambda D}a$, so $y_3-y_1=\dfrac{2\lambda D}a=3\times10^{-3}$. $a=\dfrac{2\times6\times10^{-7}\times0.5}{3\times10^{-3}}=2\times10^{-4}$ m.

### P-WOP-05 — JEE Main 2024 · 30 Jan Shift 1 · Q17 · MCQ

The diffraction pattern of light of wavelength $400$ nm diffracting from a slit of width $0.2$ mm is focused on the focal plane of a convex lens of focal length $100$ cm. The width of the first secondary maximum will be

(1) 2 mm  (2) 2 cm  (3) 0.02 mm  (4) 0.2 mm

**Answer: (1)** · key ✔

**Solution.** The width of each secondary maximum equals the distance between successive minima: $\beta=\dfrac{\lambda f}{a}=\dfrac{400\times10^{-9}\times1}{0.2\times10^{-3}}=2\times10^{-3}$ m $=2$ mm.

### P-WOP-06 — JEE Main 2024 · 30 Jan Shift 2 · Q14 · MCQ

A beam of unpolarised light of intensity $I_0$ is passed through a polaroid $A$ and then through another polaroid $B$ oriented so that its principal plane makes an angle of $45^\circ$ relative to that of $A$. The intensity of the emergent light is

(1) $\dfrac{I_0}4$  (2) $I_0$  (3) $\dfrac{I_0}2$  (4) $\dfrac{I_0}8$

**Answer: (1)** · key ✔

**Solution.** After $A$: $\dfrac{I_0}2$. By Malus's law, after $B$: $\dfrac{I_0}2\cos^245^\circ=\dfrac{I_0}2\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac{I_0}4$.

### P-WOP-07 — JEE Main 2024 · 31 Jan Shift 2 · Q16 · MCQ

When unpolarised light is incident at an angle of $60^\circ$ on a transparent medium from air, the reflected ray is completely polarised. The angle of refraction in the medium is

(1) $30^\circ$  (2) $60^\circ$  (3) $90^\circ$  (4) $45^\circ$

**Answer: (1)** · key ✔

**Solution.** At the polarising (Brewster) angle $i_p$ the reflected and refracted rays are perpendicular: $i_p+r=90^\circ\Rightarrow r=90^\circ-60^\circ=30^\circ$ (and $\mu=\tan60^\circ=\sqrt3$).

### P-WOP-08 — JEE Main 2024 · 1 Feb Shift 1 · Q16 · MCQ

A monochromatic light of wavelength $6000$ Å is incident on a single slit of width $0.01$ mm. If the diffraction pattern is formed at the focus of a convex lens of focal length $20$ cm, the linear width of the central maximum is

(1) 60 mm  (2) 24 mm  (3) 120 mm  (4) 12 mm

**Answer: (2)** · key ✔

**Solution.** The central maximum extends between the first minima on both sides: width $=\dfrac{2\lambda f}a=\dfrac{2\times6\times10^{-7}\times0.2}{10^{-5}}=2.4\times10^{-2}$ m $=24$ mm.

### P-WOP-09 — JEE Main 2024 · 1 Feb Shift 2 · Q17 · MCQ

A microwave of wavelength $2.0$ cm falls normally on a slit of width $4.0$ cm. The angular spread of the central maximum of the diffraction pattern on a screen $1.5$ m away is:

(1) $30^\circ$  (2) $15^\circ$  (3) $60^\circ$  (4) $45^\circ$

**Solution.** First minimum: $a\sin\theta=\lambda\Rightarrow\sin\theta=\tfrac{2}{4}=\tfrac12$, $\theta=30^\circ$. The angular spread is on both sides: $2\theta=60^\circ$.

### P-WOP-10 — JEE Main 2024 · 1 Feb Shift 2 · Q29 · Numerical

In Young's double slit experiment, light of wavelength $5000\ \text{Å}$ is used, slit separation $1.0$ mm and screen distance $1.0$ m. The distance from the centre where intensity first becomes half of the maximum is ______ $\times10^{-6}$ m.

**Answer: 125** · key ✔

**Solution.** $I=I_0\cos^2(\phi/2)=I_0/2\Rightarrow\phi=\pi/2$, i.e. path difference $\lambda/4$. $y=\dfrac{D}{d}\cdot\dfrac\lambda4=\dfrac{1\times5\times10^{-7}}{10^{-3}\times4}=1.25\times10^{-4}$ m $=125\times10^{-6}$ m.

### P-WOP-11 — JEE Main 2024 · 4 Apr Shift 1 · Q29 · Numerical

Wavelengths $\lambda_1=450$ nm and $\lambda_2=650$ nm are used in Young's double-slit experiment. The minimum order of the fringe of $\lambda_2$ that overlaps with a fringe of $\lambda_1$ is $n$. The value of $n$ is ______.

**Answer: 9** · key ✔

**Solution.** Bright fringes coincide when $n\lambda_2=m\lambda_1$: $650n=450m\Rightarrow13n=9m$. The smallest integers are $n=9$, $m=13$.

### P-WOP-12 — JEE Main 2024 · 4 Apr Shift 2 · Q16 · MCQ

The width of one of the two slits in Young's double-slit experiment is $4$ times that of the other. The ratio of the maximum to the minimum intensity in the interference pattern is:

(1) 1 : 1  (2) 4 : 1  (3) 9 : 1  (4) 16 : 1

**Answer: (3)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Intensity $\propto$ width: $I_1:I_2=4:1$, amplitudes $2:1$. $\dfrac{I_{max}}{I_{min}}=\dfrac{(2+1)^2}{(2-1)^2}=9:1$.

### P-WOP-14 — JEE Main 2024 · 5 Apr Shift 2 · Q29 · Numerical

### P-WOP-15 — JEE Main 2024 · 6 Apr Shift 2 · Q29 · Numerical

Two coherent monochromatic light beams of intensities $I$ and $4I$ are superposed. The difference between the maximum and minimum possible intensities in the resulting beam is $xI$. The value of $x$ is ______.

**Answer: 8** · key ✔

**Solution.** $I_{max}=(\sqrt I+2\sqrt I)^2=9I$, $I_{min}=(2\sqrt I-\sqrt I)^2=I$. Difference $=8I$, so $x=8$.

### P-WOP-16 — JEE Main 2024 · 8 Apr Shift 1 · Q29 · Numerical

A parallel beam of monochromatic light of wavelength $600$ nm passes through a single slit of width $0.4$ mm. The angular divergence corresponding to the second-order minima is ______ $\times10^{-3}$ rad.

**Answer: 6** · key ✔

**Solution.** Second minimum: $\sin\theta=\dfrac{2\lambda}{a}=\dfrac{2\times600\times10^{-9}}{0.4\times10^{-3}}=3\times10^{-3}$ rad on each side of the axis. The full divergence (both sides) is $2\theta=6\times10^{-3}$ rad.

### P-WOP-17 — JEE Main 2024 · 8 Apr Shift 2 · Q29 · Numerical

**Solution.** Central maximum width $=\dfrac{2\lambda D}{a}$. Fringe width $\beta=\dfrac{\lambda D}{d}$. Setting $\dfrac{2\lambda D}{a}=10\beta=\dfrac{10\lambda D}{d}\Rightarrow a=\dfrac d5=0.2$ mm $=2\times10^{-4}$ m.

### P-WOP-18 — JEE Main 2024 · 9 Apr Shift 1 · Q29 · Numerical

In Young's double-slit experiment the intensity at a point is $\left(\tfrac14\right)$th of the maximum intensity. The minimum distance of the point from the central maximum is ______ $\mu$m. ($\lambda=600$ nm, $d=1.0$ mm, $D=1.0$ m)

**Solution.** $I=I_{max}\cos^2\dfrac\phi2=\dfrac{I_{max}}{4}\Rightarrow\cos\dfrac\phi2=\dfrac12\Rightarrow\phi=\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$, i.e. path difference $\dfrac\lambda3$. $y=\dfrac{D}{d}\cdot\dfrac\lambda3=\dfrac{1\times600\times10^{-9}}{10^{-3}\times3}=2\times10^{-4}$ m $=200\ \mu$m.

### P-WOP-19 — JEE Advanced 2025 · Paper 1 · Q12 · Numerical

In a single-slit experiment the slit width is found from $\dfrac{bd}D=m\lambda$ where $d$ is the distance of the $m$th maximum from the central maximum and $D$ the slit–screen distance. $D$ and $d$ are measured with scales of least count $1$ cm and $1$ mm; $\lambda=600$ nm and $m=3$ are exact. For $m=3$, $d=5$ mm and $D=1$ m, the absolute error in $b$ (in $\mu$m) is ______.

**Answer: ≈ 75.6** (accepted ranges $75$–$79$ or $94$–$95$) · official key ✔ (re-derived)

**Solution.** $b=\dfrac{m\lambda D}{d}=\dfrac{3\times600\times10^{-9}\times1}{5\times10^{-3}}=360\ \mu\text{m}$. $\dfrac{\Delta b}b=\dfrac{\Delta D}D+\dfrac{\Delta d}d=\dfrac{1\ \text{cm}}{100\ \text{cm}}+\dfrac{1\ \text{mm}}{5\ \text{mm}}=0.01+0.2=0.21$. $\Delta b=0.21\times360=75.6\ \mu\text{m}$.
