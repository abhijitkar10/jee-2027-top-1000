### P-EMW-01 · B — JEE Main 2026 · 21 Jan Shift 2 · set Q1 · Numerical

An electromagnetic wave of frequency $100$ MHz propagates through a medium of conductivity $\sigma=10$ mho/m. The ratio of the maximum conduction current density to the maximum displacement current density is ______. [Take $\dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}=9\times10^9\ \text{N m}^2/\text{C}^2$]

**Answer: 1800** · key (compiled)

**Solution.** $J_{c,max}=\sigma E_0$ and $J_{d,max}=\epsilon_0\omega E_0$, so the ratio is $\dfrac{\sigma}{\epsilon_0\omega}$. With $\epsilon_0\omega=\dfrac{2\pi\times10^8}{4\pi\times9\times10^9}=\dfrac1{180}$, the ratio is $10\times180=1800$.

### P-EMW-02 · B — JEE Main 2026 · 23 Jan Shift 1 · set Q2 · MCQ

### P-EMW-03 · B — JEE Main 2026 · 28 Jan Shift 2 · set Q4 · MCQ

A plane electromagnetic wave is moving in free space with velocity $c=3\times10^8$ m/s and its electric field is given by $\vec E=54\sin(kz-\omega t)\,\hat j$ V/m, where $\hat j$ is the unit vector along the $y$-axis. The magnetic field vector $\vec B$ of the wave is:

(1) $+1.8\times10^{-7}\sin(kz-\omega t)\,\hat i$ T  (2) $1.4\times10^{-7}\sin(kz-\omega t)\,\hat k$ T  (3) $-1.8\times10^{-7}\sin(kz-\omega t)\,\hat i$ T  (4) $1.4\times10^{-7}\sin(kz-\omega t)\,\hat i$ T

**Answer: (3)** · key (compiled)

**Solution.** $B_0=\dfrac{E_0}{c}=\dfrac{54}{3\times10^8}=1.8\times10^{-7}$ T. The wave travels along $+z$ and $\vec E\times\vec B$ must point along $+z$: $\hat j\times\vec B=\hat k$ requires $\vec B$ along $-\hat i$ (since $\hat j\times\hat i=-\hat k$). So $\vec B=-1.8\times10^{-7}\sin(kz-\omega t)\,\hat i$ T.

### P-EMW-04 · B — JEE Main 2026 · 22 Jan Shift 2 · set Q5 · MCQ

A laser beam has an intensity of $4.0\times10^{14}\ \text{W/m}^2$. The amplitude of the magnetic field associated with the beam is ______ T. (Take $\epsilon_0=8.85\times10^{-12}\ \text{C}^2/\text{N m}^2$ and $c=3\times10^8$ m/s)

(1) 18.3  (2) 1.83  (3) 5.5  (4) 2.0

**Answer: (2)** · key (compiled)

**Solution.** $I=\tfrac12\epsilon_0cE_0^2$ with $E_0=cB_0$ gives $I=\tfrac12\epsilon_0c^3B_0^2$. $B_0=\sqrt{\dfrac{2I}{\epsilon_0c^3}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{8.0\times10^{14}}{8.85\times10^{-12}\times2.7\times10^{25}}}=\sqrt{3.35}=1.83$ T.

### P-EMW-05 · B — JEE Main 2026 · 22 Jan Shift 1 · set Q6 · Numerical

The electric field of a plane electromagnetic wave travelling in an unknown non-magnetic medium is given by $E_y=20\sin\left(3\times10^6x-4.5\times10^{14}t\right)$ V/m (where $x$, $t$ and other values have SI units). The dielectric constant of the medium is ______. (speed of light in free space is $3\times10^8$ m/s)

**Answer: 4** · key (compiled)

**Solution.** $v=\dfrac{\omega}{k}=\dfrac{4.5\times10^{14}}{3\times10^6}=1.5\times10^8$ m/s, so $n=\dfrac cv=2$. For a non-magnetic medium the dielectric constant is $n^2=4$.

### P-EMW-06 · B — JEE Main 2026 · 23 Jan Shift 2 · set Q7 · MCQ

The ratio of the speeds of electromagnetic waves in vacuum and in a medium having dielectric constant $k=3$ and permeability $\mu=2\mu_0$ is: ($\mu_0$ = permeability of vacuum)

(1) 36 : 1  (2) 3 : 2  (3) 6 : 1  (4) $\sqrt6$ : 1

**Answer: (4)** · key (compiled)

**Solution.** $c=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{\mu_0\epsilon_0}}$ and $v=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{\mu\epsilon}}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2\mu_0\cdot3\epsilon_0}}=\dfrac{c}{\sqrt6}$. So $c:v=\sqrt6:1$.

### P-EMW-07 · B — JEE Main 2026 · 21 Jan Shift 1 · set Q8 · MCQ

The electric field in a plane electromagnetic wave is given by $E_y=69\sin\left[0.6\times10^3x-1.8\times10^{11}t\right]$ V/m. The expression for the magnetic field associated with this electromagnetic wave is ______ T.

(1) $B_z=2.3\times10^{-7}\sin\left[0.6\times10^3x-1.8\times10^{11}t\right]$  (2) $B_z=2.3\times10^{-7}\sin\left[0.6\times10^3x+1.8\times10^{11}t\right]$  (3) $B_y=69\sin\left[0.6\times10^3x+1.8\times10^{11}t\right]$  (4) $B_y=2.3\times10^{-7}\sin\left[0.6\times10^3x-1.8\times10^{11}t\right]$

**Answer: (1)** · key (compiled)

**Solution.** The wave travels along $+x$ (form $kx-\omega t$) with $\vec E$ along $y$, so $\vec B$ is along $z$ ($\hat j\times\hat k=\hat i$), in phase with $E$. $B_0=\dfrac{E_0}{c}=\dfrac{69}{3\times10^8}=2.3\times10^{-7}$ T, so $B_z=2.3\times10^{-7}\sin\left[0.6\times10^3x-1.8\times10^{11}t\right]$.

### P-EMW-08 · B — JEE Main 2025 · Jan session · EMW set Q1 · MCQ

A plane electromagnetic wave of frequency 20 MHz travels in free space along the $+x$ direction. At a particular point in space and time, the electric field vector of the wave is $E_y=9.3\ \text{V m}^{-1}$. Then the magnetic field vector of the wave at that point is:

(1) $B_z=6.2\times10^{-8}$ T  (2) $B_z=3.1\times10^{-8}$ T  (3) $B_z=1.55\times10^{-8}$ T  (4) $B_z=9.3\times10^{-8}$ T

**Answer: (2)** · key (compiled)

**Solution.** $B=\dfrac Ec=\dfrac{9.3}{3\times10^8}=3.1\times10^{-8}$ T. For propagation along $+x$ with $\vec E$ along $y$, $\hat E\times\hat B=\hat x$ requires $\vec B$ along $+z$.

### P-EMW-09 · B — JEE Main 2025 · Jan session · EMW set Q2 · MCQ

Assertion (A): Electromagnetic waves carry energy but not momentum. Reason (R): The mass of a photon is zero. Choose the most appropriate answer.

(1) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)  (2) Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A)  (3) (A) is false but (R) is true  (4) (A) is true but (R) is false

**Answer: (3)** · key (compiled)

**Solution.** EM waves carry both energy and momentum ($p=U/c$), so (A) is false. A photon is massless, so (R) is true.

### P-EMW-10 · B — JEE Main 2025 · Jan session · EMW set Q3 · MCQ

A plane electromagnetic wave propagates along the $+x$ direction in free space. The components of the electric field $\vec E$ and magnetic field $\vec B$ vectors associated with the wave in the Cartesian frame are:

(1) $E_x,B_y$  (2) $E_y,B_z$  (3) $E_z,B_y$  (4) $E_y,B_x$

**Answer: (2)** · key (compiled)

**Solution.** The wave is transverse: $\vec E$, $\vec B$ and the direction $x$ are mutually perpendicular, so neither field has an $x$-component. Of the options, only $(E_y,B_z)$ satisfies $\vec E\times\vec B\parallel+\hat x$ ($\hat y\times\hat z=\hat x$).

### P-EMW-13 · C — JEE Advanced 2025 · Paper 1 · Q9

A unit-volume cube contains $35\times10^7$ photons of frequency $10^{15}$ Hz. Treating the total photon energy as the average energy stored in an electromagnetic wave in the same volume, the amplitude of the magnetic field is $\alpha\times10^{-9}$ T ($\mu_0=4\pi\times10^{-7}$, $h=6\times10^{-34}$ J s, $\pi=\tfrac{22}7$). $\alpha=$ ______.

**Answer: ≈ 23** (accepted range $21$–$25$) · official key ✔ (re-derived)

**Solution.** Energy density $u=N h\nu=35\times10^7\times6\times10^{-34}\times10^{15}=2.1\times10^{-10}\ \text{J m}^{-3}$. In an EM wave the average energy density is $u=\dfrac{B_0^2}{2\mu_0}$ (electric and magnetic parts each contribute $\dfrac{B_0^2}{4\mu_0}$). $B_0=\sqrt{2\mu_0u}=\sqrt{2\times\dfrac{88}7\times10^{-7}\times2.1\times10^{-10}}\approx2.3\times10^{-8}$ T $=23\times10^{-9}$ T.

### P-EMW-14 · C — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 1 · Q15 · MCQ

A plane electromagnetic wave travelling in the $x$-direction is described by $E_y=(200\ \text{V m}^{-1})\sin[1.5\times10^{7}t-0.05x]$. The intensity of the wave is (Use $\varepsilon_0=8.85\times10^{-12}\ \text{C}^2\text{N}^{-1}\text{m}^{-2}$):

(1) 35.4 W m$^{-2}$  (2) 53.1 W m$^{-2}$  (3) 26.6 W m$^{-2}$  (4) 106.2 W m$^{-2}$

**Answer: (2)** · key ✔

**Solution.** $c=\omega/k=1.5\times10^7/0.05=3\times10^8$ m/s. $I=\tfrac12\varepsilon_0E_0^2c=\tfrac12(8.85\times10^{-12})(200)^2(3\times10^8)=53.1$ W m$^{-2}$.

### P-EMW-15 · C — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 2 · Q15 · MCQ

An object is placed in a medium of refractive index $3$. An electromagnetic wave of intensity $6\times10^8\ \text{W m}^{-2}$ falls normally on the object and is completely absorbed. The radiation pressure on the object is (speed of light in free space $=3\times10^8\ \text{m s}^{-1}$):

(1) 36 N m$^{-2}$  (2) 18 N m$^{-2}$  (3) 6 N m$^{-2}$  (4) 2 N m$^{-2}$

**Answer: (3)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Radiation pressure for complete absorption is $P=I/v$, with $v=c/n=10^8$ m/s in the medium. $P=\dfrac{6\times10^8}{10^8}=6\ \text{N m}^{-2}$.

### P-EMW-16 · C — JEE Main 2024 · 29 Jan Shift 2 · Q15 · MCQ

A plane electromagnetic wave of frequency $35$ MHz travels in free space along the $X$-direction. At a particular point (in space and time) $\vec E=9.6\hat j\ \text{V m}^{-1}$. The value of the magnetic field at this point is

(1) $3.2\times10^{-8}\hat k$ T  (2) $3.2\times10^{-8}\hat i$ T  (3) $9.6\hat j$ T  (4) $9.6\times10^{-8}\hat k$ T

**Answer: (1)** · key ✔

**Solution.** $B=E/c=\dfrac{9.6}{3\times10^8}=3.2\times10^{-8}$ T. The direction of propagation is $\hat E\times\hat B$: $\hat j\times\hat k=\hat i$, so $\vec B$ is along $+\hat k$.

### P-EMW-17 · C — JEE Main 2024 · 30 Jan Shift 2 · Q13 · MCQ

Match List I with List II. A. Gauss's law of magnetostatics  B. Faraday's law of electromagnetic induction  C. Ampere's law  D. Gauss's law of electrostatics. (i) $\oint\vec E\cdot d\vec a=\dfrac1{\varepsilon_0}\int\rho\,dV$  (ii) $\oint\vec B\cdot d\vec a=0$  (iii) $\oint\vec E\cdot d\vec l=-\dfrac d{dt}\int\vec B\cdot d\vec a$  (iv) $\oint\vec B\cdot d\vec l=\mu_0I$

(1) A-I, B-III, C-IV, D-II  (2) A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II  (3) A-IV, B-II, C-III, D-I  (4) A-II, B-III, C-IV, D-I

**Answer: (4)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Gauss's law for magnetism: no monopoles, $\oint\vec B\cdot d\vec a=0$ (ii). Faraday: (iii). Ampere: (iv). Gauss's law for electricity: (i). So A-II, B-III, C-IV, D-I.
