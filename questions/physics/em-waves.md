### P-EMW-01 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 1 · Q15 · MCQ

A plane electromagnetic wave travelling in the $x$-direction is described by $E_y=(200\ \text{V m}^{-1})\sin[1.5\times10^{7}t-0.05x]$. The intensity of the wave is (Use $\varepsilon_0=8.85\times10^{-12}\ \text{C}^2\text{N}^{-1}\text{m}^{-2}$):

(1) 35.4 W m$^{-2}$  (2) 53.1 W m$^{-2}$  (3) 26.6 W m$^{-2}$  (4) 106.2 W m$^{-2}$

**Answer: (2)** · key ✔

**Solution.** $c=\omega/k=1.5\times10^7/0.05=3\times10^8$ m/s. $I=\tfrac12\varepsilon_0E_0^2c=\tfrac12(8.85\times10^{-12})(200)^2(3\times10^8)=53.1$ W m$^{-2}$.
