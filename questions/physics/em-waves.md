### P-EMW-01 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 1 · Q15 · MCQ

A plane electromagnetic wave travelling in the $x$-direction is described by $E_y=(200\ \text{V m}^{-1})\sin[1.5\times10^{7}t-0.05x]$. The intensity of the wave is (Use $\varepsilon_0=8.85\times10^{-12}\ \text{C}^2\text{N}^{-1}\text{m}^{-2}$):

(1) 35.4 W m$^{-2}$  (2) 53.1 W m$^{-2}$  (3) 26.6 W m$^{-2}$  (4) 106.2 W m$^{-2}$

**Answer: (2)** · key ✔

**Solution.** $c=\omega/k=1.5\times10^7/0.05=3\times10^8$ m/s. $I=\tfrac12\varepsilon_0E_0^2c=\tfrac12(8.85\times10^{-12})(200)^2(3\times10^8)=53.1$ W m$^{-2}$.

### P-EMW-02 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 2 · Q15 · MCQ

An object is placed in a medium of refractive index $3$. An electromagnetic wave of intensity $6\times10^8\ \text{W m}^{-2}$ falls normally on the object and is completely absorbed. The radiation pressure on the object is (speed of light in free space $=3\times10^8\ \text{m s}^{-1}$):

(1) 36 N m$^{-2}$  (2) 18 N m$^{-2}$  (3) 6 N m$^{-2}$  (4) 2 N m$^{-2}$

**Answer: (3)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Radiation pressure for complete absorption is $P=I/v$, with $v=c/n=10^8$ m/s in the medium. $P=\dfrac{6\times10^8}{10^8}=6\ \text{N m}^{-2}$.

### P-EMW-03 — JEE Main 2024 · 29 Jan Shift 2 · Q15 · MCQ

A plane electromagnetic wave of frequency $35$ MHz travels in free space along the $X$-direction. At a particular point (in space and time) $\vec E=9.6\hat j\ \text{V m}^{-1}$. The value of the magnetic field at this point is

(1) $3.2\times10^{-8}\hat k$ T  (2) $3.2\times10^{-8}\hat i$ T  (3) $9.6\hat j$ T  (4) $9.6\times10^{-8}\hat k$ T

**Answer: (1)** · key ✔

**Solution.** $B=E/c=\dfrac{9.6}{3\times10^8}=3.2\times10^{-8}$ T. The direction of propagation is $\hat E\times\hat B$: $\hat j\times\hat k=\hat i$, so $\vec B$ is along $+\hat k$.

### P-EMW-04 — JEE Main 2024 · 30 Jan Shift 1 · Q16 · MCQ

The electric field of an electromagnetic wave in free space is $\vec E=E_0\cos(\omega t-kz)\,\hat i$. The corresponding magnetic induction vector will be

(1) $\vec B=E_0C\cos(\omega t-kz)\,\hat j$  (2) $\vec B=\dfrac{E_0}{C}\cos(\omega t-kz)\,\hat j$  (3) $\vec B=E_0C\cos(\omega t+kz)\,\hat j$  (4) $\vec B=\dfrac{E_0}{C}\cos(\omega t+kz)\,\hat j$

**Answer: (2)** · key ✔

**Solution.** The wave travels along $+z$ (phase $\omega t-kz$). $\vec E\times\vec B$ must point along $+z$: $\hat i\times\hat j=\hat k$, so $\vec B\parallel+\hat j$. In free space $B_0=E_0/C$ and $\vec B$ is in phase with $\vec E$.

### P-EMW-05 — JEE Main 2024 · 30 Jan Shift 2 · Q13 · MCQ

Match List I with List II. A. Gauss's law of magnetostatics  B. Faraday's law of electromagnetic induction  C. Ampere's law  D. Gauss's law of electrostatics. (i) $\oint\vec E\cdot d\vec a=\dfrac1{\varepsilon_0}\int\rho\,dV$  (ii) $\oint\vec B\cdot d\vec a=0$  (iii) $\oint\vec E\cdot d\vec l=-\dfrac d{dt}\int\vec B\cdot d\vec a$  (iv) $\oint\vec B\cdot d\vec l=\mu_0I$

(1) A-I, B-III, C-IV, D-II  (2) A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II  (3) A-IV, B-II, C-III, D-I  (4) A-II, B-III, C-IV, D-I

**Answer: (4)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Gauss's law for magnetism: no monopoles, $\oint\vec B\cdot d\vec a=0$ (ii). Faraday: (iii). Ampere: (iv). Gauss's law for electricity: (i). So A-II, B-III, C-IV, D-I.

### P-EMW-06 — JEE Main 2024 · 30 Jan Shift 2 · Q15 · MCQ

If the total energy transferred to a surface in time $t$ is $6.48\times10^5$ J, then the magnitude of the total momentum delivered to this surface for complete absorption will be

(1) $2.46\times10^{-3}$  (2) $2.16\times10^{-3}$  (3) $1.58\times10^{-3}$  (4) $4.32\times10^{-3}$ (all in kg m s$^{-1}$)

**Answer: (2)** · key ✔

**Solution.** For complete absorption of radiation, $p=\dfrac Uc=\dfrac{6.48\times10^5}{3\times10^8}=2.16\times10^{-3}\ \text{kg m s}^{-1}$.

### P-EMW-07 — JEE Main 2024 · 31 Jan Shift 1 · Q16 · MCQ

In a plane EM wave the electric field oscillates sinusoidally at a frequency of $5\times10^{10}$ Hz with an amplitude of $50\ \text{V m}^{-1}$. The total average energy density of the electromagnetic field of the wave is (use $\varepsilon_0=8.85\times10^{-12}\ \text{C}^2\text{N}^{-1}\text{m}^{-2}$)

(1) $1.106\times10^{-8}\ \text{J m}^{-3}$  (2) $4.425\times10^{-8}\ \text{J m}^{-3}$  (3) $2.212\times10^{-8}\ \text{J m}^{-3}$  (4) $2.212\times10^{-10}\ \text{J m}^{-3}$

**Answer: (1)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Average total energy density $u=\tfrac12\varepsilon_0E_0^2$ (the electric and magnetic parts contribute equally: $\tfrac14\varepsilon_0E_0^2$ each). $u=\tfrac12(8.85\times10^{-12})(50)^2=1.106\times10^{-8}\ \text{J m}^{-3}$.

### P-EMW-08 — JEE Main 2024 · 31 Jan Shift 2 · Q15 · MCQ

Statement I: Electromagnetic waves carry energy as they travel through space, and this energy is equally shared by the electric and magnetic fields.
Statement II: When electromagnetic waves strike a surface, a pressure is exerted on the surface.

(1) I incorrect, II correct  (2) Both correct  (3) Both incorrect  (4) I correct, II incorrect

**Answer: (2)** · key ✔

**Solution.** The energy densities are equal, $u_E=u_B=\tfrac12\varepsilon_0E^2=\dfrac{B^2}{2\mu_0}$, so I is true. Radiation carries momentum $p=U/c$ and exerts radiation pressure, so II is true.

### P-EMW-09 — JEE Main 2024 · 1 Feb Shift 2 · Q16 · MCQ

An electromagnetic wave of frequency $60$ MHz travels in air along the $z$ direction. The wavelength of the wave (in m) is:

(1) 2.5  (2) 10  (3) 5  (4) 2

**Answer: (3)** · key ✔

**Solution.** $\lambda=\dfrac{c}{f}=\dfrac{3\times10^8}{60\times10^6}=5$ m. ($\vec E$, $\vec B$ and the propagation direction are mutually perpendicular.)

### P-EMW-10 — JEE Main 2024 · 4 Apr Shift 1 · Q16 · MCQ

The electric field in an EM wave is $\vec E=\hat i\,40\cos\omega(t-z/c)\ \text{N C}^{-1}$. The magnetic induction of this wave (SI) is:

(1) $\hat k\dfrac{40}{c}\cos\omega(t-z/c)$  (2) $\hat j\,40\cos\omega(t-z/c)$  (3) $\hat i\dfrac{40}{c}\cos\omega(t-z/c)$  (4) $\hat j\dfrac{40}{c}\cos\omega(t-z/c)$

**Answer: (4)** · key ✔

**Solution.** The wave travels along $+z$, and $\hat E\times\hat B=\hat k$ with $\hat E=\hat i$ gives $\hat B=\hat j$. The magnitude is $B_0=E_0/c=40/c$.

### P-EMW-11 — JEE Main 2024 · 6 Apr Shift 1 · Q15 · MCQ

Electromagnetic waves travel in a medium with a speed of $1.5\times10^8\ \text{m s}^{-1}$. The relative permeability of the medium is $2.0$. The relative permittivity will be:

(1) 2  (2) 4  (3) 5  (4) 1

**Answer: (1)** · key ✔

**Solution.** $v=\dfrac{c}{\sqrt{\mu_r\epsilon_r}}\Rightarrow\sqrt{\mu_r\epsilon_r}=\dfrac{3\times10^8}{1.5\times10^8}=2$, so $\mu_r\epsilon_r=4$. With $\mu_r=2$: $\epsilon_r=2$.
