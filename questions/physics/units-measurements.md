### P-UNT-01 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 1 · Q1 · MCQ

Statement I: Planck's constant and angular momentum have the same dimensions.
Statement II: Linear momentum and moment of force have the same dimensions.

(1) Statement I is true but Statement II is false  (2) Both are false  (3) Both are true  (4) Statement I is false but Statement II is true

**Answer: (1)** · key ✔

**Solution.** $[h]=[E][T]=ML^2T^{-2}\cdot T=ML^2T^{-1}$ and angular momentum $L=mvr$ has $ML^2T^{-1}$, so I is true. Linear momentum is $MLT^{-1}$ but moment of force (torque) is $ML^2T^{-2}$, so II is false.

### P-UNT-02 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 2 · Q1 · MCQ

The equation of state of a real gas is $\left(P+\dfrac{a}{V^2}\right)(V-b)=RT$, where $P,V,T$ are pressure, volume and temperature and $R$ is the universal gas constant. The dimensions of $\dfrac{a}{b^2}$ are similar to those of

(1) $PV$  (2) $P$  (3) $RT$  (4) $R$

**Answer: (2)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Terms added must share dimensions, so $[a/V^2]=[P]\Rightarrow[a]=[P][V^2]$. Also $[b]=[V]$. Hence $\left[\dfrac a{b^2}\right]=\dfrac{[P][V^2]}{[V^2]}=[P]$.

### P-UNT-03 — JEE Main 2024 · 29 Jan Shift 1 · Q1 · MCQ

The resistance $R=\dfrac VI$, where $V=(200\pm5)$ V and $I=(20\pm0.2)$ A. The percentage error in the measurement of $R$ is

(1) 3.5%  (2) 7%  (3) 3%  (4) 5.5%

**Answer: (1)** · key ✔

**Solution.** For a quotient, maximum fractional errors add: $\dfrac{\Delta R}R=\dfrac{\Delta V}V+\dfrac{\Delta I}I=\dfrac5{200}+\dfrac{0.2}{20}=0.025+0.01=0.035$, i.e. $3.5\%$.

### P-UNT-04 — JEE Main 2024 · 29 Jan Shift 2 · Q1 · MCQ

A physical quantity $Q$ is found to depend on $a,b,c$ by the relation $Q=\dfrac{a^4b^3}{c^2}$. The percentage errors in $a,b,c$ are $3\%,4\%$ and $5\%$ respectively. The percentage error in $Q$ is

(1) 66%  (2) 43%  (3) 34%  (4) 14%

**Answer: (3)** · key ✔

**Solution.** For $Q=a^pb^qc^r$ the maximum fractional error is $|p|\dfrac{\Delta a}a+|q|\dfrac{\Delta b}b+|r|\dfrac{\Delta c}c=4(3)+3(4)+2(5)=12+12+10=34\%$.

### P-UNT-05 — JEE Main 2024 · 30 Jan Shift 2 · Q1 · MCQ

If mass is written as $m=kc^PG^{-1/2}h^{1/2}$ (constants have their usual meaning, $k$ a dimensionless constant), then the value of $P$ is

(1) $\tfrac12$  (2) $\tfrac13$  (3) 2  (4) $-\tfrac13$

**Answer: (1)** · key ✔

**Solution.** $[G]=M^{-1}L^3T^{-2}$ and $[h]=ML^2T^{-1}$, so $[G^{-1/2}h^{1/2}]=M^{1/2}L^{-3/2}T\cdot M^{1/2}L\,T^{-1/2}=ML^{-1/2}T^{1/2}$. Multiplying by $[c^P]=L^PT^{-P}$ must give $M$: the $L$ exponent $P-\tfrac12=0$ and the $T$ exponent $\tfrac12-P=0$ both give $P=\tfrac12$.

### P-UNT-06 — JEE Main 2024 · 31 Jan Shift 1 · Q1 · MCQ

If the percentage errors in measuring the length and the diameter of a wire are $0.1\%$ each, the percentage error in measuring its resistance will be

(1) 0.2%  (2) 0.3%  (3) 0.1%  (4) 0.144%

**Answer: (2)** · key ✔

**Solution.** $R=\dfrac{\rho l}{A}=\dfrac{4\rho l}{\pi d^2}$, so $\dfrac{\Delta R}R=\dfrac{\Delta l}l+2\dfrac{\Delta d}d=0.1+2(0.1)=0.3\%$.

### P-UNT-07 — JEE Main 2024 · 31 Jan Shift 1 · Q2 · MCQ

A force is represented by $F=ax^2+bt^{1/2}$, where $x$ is distance and $t$ is time. The dimensions of $\dfrac{b^2}a$ are

(1) $[ML^3T^{-3}]$  (2) $[MLT^{-2}]$  (3) $[ML^{-1}T^{-1}]$  (4) $[ML^2T^{-3}]$

**Answer: (1)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Each term has the dimensions of force. $[a]=\dfrac{MLT^{-2}}{L^2}=ML^{-1}T^{-2}$ and $[b]=\dfrac{MLT^{-2}}{T^{1/2}}=MLT^{-5/2}$, so $[b^2]=M^2L^2T^{-5}$. $\left[\dfrac{b^2}a\right]=\dfrac{M^2L^2T^{-5}}{ML^{-1}T^{-2}}=ML^3T^{-3}$.

### P-UNT-08 — JEE Main 2024 · 31 Jan Shift 2 · Q2 · MCQ

Two physical quantities $A$ and $B$ are related as $E=\dfrac{B-x^2}{At}$, where $E$, $x$ and $t$ have the dimensions of energy, length and time respectively. The dimension of $AB$ is

(1) $L^{-2}M^{1}T^{0}$  (2) $L^{2}M^{-1}T^{1}$  (3) $L^{-2}M^{-1}T^{1}$  (4) $L^{0}M^{-1}T^{1}$

**Answer: (2)** · key ✔

**Solution.** $B$ is added to $x^2$, so $[B]=L^2$. Then $[E]=\dfrac{[B]}{[A][t]}\Rightarrow[A]=\dfrac{L^2}{ML^2T^{-2}\cdot T}=M^{-1}T$. So $[AB]=M^{-1}T\cdot L^2=L^2M^{-1}T^1$.

### P-UNT-09 — JEE Main 2024 · 31 Jan Shift 2 · Q20 · MCQ

The measured value of the length of a simple pendulum is $20$ cm with $2$ mm accuracy. The time for $50$ oscillations was measured to be $40$ s with $1$ s resolution. From these measurements, the accuracy in the measurement of the acceleration due to gravity is $N\%$. The value of $N$ is

(1) 4  (2) 8  (3) 6  (4) 5

**Answer: (3)** · key ✔

**Solution.** $g=\dfrac{4\pi^2L}{T^2}$, so $\dfrac{\Delta g}g=\dfrac{\Delta L}L+2\dfrac{\Delta T}T=\dfrac{0.2}{20}+2\cdot\dfrac{1}{40}=0.01+0.05=0.06$, i.e. $6\%$.

### P-UNT-10 — JEE Main 2024 · 1 Feb Shift 1 · Q1 · MCQ

The radius $r$, length $l$ and resistance $R$ of a metal wire were measured as $r=0.35\pm0.05$ cm, $R=100\pm10$ ohm, $l=15\pm0.2$ cm. The percentage error in the resistivity of the material of the wire is

(1) 25.6%  (2) 39.9%  (3) 37.3%  (4) 35.6%

**Answer: (2)** · key ✔

**Solution.** $\rho=\dfrac{R\pi r^2}{l}$, so $\dfrac{\Delta\rho}\rho=\dfrac{\Delta R}R+2\dfrac{\Delta r}r+\dfrac{\Delta l}l=10\%+2\left(\dfrac{0.05}{0.35}\times100\right)\%+\dfrac{0.2}{15}\times100\%=10+28.57+1.33\approx39.9\%$.

### P-UNT-11 — JEE Main 2024 · 1 Feb Shift 1 · Q2 · MCQ

The dimensional formula of angular impulse is

(1) $[ML^{-2}T^{-1}]$  (2) $[ML^2T^{-2}]$  (3) $[MLT^{-1}]$  (4) $[ML^2T^{-1}]$

**Answer: (4)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Angular impulse $=\text{torque}\times\text{time}=[ML^2T^{-2}][T]=[ML^2T^{-1}]$, the same as angular momentum.
