### P-ACC-01 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 2 · Q14 · MCQ

The primary side of a transformer is connected to a $230$ V, $50$ Hz supply. The turns ratio of primary to secondary is $10:1$. The load resistance on the secondary is $46\ \Omega$. The power consumed in it is

(1) 12.5 W  (2) 10.0 W  (3) 11.5 W  (4) 12.0 W

**Answer: (3)** · key ✔

**Solution.** $V_s=230/10=23$ V, so $P=\dfrac{V_s^2}{R}=\dfrac{23^2}{46}=11.5$ W.

### P-ACC-02 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 2 · Q28 · Numerical

A series LCR circuit with $L=\dfrac{100}{\pi}$ mH, $C=\dfrac{10^{-3}}{\pi}$ F and $R=10\ \Omega$ is connected across a $220$ V, $50$ Hz supply. The power factor of the circuit is ______.

**Answer: 1** · key ✔

**Solution.** $X_L=2\pi(50)\dfrac{0.1}\pi=10\ \Omega$ and $X_C=\dfrac1{2\pi(50)\cdot10^{-3}/\pi}=10\ \Omega$. $X_L=X_C$: resonance, $Z=R$, so $\cos\phi=R/Z=1$.

### P-ACC-03 — JEE Main 2024 · 29 Jan Shift 1 · Q12 · MCQ

A capacitor of capacitance $100\ \mu$F is charged to a potential of $12$ V and connected to a $6.4$ mH inductor to produce oscillations. The maximum current in the circuit would be

(1) 3.2 A  (2) 1.5 A  (3) 2.0 A  (4) 1.2 A

**Answer: (2)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Energy conservation: $\tfrac12LI_{\max}^2=\tfrac12CV^2\Rightarrow I_{\max}=V\sqrt{C/L}=12\sqrt{\dfrac{100\times10^{-6}}{6.4\times10^{-3}}}=12\times0.125=1.5$ A.

### P-ACC-04 — JEE Main 2024 · 29 Jan Shift 2 · Q14 · MCQ

In an a.c. circuit, the voltage and current are $V=100\sin(100t)$ V and $I=100\sin\!\left(100t+\dfrac\pi3\right)$ mA respectively. The average power dissipated in one cycle is

(1) 5 W  (2) 10 W  (3) 2.5 W  (4) 25 W

**Answer: (3)** · key ✔

### P-ACC-05 — JEE Main 2024 · 30 Jan Shift 1 · Q14 · MCQ

A series $LR$ circuit connected to an ac source $E=(25\sqrt2\sin1000t)$ V has a power factor of $\dfrac1{\sqrt2}$. If the source of emf is changed to $E=(20\sin2000t)$ V, the new power factor of the circuit will be

(1) $\dfrac1{\sqrt2}$  (2) $\dfrac1{\sqrt3}$  (3) $\dfrac1{\sqrt5}$  (4) $\dfrac1{\sqrt7}$

**Answer: (3)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Initially $\cos\phi=\dfrac R{\sqrt{R^2+X_L^2}}=\dfrac1{\sqrt2}\Rightarrow X_L=R$ at $\omega=1000$. Doubling $\omega$ doubles $X_L$: $X_L'=2R$. New power factor $=\dfrac R{\sqrt{R^2+4R^2}}=\dfrac1{\sqrt5}$. (The change in source amplitude does not affect it.)

### P-ACC-06 — JEE Main 2024 · 30 Jan Shift 2 · Q12 · MCQ

An alternating voltage $V(t)=220\sin100\pi t$ volt is applied to a purely resistive load. The time taken for the current to rise from half of the peak value to the peak value is

(1) 5 ms  (2) 3.3 ms  (3) 7.2 ms  (4) 2.2 ms

**Answer: (2)** · key ✔

**Solution.** The current is in phase with the voltage: $I=I_0\sin\omega t$ with $\omega=100\pi$. Half the peak occurs at $\omega t=\tfrac\pi6$ and the peak at $\omega t=\tfrac\pi2$. $\Delta t=\dfrac{\pi/3}{100\pi}=\dfrac1{300}\ \text{s}=3.3$ ms.

### P-ACC-09 — JEE Main 2024 · 1 Feb Shift 1 · Q14 · MCQ

A parallel plate capacitor has capacitance $C=200$ pF. It is connected to a $230$ V ac supply with an angular frequency of $300\ \text{rad s}^{-1}$. The rms values of the conduction current in the circuit and the displacement current in the capacitor respectively are

(1) 1.38 μA and 1.38 μA  (2) 14.3 μA and 143 μA  (3) 13.8 μA and 138 μA  (4) 13.8 μA and 13.8 μA

**Answer: (4)** · key ✔

**Solution.** $I_{rms}=\dfrac{V_{rms}}{X_C}=V_{rms}\,\omega C=230\times300\times200\times10^{-12}=13.8\ \mu\text{A}$. By continuity of current, the displacement current between the plates has the same value, $13.8\ \mu$A.

### P-ACC-10 — JEE Main 2024 · 1 Feb Shift 1 · Q15 · MCQ

In a series LCR circuit the capacitance is changed from $C$ to $4C$. To keep the resonance frequency unchanged, the new inductance should be

(1) reduced by $\tfrac14L$  (2) increased by $2L$  (3) reduced by $\tfrac34L$  (4) increased to $4L$

**Answer: (3)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Resonance frequency $f=\dfrac1{2\pi\sqrt{LC}}$ is fixed, so $LC$ is constant: $L'(4C)=LC\Rightarrow L'=\tfrac L4$. This is a reduction by $L-\tfrac L4=\tfrac34L$.

### P-ACC-12 — JEE Main 2024 · 4 Apr Shift 1 · Q15 · MCQ

In an ac circuit the instantaneous current is zero when the instantaneous voltage is maximum. The source may be connected to: A. pure inductor  B. pure capacitor  C. pure resistor  D. a combination of an inductor and a capacitor.

(1) A, B and C only  (2) A and B only  (3) B, C and D only  (4) A, B and D only

**Answer: (4)** · key ✔

**Solution.** This needs a $90^\circ$ phase difference between $V$ and $I$. A pure $L$ or pure $C$ gives exactly $\pm90^\circ$, and an ideal series $L$–$C$ combination (no resistance) also gives $\pm90^\circ$. A pure resistor gives $0^\circ$. So A, B and D.

### P-ACC-13 — JEE Main 2024 · 4 Apr Shift 1 · Q28 · Numerical

### P-ACC-15 — JEE Main 2024 · 5 Apr Shift 1 · Q28 · Numerical

A series LCR circuit has $L=1$ H, $R=300\ \Omega$, $C=20\ \mu\text{F}$, driven by $V=50\sqrt2\sin(100t)$ V. The r.m.s. potential difference across the capacitor is ______ V.

**Answer: 50** · key ✔

**Solution.** $X_L=100\times1=100\ \Omega$, $X_C=\dfrac{1}{100\times20\times10^{-6}}=500\ \Omega$. $Z=\sqrt{300^2+400^2}=500\ \Omega$. $I_{rms}=\dfrac{50}{500}=0.1$ A, so $V_C=0.1\times500=50$ V.

### P-ACC-17 — JEE Main 2024 · 6 Apr Shift 1 · Q14 · MCQ

Statement I: In an LCR series circuit, the current is maximum at resonance.
Statement II: The current in a purely resistive circuit can never be less than that in a series LCR circuit connected to the same voltage source.

(1) I false, II true  (2) I true, II false  (3) Both true  (4) Both false

**Answer: (3)** · key ✔

**Solution.** At resonance $Z=R$ (its minimum), so $I=V/R$ is maximum: I true. For any $\omega$, $Z=\sqrt{R^2+(X_L-X_C)^2}\ge R$, so the LCR current never exceeds the purely resistive current $V/R$: II true.

### P-ACC-18 — JEE Main 2024 · 6 Apr Shift 1 · Q28 · Numerical

A dc voltage of $100$ V applied to an inductor gives a dc current of $5$ A. When an ac voltage of $200$ V peak is connected to the inductor, its inductive reactance is $20\sqrt3\ \Omega$. The power dissipated in the circuit is ______ W.

**Answer: 250** · key ✔

**Solution.** The dc resistance is $R=\dfrac{100}{5}=20\ \Omega$. $Z=\sqrt{20^2+(20\sqrt3)^2}=40\ \Omega$. $I_{rms}=\dfrac{200/\sqrt2}{40}=\dfrac{5}{\sqrt2}$ A, so $P=I_{rms}^2R=12.5\times20=250$ W.

### P-ACC-19 — JEE Main 2024 · 8 Apr Shift 1 · Q14 · MCQ

An LCR circuit is at resonance for a capacitor $C$, inductance $L$ and resistance $R$. The value of the resistance is now halved keeping all other parameters the same. The current amplitude at resonance will now be:

(1) zero  (2) same  (3) halved  (4) double

**Answer: (4)** · key ✔

**Solution.** At resonance $Z=R$, so $I_0=\dfrac{V_0}{R}$. Halving $R$ doubles the current amplitude.

### P-ACC-20 — JEE Main 2024 · 8 Apr Shift 2 · Q14 · MCQ

A coil of negligible resistance is connected in series with a $90\ \Omega$ resistor across a $120$ V, $60$ Hz supply. A voltmeter reads $36$ V across the resistance. The inductance of the coil is:

(1) 0.286 H  (2) 0.76 H  (3) 2.86 H  (4) 0.91 H

**Answer: (2)** · key ✔

**Solution.** $I=\dfrac{36}{90}=0.4$ A. $Z=\dfrac{120}{0.4}=300\ \Omega$. $X_L=\sqrt{Z^2-R^2}=\sqrt{300^2-90^2}=286\ \Omega$. $L=\dfrac{X_L}{2\pi f}=\dfrac{286}{2\pi\times60}=0.76$ H.

### P-ACC-23 — JEE Main 2024 · 9 Apr Shift 1 · Q28 · Numerical

A coil connected across a $20$ V dc supply draws a current of $5$ A. Connected across a $20$ V, $50$ Hz ac supply, it draws $4$ A. The self-inductance of the coil is ______ mH. (Take $\pi=3$)

**Answer: 10** · key ✔

**Solution.** $R=\dfrac{20}{5}=4\ \Omega$. On ac: $Z=\dfrac{20}{4}=5\ \Omega$, so $X_L=\sqrt{25-16}=3\ \Omega$. $L=\dfrac{X_L}{2\pi f}=\dfrac{3}{2\times3\times50}=0.01$ H $=10$ mH.
