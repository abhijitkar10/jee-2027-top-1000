### P-COM-01 — JEE Main 2024 · 30 Jan Shift 1 · Q5 · MCQ

A spherical body of mass $100$ g is dropped from a height of $10$ m. After hitting the ground the body rebounds to a height of $5$ m. The impulse of force imparted by the ground to the body is (given $g=9.8\ \text{m s}^{-2}$)

Find the impulse in kg m s$^{-1}$ (the options were $4.32$, $23.9$, $43.2$ and $2.39$).

**Answer: $2.39\ \text{kg m s}^{-1}$ (option 4)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Speed just before impact: $v_1=\sqrt{2gh_1}=\sqrt{2(9.8)(10)}=14$ m/s (downward). Speed just after: $v_2=\sqrt{2(9.8)(5)}=9.9$ m/s (upward). Impulse $=\Delta p=m(v_1+v_2)=0.1\times(14+9.9)=2.39$ kg m s$^{-1}$.

### P-COM-02 — JEE Main 2024 · 31 Jan Shift 1 · Q7 · MCQ

An artillery piece of mass $M_1$ fires a shell of mass $M_2$ horizontally. Instantaneously after the firing, the ratio of the kinetic energy of the artillery to that of the shell is

(1) $\dfrac{M_1}{M_1+M_2}$  (2) $\dfrac{M_2}{M_1}$  (3) $\dfrac{M_2}{M_1+M_2}$  (4) $\dfrac{M_1}{M_2}$

**Answer: (2)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Momentum conservation from rest: equal and opposite momenta $p$. $K=\dfrac{p^2}{2m}$, so $\dfrac{K_{\text{artillery}}}{K_{\text{shell}}}=\dfrac{1/M_1}{1/M_2}=\dfrac{M_2}{M_1}$.
