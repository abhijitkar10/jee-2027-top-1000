### P-COM-01 — JEE Main 2024 · 30 Jan Shift 1 · Q5 · MCQ

A spherical body of mass $100$ g is dropped from a height of $10$ m. After hitting the ground the body rebounds to a height of $5$ m. The impulse of force imparted by the ground to the body is (given $g=9.8\ \text{m s}^{-2}$)

Find the impulse in kg m s$^{-1}$ (the options were $4.32$, $23.9$, $43.2$ and $2.39$).

**Answer: $2.39\ \text{kg m s}^{-1}$ (option 4)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Speed just before impact: $v_1=\sqrt{2gh_1}=\sqrt{2(9.8)(10)}=14$ m/s (downward). Speed just after: $v_2=\sqrt{2(9.8)(5)}=9.9$ m/s (upward). Impulse $=\Delta p=m(v_1+v_2)=0.1\times(14+9.9)=2.39$ kg m s$^{-1}$.

### P-COM-02 — JEE Main 2024 · 31 Jan Shift 1 · Q7 · MCQ

An artillery piece of mass $M_1$ fires a shell of mass $M_2$ horizontally. Instantaneously after the firing, the ratio of the kinetic energy of the artillery to that of the shell is

(1) $\dfrac{M_1}{M_1+M_2}$  (2) $\dfrac{M_2}{M_1}$  (3) $\dfrac{M_2}{M_1+M_2}$  (4) $\dfrac{M_1}{M_2}$

**Answer: (2)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Momentum conservation from rest: equal and opposite momenta $p$. $K=\dfrac{p^2}{2m}$, so $\dfrac{K_{\text{artillery}}}{K_{\text{shell}}}=\dfrac{1/M_1}{1/M_2}=\dfrac{M_2}{M_1}$.

### P-COM-03 — JEE Main 2024 · 1 Feb Shift 1 · Q5 · MCQ

A simple pendulum of length $1$ m has a wooden bob of mass $1$ kg. It is struck by a bullet of mass $10^{-2}$ kg moving with a speed of $2\times10^2\ \text{m s}^{-1}$. The bullet gets embedded into the bob. The height to which the bob rises before swinging back is ($g=10\ \text{m s}^{-2}$)

(1) 0.30 m  (2) 0.20 m  (3) 0.35 m  (4) 0.40 m

**Answer: (2)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Momentum conservation: $0.01\times200=(1.01)v\Rightarrow v=1.98$ m/s. Then $h=\dfrac{v^2}{2g}=\dfrac{3.92}{20}=0.196\approx0.20$ m.

### P-COM-04 — JEE Main 2024 · 1 Feb Shift 1 · Q22 · Numerical

Identical spheres each of mass $2M$ are placed at the corners of a right-angled triangle with mutually perpendicular sides equal to $4$ m each. Taking the point of intersection of these two sides as the origin, the magnitude of the position vector of the centre of mass of the system is $\dfrac{4\sqrt2}x$, where $x=$ ______.

**Answer: 3** · key ✔

**Solution.** The three equal masses are at $(0,0)$, $(4,0)$ and $(0,4)$. $\vec r_{cm}=\dfrac{(0+4+0,\ 0+0+4)}3=\left(\tfrac43,\tfrac43\right)$, with magnitude $\dfrac{4\sqrt2}3$. So $x=3$.

### P-COM-05 — JEE Main 2024 · 4 Apr Shift 2 · Q22 · Numerical

Two particles of masses $m_1=3$ kg and $m_2=2$ kg are placed at some distance. $m_1$ is moved $2$ cm towards the centre of mass. To keep the centre of mass at its original position, $m_2$ should move towards the centre of mass by ______ cm.

**Answer: 3** · key ✔

**Solution.** The centre of mass is unchanged if the moments about it stay balanced: $m_1\Delta_1=m_2\Delta_2\Rightarrow3\times2=2\times\Delta_2$, so $\Delta_2=3$ cm.

### P-COM-06 — JEE Main 2024 · 8 Apr Shift 1 · Q4 · MCQ

A stationary particle breaks into two parts of masses $m_A$ and $m_B$ which move with velocities $v_A$ and $v_B$. The ratio of their kinetic energies $(K_B:K_A)$ is:

(1) $v_B:v_A$  (2) $m_B:m_A$  (3) $m_Bv_B:m_Av_A$  (4) $1:1$

**Answer: (1)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Momentum conservation: $m_Av_A=m_Bv_B$ (equal and opposite momenta). $\dfrac{K_B}{K_A}=\dfrac{\tfrac12m_Bv_B^2}{\tfrac12m_Av_A^2}=\dfrac{(m_Bv_B)v_B}{(m_Av_A)v_A}=\dfrac{v_B}{v_A}$.
