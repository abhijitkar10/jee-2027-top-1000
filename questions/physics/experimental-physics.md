### P-EXP-01 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 1 · Q20 · MCQ

Identify the physical quantity that cannot be measured using a spherometer.

(1) Radius of curvature of a concave surface  (2) Specific rotation of liquids  (3) Thickness of thin plates  (4) Radius of curvature of a convex surface

**Answer: (2)** · key ✔

**Solution.** A spherometer measures the sagitta of a surface, so it gives radius of curvature (concave or convex) and small thicknesses. Specific rotation of a liquid needs a polarimeter.

### P-EXP-02 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 2 · Q20 · MCQ

Assertion (A): In a vernier calliper, if a positive zero error exists, the reading taken while measuring will be more than the actual reading.
Reason (R): The zero error in a vernier calliper might have happened due to a manufacturing defect or due to rough handling.

(1) Both correct and R explains A  (2) Both correct but R does not explain A  (3) A true, R false  (4) A false, R true

**Answer: (2)** · key ✔

**Solution.** With positive zero error the observed reading $=$ true value $+$ zero error, so it is larger than the actual value (A true). R also states a true origin of zero error, but that does not explain why readings come out larger, so R is not the explanation of A.

### P-EXP-03 — JEE Main 2024 · 30 Jan Shift 2 · Q20 · MCQ

If $50$ vernier divisions are equal to $49$ main scale divisions of a travelling microscope and one smallest reading of the main scale is $0.5$ mm, the vernier constant of the travelling microscope is

(1) 0.1 mm  (2) 0.1 cm  (3) 0.01 cm  (4) 0.01 mm

**Answer: (4)** · key ✔

**Solution.** $50\,\text{VSD}=49\,\text{MSD}\Rightarrow1\,\text{VSD}=\tfrac{49}{50}\,\text{MSD}$. Vernier constant $=1\,\text{MSD}-1\,\text{VSD}=\tfrac1{50}\,\text{MSD}=\dfrac{0.5}{50}=0.01$ mm.

### P-EXP-04 — JEE Main 2024 · 1 Feb Shift 1 · Q20 · MCQ

$10$ divisions on the main scale of a vernier calliper coincide with $11$ divisions on the vernier scale. If each division on the main scale is $5$ units, the least count of the instrument is

(1) $\tfrac12$  (2) $\tfrac{10}{11}$  (3) $\tfrac{50}{11}$  (4) $\tfrac5{11}$

**Answer: (4)** · key ✔

**Solution.** $11\,\text{VSD}=10\,\text{MSD}=50$ units, so $1\,\text{VSD}=\tfrac{50}{11}$. Least count $=1\,\text{MSD}-1\,\text{VSD}=5-\tfrac{50}{11}=\tfrac5{11}$ unit.

### § — JEE Main 2026 · 23 Jan Shift 1 · set Q1 · MCQ

In a screw gauge, the zero of the circular scale lies $3$ divisions above the horizontal pitch line when the metallic studs are brought in contact. Using this instrument the thickness of a sheet is measured. If the pitch scale reading is $1$ mm and the circular scale reading is $51$, the correct thickness of the sheet is ______ mm. [Assume the least count is $0.01$ mm]

(1) 1.50  (2) 1.48  (3) 1.54  (4) 1.51

**Answer: (3)** · key (compiled)

**Solution.** The zero lies above the pitch line, so the zero error is negative: $e=-3\times0.01=-0.03$ mm. Reading $=1+51\times0.01-(-0.03)=1.54$ mm.

### § — JEE Main 2026 · 28 Jan Shift 2 · set Q2 · MCQ

In an experiment, a set of readings is obtained as follows: $1.24$ mm, $1.25$ mm, $1.23$ mm, $1.21$ mm. The expected least count of the instrument used in recording these readings is ______ mm.

(1) 0.001  (2) 0.1  (3) 0.01  (4) 0.05

**Answer: (3)** · key (compiled)

**Solution.** All readings are recorded to two decimal places and differ by multiples of $0.01$ mm, so the smallest readable division (least count) is $0.01$ mm.

### § — JEE Main 2026 · 24 Jan Shift 2 · set Q3 · MCQ

In a vernier callipers, $50$ vernier scale divisions are equal to $48$ main scale divisions. If one main scale division $=0.05$ mm, the least count of the vernier callipers is ______ mm.

(1) 0.005  (2) 0.05  (3) 0.002  (4) 0.02

**Answer: (3)** · key (compiled)

**Solution.** $50\ \text{VSD}=48\times0.05=2.4$ mm, so $1\ \text{VSD}=0.048$ mm. Least count $=1\ \text{MSD}-1\ \text{VSD}=0.05-0.048=0.002$ mm.

### § — JEE Main 2026 · 28 Jan Shift 1 · set Q4 · MCQ

When both jaws of a vernier callipers touch each other, the zero mark of the vernier scale is to the right of the zero mark of the main scale and the $4^{th}$ mark on the vernier scale coincides with a mark on the main scale. While measuring the length of a cylinder, the observer notes $15$ divisions on the main scale and the $5^{th}$ division of the vernier scale coincides with a main scale division. The measured length of the cylinder is ______ mm. (Least count of the vernier callipers $=0.1$ mm)

(1) 15.5  (2) 15.9  (3) 15.1  (4) 15.4

**Answer: (3)** · key (compiled)

**Solution.** The zero error is positive: $e=+4\times0.1=+0.4$ mm. Observed reading $=15+5\times0.1=15.5$ mm. Correct length $=15.5-0.4=15.1$ mm.
