### P-ATN-01 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 1 · Q18 · MCQ

The radius of the third stationary orbit of an electron in Bohr's atom is $R$. The radius of the fourth stationary orbit will be

(1) $\tfrac43R$  (2) $\tfrac{16}{9}R$  (3) $\tfrac34R$  (4) $\tfrac{9}{16}R$

**Answer: (2)** · key ✔

**Solution.** $r_n\propto n^2$, so $r_4/r_3=16/9$ and $r_4=\tfrac{16}{9}R$.

### P-ATN-02 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 1 · Q30 · Numerical

In a nuclear fission process, a high-mass nuclide ($A\approx236$) with binding energy $7.6$ MeV/nucleon splits into two middle-mass nuclides ($A\approx118$ each) with binding energy $8.6$ MeV/nucleon. The energy released is ______ MeV.

**Answer: 236** · key ✔

**Solution.** $Q=B_{\text{products}}-B_{\text{parent}}=2(118)(8.6)-236(7.6)=236(8.6-7.6)=236$ MeV.

### P-ATN-03 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 2 · Q18 · MCQ

The atomic mass of ${}^{12}_{6}\text{C}$ is $12.000000$ u and that of ${}^{13}_{6}\text{C}$ is $13.003354$ u. The energy required to remove a neutron from ${}^{13}_{6}\text{C}$, if the mass of a neutron is $1.008665$ u, is

(1) 62.5 MeV  (2) 6.25 MeV  (3) 4.95 MeV  (4) 49.5 MeV

**Answer: (3)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Mass defect $=m({}^{12}\text{C})+m_n-m({}^{13}\text{C})=12+1.008665-13.003354=0.005311$ u. Energy $=0.005311\times931.5\approx4.95$ MeV.

### P-ATN-04 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 2 · Q30 · Numerical

If the Rydberg constant is $R$, the longest wavelength of radiation in the Paschen series is $\dfrac{\alpha}{7R}$. Find $\alpha$.

**Answer: 144** · key ✔

**Solution.** Paschen: $n_f=3$. Longest wavelength is for $n_i=4$: $\dfrac1\lambda=R\left(\dfrac19-\dfrac1{16}\right)=\dfrac{7R}{144}$, so $\lambda=\dfrac{144}{7R}$ and $\alpha=144$.

### P-ATN-05 — JEE Main 2024 · 29 Jan Shift 1 · Q19 · MCQ

The explosive in a hydrogen bomb is a mixture of ${}^2_1\text{H}$, ${}^3_1\text{H}$ and ${}^6_3\text{Li}$ in some condensed form. The reactions are ${}^6_3\text{Li}+{}^1_0n\to{}^4_2\text{He}+{}^3_1\text{H}$ and ${}^2_1\text{H}+{}^3_1\text{H}\to{}^4_2\text{He}+{}^1_0n$. During the explosion the energy released is approximately [Given $M(\text{Li})=6.01690$ amu, $M({}^2_1\text{H})=2.01471$ amu, $M({}^4_2\text{He})=4.00388$ amu, $1\text{ amu}=931.5$ MeV]

(1) 28.12 MeV  (2) 12.64 MeV  (3) 16.48 MeV  (4) 22.22 MeV

**Answer: (4)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Adding the two reactions, the neutron and the tritium cancel, leaving the net reaction ${}^6\text{Li}+{}^2\text{H}\to2\,{}^4\text{He}$. Mass defect $=6.01690+2.01471-2(4.00388)=0.02385$ amu, so $Q=0.02385\times931.5\approx22.22$ MeV.

### P-ATN-06 — JEE Main 2024 · 29 Jan Shift 1 · Q30 · Numerical

When a hydrogen atom goes from $n=2$ to $n=1$ it emits a photon, and its recoil speed is $\dfrac x5\ \text{m s}^{-1}$. Find $x$. (Use mass of hydrogen atom $=1.6\times10^{-27}$ kg, $e=1.6\times10^{-19}$ C)

**Answer: 17** · key ✔

**Solution.** $E=13.6\left(1-\tfrac14\right)=10.2$ eV $=1.632\times10^{-18}$ J. Photon momentum $p=E/c=5.44\times10^{-27}$ kg m/s. Recoil speed $v=\dfrac pm=\dfrac{5.44\times10^{-27}}{1.6\times10^{-27}}=3.4=\dfrac{17}5$ m/s, so $x=17$.
