### P-WAV-01 — JEE Main 2024 · 27 Jan Shift 2 · Q24 · Numerical

A closed organ pipe $150$ cm long gives $7$ beats per second with an open organ pipe of length $350$ cm, both vibrating in the fundamental mode. The velocity of sound is ______ m s$^{-1}$.

**Answer: 294** · key ✔

**Solution.** Closed: $f_c=\dfrac v{4L}=\dfrac v{6}$ ($L=1.5$ m). Open: $f_o=\dfrac v{2L}=\dfrac v7$ ($L=3.5$ m). Beat frequency $v\left(\dfrac16-\dfrac17\right)=\dfrac v{42}=7\Rightarrow v=294$ m/s.

### P-WAV-02 — JEE Main 2024 · 30 Jan Shift 1 · Q24 · Numerical

In a closed organ pipe, the frequency of the fundamental note is $30$ Hz. Water is poured into the pipe so that the fundamental frequency increases to $110$ Hz. If the organ pipe has a cross-sectional area of $2\ \text{cm}^2$, the mass of water poured into the tube is ______ g. (Speed of sound in air $=330\ \text{m s}^{-1}$; take the density of water $1$ g cm$^{-3}$)

**Answer: 400** · key ✔

**Solution.** Closed pipe: $f=\dfrac v{4L}$. Initially $L_1=\dfrac{330}{4\times30}=2.75$ m; finally $L_2=\dfrac{330}{4\times110}=0.75$ m. Height of water $=2.75-0.75=2$ m $=200$ cm. Volume $=200\times2=400\ \text{cm}^3$, i.e. $400$ g.

### P-WAV-03 — JEE Main 2024 · 31 Jan Shift 1 · Q12 · MCQ

The fundamental frequency of a closed organ pipe is equal to the first overtone frequency of an open organ pipe. If the length of the open pipe is $60$ cm, the length of the closed pipe will be

(1) 60 cm  (2) 45 cm  (3) 30 cm  (4) 15 cm

**Answer: (4)** · key ✔

**Solution.** Open pipe first overtone (second harmonic): $f=\dfrac vL=\dfrac v{0.6}$. Closed pipe fundamental: $f=\dfrac v{4L_c}$. Equating: $4L_c=0.6\Rightarrow L_c=0.15$ m $=15$ cm.

### P-WAV-04 — JEE Main 2024 · 31 Jan Shift 1 · Q29 · Numerical

Two waves of intensity ratio $1:9$ cross each other at a point. The resultant intensity at the point when (a) the waves are incoherent is $I_1$, and (b) the waves are coherent and differ in phase by $60^\circ$ is $I_2$. If $\dfrac{I_1}{I_2}=\dfrac{10}x$, then $x=$ ______.

**Answer: 13** · key ✔

**Solution.** Let the intensities be $I$ and $9I$. (a) Incoherent: $I_1=I+9I=10I$. (b) Coherent: $I_2=I+9I+2\sqrt{I\cdot9I}\cos60^\circ=10I+3I=13I$. So $\dfrac{I_1}{I_2}=\dfrac{10}{13}$ and $x=13$.

### P-WAV-05 — JEE Main 2024 · 31 Jan Shift 2 · Q9 · MCQ

The speed of sound in oxygen at S.T.P. will be approximately (given $R=8.3\ \text{J K}^{-1}\text{mol}^{-1}$, $\gamma=1.4$)

(1) 310 m s$^{-1}$  (2) 333 m s$^{-1}$  (3) 341 m s$^{-1}$  (4) 325 m s$^{-1}$

**Answer: (1)** · key ✔

**Solution.** $v=\sqrt{\dfrac{\gamma RT}M}=\sqrt{\dfrac{1.4\times8.3\times273}{32\times10^{-3}}}\approx\sqrt{9.9\times10^4}\approx315\ \text{m s}^{-1}$, closest to $310\ \text{m s}^{-1}$.

### P-WAV-06 — JEE Main 2024 · 1 Feb Shift 1 · Q24 · Numerical

A tuning fork resonates with a sonometer wire of length $1$ m stretched with a tension of $6$ N. When the tension in the wire is changed to $54$ N, the same tuning fork produces $12$ beats per second with it. The frequency of the tuning fork is ______ Hz.

**Answer: 6** · key ✔

**Solution.** $f\propto\sqrt T$ for the same length: $\dfrac{f_2}{f_1}=\sqrt{\dfrac{54}6}=3$. The tuning fork matched the wire at $6$ N, so $f_1=f_{fork}$, and at $54$ N the beat frequency is $f_2-f_1=2f_1=12\Rightarrow f_1=6$ Hz.

### P-WAV-07 — JEE Main 2024 · 5 Apr Shift 2 · Q24 · Numerical

A sonometer wire of resonating length $90$ cm has a fundamental frequency of $400$ Hz under some tension. The resonating length for a fundamental frequency of $600$ Hz under the same tension is ______ cm.

**Answer: 60** · key ✔

**Solution.** $f=\dfrac{1}{2l}\sqrt{T/\mu}\Rightarrow f\propto\dfrac1l$ at fixed tension. $l'=90\times\dfrac{400}{600}=60$ cm.
